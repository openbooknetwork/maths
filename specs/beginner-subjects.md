# Beginner subjects

This file lists the 60 subjects of the beginner level, in teaching order. Each one is the size of one story, and
`specs/stories/<id>.md` holds the plan of that story page by page.

`specs/subject-candidates.md` gives the rule for a level and the documents the placements come from. A subject is
here when every curriculum that states where it teaches the subject does so by the end of grade 5. The column
"Latest in" names the curriculum that teaches it last. England's Year N is grade N minus 1.

## Subjects that look beginner and are not

One curriculum teaches each of these after grade 5, so the rule puts it at the intermediate level.

| Subject | Taught after grade 5 by |
|---|---|
| A fraction of an amount | Turkey G6 |
| Decimals to thousandths, rounding and ordering decimals, adding decimals | Turkey G6 |
| Simplest form of a fraction | Turkey G6 |
| Line graphs | Turkey G7 |
| Line symmetry | Japan G6 |
| Volume by counting cubes | Turkey G7 |
| Nets of solids | US G6 |
| The language of chance | US G7, Japan G8 |
| Listing every combination | US G7 |
| Coordinates | Japan G7, Turkey G8 |
| Moving a shape on a grid | US G8, Turkey G8 |
| Negative numbers | US G6, Japan G7, Turkey G7 |
| Square and cube numbers | US G6 |
| Factors, multiples and primes | Turkey G6 |
| Percentages | US G6 |
| The mean | US G6, Turkey G6 |
| Area of triangles and parallelograms | US G6, Turkey G6 |
| Circumference and π | US G7, Turkey G6 |
| The angle sum of a triangle | US G8 |
| A quantity per unit | US G6 |

Turkey's programme for grades 1 to 4 is unread. Its grade 5 units list what a pupil already knows, and that list
settles most subjects above. A fraction of an amount, decimals to thousandths, line graphs and volume by counting
cubes may be taught earlier in Turkey than the table says.
