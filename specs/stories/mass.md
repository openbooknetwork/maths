# `mass` Mass

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `centimetres-and-metres`, `skip-counting`, `hundreds-and-thousands`
- Leads to: `converting-units`
- Sections: heavier and lighter, units of mass, grams and kilograms, reading a scale, masses add up
- In the world: a parcel weighed at the post office, a suitcase kept under an airline's limit
- Figure: a pan balance that tips towards the heavier load, and a kitchen scale with a dial whose needle reads the
  load. The reader drags objects and weights onto the pans or scoops food onto the scale.
- Engine: family `measure`, new scene `scale`. It draws a pan balance that tilts by the difference of its loads
  and a dial scale whose needle turns with the total, with the step between marks and between numbers set by the
  page. The pans and weights of the `balance` family are a starting point for the drawing.

## Challenges

### Balance the parcel with weights
- Goal: the balance is level.
- Drawn each time: a parcel mass from 150 g to 950 g in steps of 50 g, and a set of weights of 500 g, 200 g, 200 g,
  100 g and 50 g that can always make it. The pans start uneven.
- Reached by: dragging weights onto and off the pan.
- Hidden: the parcel's mass and the total of the weights.
- Idea used: heavier and lighter, units of mass.

### Put 650 g of rice in the bowl
- Goal: the needle points to the drawn mass. The 650 in the title is the drawn mass.
- Drawn each time: a step between marks of 10, 20 or 50 g, a step between numbers of 100 or 200 g, and a
  target from 100 to 950 g that sits on a mark and not on a number. The dial runs to 1 kg. The bowl starts empty.
- Reached by: scooping rice in and out, a scoop that holds one step.
- Hidden: a digital readout of the mass.
- Idea used: reading a scale.

### Pack the bag up to the limit
- Goal: the total mass in the bag is at the limit or below it by no more than one mark.
- Drawn each time: a limit from 5 kg to 20 kg, six items from 1 kg to 9 kg, and a dial with a mark every 1 kg or
  2 kg. At least one choice of items reaches the goal, and all six together exceed the limit. The dial runs past
  the limit, and the bag starts empty.
- Reached by: dragging items into and out of the bag.
- Hidden: the items' masses, until an item sits alone on the scale, and the digital total.
- Idea used: reading a scale, masses add up.
