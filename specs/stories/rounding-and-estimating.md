# `rounding-and-estimating` Rounding and estimating

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `large-numbers`, `number-line`, `column-methods`
- Leads to: `word-problems`, `long-multiplication`
- Sections: between two tens, the nearest ten, the nearest hundred, the nearest thousand, an estimate of a sum or a
  difference, an estimate that checks an answer
- In the world: adding up a shopping basket in the head before the till, the visitors to a museum over a weekend
- Figure: a number line that shows the two round numbers on either side of a number, with a bracket between them
  and the nearer end lit. The reader drags the number along the line, picks the place to round to, and on later
  pages drags two numbers whose rounded hops and exact hops are drawn above and below the line.
- Engine: `axes` `number-line` with the extension from `number-line`, plus a rounding mode. The mode draws the
  bracket between the round numbers either side of the point for a chosen place of 10, 100 or 1000, lights the
  nearer end, marks the halfway point, and draws two hops of a sum or a difference with their rounded twins.

## Challenges

### Put the point where it rounds to 60
- Goal: the point sits on a number that rounds to the target.
- Drawn each time: a place of 10 or 100. For 10 the line runs from 0 to 100 and the target is a ten from 10 to 90.
  For 100 the line runs from 0 to 1000 and the target is a hundred from 100 to 900. The point starts at 0, which
  rounds to 0, so the page opens unsolved.
- Reached by: dragging the point, which snaps to whole numbers on the shorter line and to tens on the longer one.
- Hidden: the bracket, the lit end and the number of the point.
- Idea used: the nearest ten, the nearest hundred.

### Find the smallest number that rounds to 700
- Goal: the point sits on the smallest whole number that rounds to the target.
- Drawn each time: a place of 100 or 1000 and a target that is a multiple of it, from 200 to 9 000. The line runs
  from one place below the target to one place above. The point starts at the target, which is larger than the
  number to find.
- Reached by: dragging the point and stepping it by 1 and by 10.
- Hidden: the bracket, the halfway mark and the rounded number.
- Idea used: the nearest hundred, the nearest thousand, halfway rounds up.

### Round both numbers and place their estimate
- Goal: each rounded number is its number rounded to the nearest hundred, and the estimate point sits on their sum.
- Drawn each time: two numbers from 120 to 980 that do not end in 00 and whose tens and ones are not 50. The rounding
  steppers start at 0 and the estimate point at 0, so the page opens unsolved. The steppers reach 1000 and the line
  runs from 0 to 2000.
- Reached by: two steppers that move by 100 and the estimate point, which snaps to hundreds.
- Hidden: the exact sum, the brackets and the lit ends.
- Idea used: the nearest hundred, an estimate of a sum.
