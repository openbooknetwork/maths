# `times-as-many` Times as many

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `equal-groups`, `more-and-less`
- Leads to: no beginner story lists this one as a need. The end page suggests `word-problems`, where a bar diagram shows
  the comparison.
- Sections: times as many, twice and ten times as many, scaling an amount, finding the smaller amount
- In the world: a sunflower beside a daisy, a recipe made for more guests, a model of a building beside the real one,
  crates in a van and in a lorry
- Figure: two bars of unit blocks, one above the other. The lower bar is made of copies of the upper bar, each copy
  outlined. The reader drags the end of a bar and sets the number of copies with a stepper.
- Engine: new scene `copies` in the beginner family that holds `counters`. It draws a small bar of 1 to 10 blocks and
  a large bar made of 1 to 10 copies of it, folds a long bar into rows of ten blocks, lets either bar's end be
  dragged with the other bar following or free, shows two or three small bars scaled together, draws the blocks as
  pictures such as crates or cups, and reads out the difference and the number of copies.

## Challenges

### Make one bar N times the other
- Goal: the large bar holds exactly the drawn number of times the small bar. The title shows the number, such as
  "Make one bar 4 times the other".
- Drawn each time: a number of times from 2 to 6 and a small bar of 2 to 9 blocks, with a product up to 50. The large
  bar opens at a length that is not that product.
- Reached by: dragging the end of the large bar.
- Hidden: the copy outlines inside the large bar and both readouts.
- Idea used: times as many.

### Find the small bar that fits N times
- Goal: the drawn number of copies of the small bar fill the large bar exactly.
- Drawn each time: a number of times from 2 to 9 and a small amount from 2 to 10, and the large bar is their product.
  The page shows the large bar and the number of times. The small bar opens at a length that does not fit, with one
  copy.
- Reached by: the stepper for copies and dragging the end of the small bar.
- Hidden: the block count of the small bar and the readout of how many times.
- Idea used: times as many and finding the smaller amount.
