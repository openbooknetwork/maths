# `column-methods` Adding and subtracting in columns

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `hundreds-and-thousands`, `adding-tens-and-ones`, `fact-families`
- Leads to: `rounding-and-estimating`, `long-multiplication`, `word-problems`
- Sections: lining up the places, adding column by column, carrying, subtracting column by column, exchanging,
  checking with the other operation
- In the world: the legs of a road trip on a map, the books left on the shelves of a library
- Figure: a written sum in columns for thousands, hundreds, tens and ones, with place value blocks above each
  column. The reader drags digits and blocks, taps a column to work it, swaps ten blocks for one of the next place
  and breaks one block into ten of the place to its right. The answer digits and the small carried digits write
  themselves as the blocks move.
- Engine: new scene `columns` in the new beginner family. It sets two or three numbers up to 9,999 in columns,
  shows the blocks of each digit above its column, carries ten blocks to the left and breaks one block to the
  right with the matching written marks, and lets the reader step the digits of a number or of the answer row. It
  extends the `blocks` scene of `adding-tens-and-ones` to hundreds and thousands.

## Challenges

### Finish the sum
- Goal: the answer row shows the sum of the two numbers.
- Drawn each time: two numbers from 100 to 999 whose sum is 1,999 or less, with at least one column making ten or
  more. Half the draws carry in two columns. The answer row opens empty.
- Reached by: the steppers on the answer digits and dragging ten blocks over the line.
- Hidden: any readout of the sum.
- Idea used: adding column by column, carrying.

### Finish the take away
- Goal: the answer row shows the top number take away the lower number.
- Drawn each time: a top number from 300 to 9,999 and a lower number with fewer or the same digits and smaller than
  the top, with at least one column whose lower digit is bigger than its upper digit. Half the draws have a zero in
  the top number just left of such a column, so the exchange crosses a zero. The answer row opens empty.
- Reached by: tapping a block to break it, and the steppers on the answer digits.
- Hidden: any readout of the answer.
- Idea used: subtracting column by column, exchanging.

### Find the number that makes the sum
- Goal: the first number and the reader's number add to the total.
- Drawn each time: a total from 1,000 to 9,999 and a first number from 100 to the total minus 100, so the missing
  number has three or four digits. At least one digit of the total is smaller than the digit of the first number
  in its column, so finding it needs at least one exchange. The reader's number opens at 0.
- Reached by: the steppers on the digits of the second number, and an Add button that works the sum in the grid to
  test the number.
- Hidden: the total of the two numbers until the reader presses Add.
- Idea used: lining up the places, exchanging, checking with the other operation.
