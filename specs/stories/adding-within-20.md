# `adding-within-20` Adding and subtracting within 20

- Level: beginner, first ideas
- Needs: `number-bonds`, `more-and-less`
- Leads to: `fact-families`, `adding-tens-and-ones`, `equals-sign`, `any-order`
- Sections: joining two groups, taking away, counting on and back, the difference, making ten
- In the world: jars of jam sold from a shelf of ten, sowing and moving seedlings in a seed tray of 20 cells
- Figure: two ten frames side by side with counters in two colours. The reader drags counters in and out, taps them
  away or sets each group with a stepper. Some pages show a number line from 0 to 20 where the reader makes jumps.
- Engine: new scene `frames` in the new beginner family that `counting` proposes for its `counters`. It draws two
  ten frames, fills them in order, moves counters by drag or stepper and reads out each group and the total. A
  second new scene `jumps` draws a number line from 0 to 20 with a start point the reader drags and jumps of one
  forward or back made by a button, and reads out where the jumps land.

## Challenges

### Make the number with two groups
- Goal: the red and blue counters together make the target, with each group holding at least one counter.
- Drawn each time: a target from 11 to 19. Both groups start at 0, so the page does not open solved.
- Reached by: the red and blue steppers, from 0 to 10 each.
- Hidden: the total under the frames.
- Idea used: joining two groups.

### Jump from the start to the target
- Goal: the last jump lands on the target.
- Drawn each time: a start from 0 to 20 and a target from 0 to 20 that differs from the start by 2 to 9, in either
  direction. The line opens with no jumps.
- Reached by: the Jump and Jump back buttons and an Undo button.
- Hidden: the readout of where the jumps land and the number of jumps made.
- Idea used: counting on and back, the difference.

### Take away to leave the number
- Goal: the frames hold the target after counters are taken away.
- Drawn each time: a start from 11 to 18 and a target from 1 to 9 with the start minus the target from 3 to 9, so
  the take away always crosses ten.
- Reached by: tapping counters away, and tapping an empty place to put one back.
- Hidden: the count under the frames.
- Idea used: taking away, making ten.
