# `sharing-and-grouping` Sharing and grouping

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `times-tables`
- Leads to: `remainders`, `ten-times-the-place`, `order-of-operations`, `word-problems`
- Sections: sharing out equally, making groups of a size, the division sign, division undoes multiplication
- In the world: strawberries shared into bowls, cards dealt for a card game, apples packed in bags of six
- Figure: a pile of counters above a row of plates or bags. The reader deals the pile out one round at a time, or
  packs it a group at a time, and sets the number of plates or the size of a group with a stepper. Some pages show
  the same counters as an array.
- Engine: new scene `share` in the beginner family that holds `counters`. It draws a pile of up to 60 counters as
  pictures such as strawberries, cards or apples, deals one to each of 2 to 10 plates per press of Deal, packs a
  group of 2 to 10 per press of Pack, slides the result into an array on a press of Line up, and reads out the
  division fact and what is left in the pile. Page 13 uses the `grid` scene `times` from `times-tables`, and the last
  challenge uses `array`.

## Challenges

### Share N so none are left
- Goal: the pile is empty and every plate holds the same. The title shows the total, such as "Share 36 so none are
  left".
- Drawn each time: a total from 12 to 40 that at least one plate count from 2 to 6 shares with none left, and that
  at least one plate count from 2 to 6 leaves counters over for. The page opens on a plate count that leaves some
  over, so it does not open solved.
- Reached by: the plate stepper from 2 to 6 and Deal.
- Hidden: the division fact.
- Idea used: sharing out equally.

### Build the array for the division
- Goal: the array holds the total and has the divisor as its rows or its columns. The title shows the division, such
  as "Build the array for 42 ÷ 6".
- Drawn each time: a divisor from 3 to 9 and an answer from 3 to 9, and the total is their product. The array opens
  as one counter.
- Reached by: the corner drag.
- Hidden: the count of the array and the times facts under it. The rows and columns show their counts.
- Idea used: the division sign and division undoes multiplication.
