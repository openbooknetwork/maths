# `equivalent-fractions` Equivalent fractions

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `fractions-on-a-line`
- Leads to: `comparing-fractions`
- Sections: two names for one amount, cutting every part again, the same point on the line, finding the top for a
  new bottom, the fraction wall
- In the world: note lengths in a bar of music, a tape measure marked in halves, quarters and eighths
- Figure: a bar and a pie whose parts the reader cuts into more pieces with a slider, and stacked number lines whose
  points the reader lines up.
- Engine: `parts` `equivalent` for the cutting pages, `parts` `compare` for two bars, and the new `parts` scene `line`
  (see `fractions-on-a-line`) with up to six stacked lines for the line and wall pages. The first challenge uses the
  existing `equal` goal with drawn targets. The others need a new goal `same-point` for two lines and a new goal
  `two-names` for two bars.

## Challenges

### Shade the same amount with more parts
- Goal: the shaded share of the bar equals the drawn fraction.
- Drawn each time: a fraction with a bottom from 2 to 6 and a top from 1 to one less than the bottom, and a number of
  parts that is 2, 3 or 4 times the bottom, at most 24. The bar is cut into that number and starts empty, so the page opens unsolved.
- Reached by: tapping parts.
- Hidden: the name of the shaded share.
- Idea used: cutting every part again.

### Line up a second point with a different bottom
- Goal: the lower point sits at the same place as the upper point, and the lower line's bottom differs from the
  upper's.
- Drawn each time: an upper fraction with a bottom from 2 to 6 and a top from 1 to one less than the bottom. The
  lower line starts with steps from 2 to 12 that have no tick at the upper point, so the page opens unsolved.
- Reached by: the lower steps slider and dragging the lower point.
- Hidden: the labels of the lower line and the lower point's name.
- Idea used: the same point on the line, finding the top for a new bottom.

### Shade two more names for this fraction
- Goal: both bars show a share equal to the drawn fraction, with two different bottoms that both differ from the
  drawn bottom.
- Drawn each time: a fraction with a bottom from 2 to 4 and a top from 1 to one less than the bottom, so at least two
  larger bottoms up to 12 fit it. Both bars start cut into the drawn bottom with nothing shaded, so the page opens unsolved.
- Reached by: the parts slider of each bar and tapping parts.
- Hidden: the sign between the bars and the names of both shares.
- Idea used: cutting every part again, finding the top for a new bottom, the fraction wall.
