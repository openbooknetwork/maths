# `equal-groups` Equal groups and arrays

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `skip-counting`
- Leads to: `times-tables`, `times-as-many`, `area-by-counting-squares`
- Sections: equal groups, adding the same number again, the times sign, arrays in rows and columns
- In the world: cupcakes on a baking tray, seedlings in a garden seed tray, windows on a block of flats
- Figure: counters on plates, and the same counters lined up as an array. The reader moves counters between plates,
  sets the number of plates and their size with steppers, and drags the corner of an array to add rows and columns.
- Engine: two new scenes in the beginner family that holds `counters`. `groups` draws up to 10 plates of up to 10
  counters, moves a counter by drag, sets the plates and their size by stepper, marks when every plate holds the
  same, and reads out the sum and the times fact. `array` draws up to 10 by 10 counters as pictures such as cakes,
  seedlings or windows, grows by a corner drag, counts by rows when a row is tapped, and covers part of itself.

## Challenges

### Put N counters on N plates
- Goal: the plates are equal and together hold the drawn total. The title shows the drawn numbers, such as "Put 20
  counters on 4 plates".
- Drawn each time: a number of plates from 2 to 6 and a plate size from 2 to 8. The total is their product. The
  page opens with a plate size that differs from the drawn one.
- Reached by: the stepper for the size of each plate. The number of plates is fixed.
- Hidden: the sum and the times fact under the plates.
- Idea used: equal groups and adding the same number again.

### Build an array of the number
- Goal: the array has at least two rows and two columns and holds exactly the total.
- Drawn each time: a total from 12 to 48 that is a product of two numbers from 2 to 10, so the reader has at least
  one array to find. The array opens as one counter.
- Reached by: the corner drag.
- Hidden: the count and the times fact. The row and column counts show beside the array.
- Idea used: arrays in rows and columns and the times sign.
