# `adding-tens-and-ones` Adding tens and ones

- Level: beginner, first ideas
- Needs: `tens-and-ones`, `adding-within-20`
- Leads to: `money`, `column-methods`, `order-of-operations`, `angles-add-up`, `perimeter`, `area-of-rectangles`
- Sections: ten more and ten less, adding tens, adding tens and ones, ten ones make a new ten, taking away tens and
  ones
- In the world: points scored in two halves of a basketball game, pages of a book read over two days
- Figure: a mat with a tens side and a ones side, holding rods of ten and single cubes, with the number they make
  written above. The reader drags rods and cubes on and off, swaps ten cubes for a rod and breaks a rod into ten
  cubes. Some pages show a number line from 0 to 100 with jumps of ten and of one.
- Engine: new scene `blocks` in the new beginner family. It draws rods and cubes on a place value mat up to 99,
  moves them by drag or stepper, swaps ten cubes for a rod and a rod for ten cubes, and reads out the number. The
  `jumps` scene of `adding-within-20` stretched to 0 to 100 with jumps of 10. Page 4 uses a hundred square, which
  could be a new `grid` scene that moves a marker one row down for ten more.

## Challenges

### Add blocks to reach the number
- Goal: the mat shows the target number.
- Drawn each time: a start from 11 to 59 and an amount to add from 11 to 39, with the ones of the start and the
  amount adding to 9 or less. The target is their sum, and the mat opens with the start on it.
- Reached by: dragging rods and cubes on and off the mat.
- Hidden: the number written above the mat.
- Idea used: adding tens and ones.

### Add the two numbers and tidy the mat
- Goal: the answer mat holds the sum of the two numbers with no more than 9 loose cubes.
- Drawn each time: two numbers from 15 to 69 whose ones add to 10 or more and whose sum is 99 or less. The answer mat
  opens empty.
- Reached by: dragging blocks onto the answer mat and the swap spot.
- Hidden: the number written above the answer mat.
- Idea used: adding tens and ones, ten ones make a new ten.

### Take away to leave the number
- Goal: the mat shows the target number.
- Drawn each time: a start from 41 to 95 with its ones from 0 to 8, and a target from 11 to the start minus 11, with
  the ones of the target larger than the ones of the start, so a rod has to be broken. The mat opens with the start
  on it.
- Reached by: tapping a rod to break it, and dragging rods and cubes off and back on.
- Hidden: the number written above the mat.
- Idea used: taking away tens and ones, ten more and ten less.
