# `halves-and-quarters` Halves and quarters

- Level: beginner, first ideas
- Needs: `counting`
- Leads to: `unit-fractions`, `telling-the-time`, `turns-and-right-angles`
- Sections: equal parts, halves of a shape, quarters of a shape, half of a set, a quarter of a set
- In the world: a chocolate bar of eight squares shared by two or four people, a garden bed split into four plots
- Figure: a square, rectangle or circle that the reader cuts by dragging and turning cut lines and shades by tapping
  its parts, or a row of counters that the reader drags onto two or four plates.
- Engine: `parts` `shade` for the bar and pie pages, and two new `parts` scenes. `cut` draws a square, rectangle or
  circle with one or two cut lines the reader drags and turns, snaps them to the middle and the diagonals, counts the
  parts, says whether they are equal, and lets the reader shade parts by tapping. `share` lays out 2 to 20 counters
  and 2 or 4 plates, lets the reader drag counters onto plates, and reads out each plate's count and whether the
  plates hold the same number.

## Challenges

### Cut this shape into quarters
- Goal: the two cut lines divide the shape into four equal parts.
- Drawn each time: one shape from a square with a side of 4 to 8 units, a rectangle with sides of 3 to 8 units that
  differ, or a circle, and two cut lines placed off the middle at angles that leave four unequal parts.
- Reached by: dragging and turning the two cut lines.
- Hidden: the readout that says whether the parts are equal and the size of each part.
- Idea used: equal parts, quarters of a shape.

### Shade the share the page asks for
- Goal: the shaded parts cover the asked share of the shape.
- Drawn each time: a shape from bar, circle and square, cut into 2, 4 or 8 equal parts, and a share from one half,
  one quarter and three quarters that those parts can make. Nothing is shaded at the start.
- Reached by: tapping parts to shade and clear them.
- Hidden: the name of the shaded share.
- Idea used: halves of a shape, quarters of a shape.

### Put the asked share of counters on the plate
- Goal: the plate holds the asked share of the counters, and the tray holds the rest.
- Drawn each time: a number of counters that is a multiple of 4 from 4 to 20, and a share of one half or one
  quarter. All the counters start on the tray.
- Reached by: dragging counters between the tray and the plate. A button sets out four empty helper plates for
  sharing first.
- Hidden: the count on the plate and the number the share needs.
- Idea used: half of a set, a quarter of a set, halves of halves.
