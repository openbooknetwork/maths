# `missing-number` The missing number

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `equals-sign`, `fact-families`
- Leads to: no beginner story lists this one as a need. The end page suggests `word-problems`, whose bars hold a box for
  the unknown part.
- Sections: a box hides a number, take the same from both sides, the box in a take away, check by putting the
  number back
- In the world: the kilometres left on a hiking trail, the tomatoes picked from a garden in one day
- Figure: a pan balance whose pans hold numbered weights and one closed box of hidden weight, with the equation
  beneath and a ▢ in the box's place. The reader takes the same from both pans, puts a guess in the box and
  watches the scale. The take away pages use a part and whole bar with a box on the hidden piece.
- Engine: the `balance` scene with one box drawn as a closed box marked ▢ in place of x, numbers from 0 to 20 and
  the steps take from both and add to both. It needs a guess stepper that fills the box and tilts the scale. The
  `bar` scene of `fact-families`, with a box on the whole or on a part. The `jumps` scene of `adding-within-20`,
  with a start the reader drags and jumps given by the page.

## Challenges

### Find the box with the scale
- Goal: the box stands alone on a level scale, or the guess in the box makes the scale level.
- Drawn each time: ▢ + a = c or a + ▢ = c, with a from 2 to 9 and c from 11 to 19, so the box is from 2 to 17.
  The box opens empty, and the guess stepper opens at 0, which never solves it.
- Reached by: Take from both with an amount stepper from 1 to 10, Undo, and the guess stepper from 0 to 20.
- Hidden: the value of the box and the total of the left pan.
- Idea used: a box hides a number, take the same from both sides, check by putting the number back.

### Find the box in a take away
- Goal: the boxed piece of the bar has the length that makes the equation true.
- Drawn each time: either ▢ − a = b with a and b from 2 to 9, or c − ▢ = b with c from 11 to 20 and b from 2 to c
  minus 2. The bar opens with its end and split at lengths that do not solve the draw.
- Reached by: dragging the end of the bar from 1 to 20 and the split along it.
- Hidden: the label on the boxed piece.
- Idea used: the box in a take away.

### Find where the jumps start
- Goal: starting at the reader's start, a jump forward of a and a jump back of b land on the landing point.
- Drawn each time: a start from 2 to 18, a from 2 to 9 and b from 2 to 9 with a different from b, with the start plus a at most 20, and
  the landing point is the start plus a minus b, kept from 0 to 20. The reader's start opens at a number that misses the landing point.
- Reached by: dragging the start along the line from 0 to 20.
- Hidden: the value of the start and the point where the first jump lands.
- Idea used: check by putting the number back, with each jump undone by its opposite in turn.
