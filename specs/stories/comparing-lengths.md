# `comparing-lengths` Comparing lengths

- Level: beginner, first ideas
- Needs: `more-and-less`
- Leads to: `centimetres-and-metres`
- Sections: side by side, with a third object, with units
- In the world: a sofa that must pass through a doorway, a rug that must fit a hall measured in floor tiles
- Figure: objects drawn as bars on a bench with a start line. The reader drags them side by side, stretches a
  string along one and carries it to another, and lays paper clips or other units end to end along them.
- Engine: new family `measure`, new scene `line-up`. It draws objects as bars that snap to a start line, a string
  the reader stretches and carries, and a tray of equal units the reader lays end to end, and it counts the units
  that cover an object with no gap and no overlap.

## Challenges

### Put the ribbons in order from shortest to longest
- Goal: the four ribbons sit in the slots in order of length, shortest first.
- Drawn each time: four lengths from 3 to 12 clips, all different, with at least two pairs that differ by one clip.
  Each ribbon is placed at a random distance from the start line, and the drawn order on the bench is not already
  the answer.
- Reached by: dragging ribbons to the start line and into the slots.
- Hidden: lengths and clip counts.
- Idea used: side by side.

### Find the stick that fits the gap
- Goal: the stick in the gap matches the gap's width.
- Drawn each time: a gap width from 4 to 14 units and five sticks, one equal to the gap and four that differ from
  it by 1 to 3 units. The gap and the sticks sit far apart so that no stick can be laid beside the gap without the
  string. The gap starts empty.
- Reached by: stretching and carrying the string, dragging a stick into the gap.
- Hidden: widths and lengths.
- Idea used: with a third object.

### Make the ribbon 3 clips longer than the pencil
- Goal: the ribbon is longer than the pencil by the drawn number of clips. The 3 in the title is the drawn number.
- Drawn each time: a pencil length from 4 to 10 clips and a difference from 1 to 4 clips. The ribbon starts at a
  length that is neither the answer nor the pencil's length.
- Reached by: laying clips along the pencil, dragging the ribbon's end, which snaps to whole clips from 1 to 16.
- Hidden: the clip count of the pencil and of the ribbon.
- Idea used: side by side and with units.
