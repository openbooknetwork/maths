# `counting` Counting

- Level: beginner, first ideas
- Needs: nothing
- Leads to: `more-and-less`, `number-line`, `number-bonds`, `tens-and-ones`, `skip-counting`, `halves-and-quarters`,
  `the-calendar`, `sorting-and-pictograms`
- Sections: one number for each object, the last number says how many, counting on, counting back, counting to 100
- In the world: passengers getting on and off a bus, climbing and coming down a staircase
- Figure: a tray of counters drawn as simple objects. The reader taps a counter to give it the next number, drags
  counters in and out of the tray, and on later pages sees 100 counters laid out in rows of ten.
- Engine: new scene `count` in the `counters` family. It draws up to 100 objects loose or in rows of ten, tags
  each tapped object with the next number, moves objects in and out of a tray by drag or by a stepper, and reads
  out the count.

## Challenges

### Count apples into the basket
- Goal: the basket holds exactly the target number of apples when the reader presses Check. The goal line names the
  drawn target.
- Drawn each time: a target from 6 to 30. The basket starts empty and the pile holds 35 apples.
- Reached by: dragging apples into and out of the basket.
- Hidden: the count of apples in the basket and the number tags on the apples.
- Idea used: one number for each object, the last number says how many.

### Count on from the box
- Goal: the box's number and the counters beside it together make the target when the reader presses Check.
- Drawn each time: a box number from 10 to 60 and a target 3 to 9 more than it. The page starts with no loose
  counters beside the box, and the pile holds 12 counters.
- Reached by: dragging counters from the pile next to the box and back.
- Hidden: the running total.
- Idea used: counting on.

### Count back across a ten
- Goal: the counters left in the rows make the target when the reader presses Check.
- Drawn each time: a starting number from 25 to 100 laid out in rows of ten, and a target 3 to 12 less. The target
  sits in a lower ten than the start, so the count crosses a ten.
- Reached by: tapping counters to take them away and tapping the empty places to put them back.
- Hidden: the count of counters left.
- Idea used: counting back, counting to 100.
