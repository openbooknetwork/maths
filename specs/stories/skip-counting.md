# `skip-counting` Skip counting

- Level: beginner, first ideas
- Needs: `counting`
- Leads to: `telling-the-time`, `sorting-and-pictograms`, `equal-groups`, `number-patterns`, `mass`, `capacity`
- Sections: counting in twos, counting in fives, counting in tens, counting in hundreds
- In the world: socks pegged in pairs on a washing line, wheels in a bike rack, pencils in boxes of ten, paper in
  packs of a hundred
- Figure: a number line with equal jumps and a group of objects above each jump. The reader adds or takes away
  jumps, picks the size of a jump and drags the start point. Some pages show the hundred square instead.
- Engine: `jumps` from the beginner family that holds `counters`, extended with a jump size of 2, 5, 10 or 100, a
  line that reaches 1000 with marks every 1, 10 or 100, a picture of the group above each jump, a jump back, and a
  limit on the number of jumps for a challenge. The hundred square pages use `grid` `multiples`.

## Challenges

### Land on the number in equal jumps
- Goal: the last jump lands exactly on the target.
- Drawn each time: a jump size from 2, 5 and 10, and a target that is a multiple of that size from 3 to 20 jumps
  away from 0, up to 100. The jumps start at 0 and the line starts with no jumps, so the page never opens solved.
- Reached by: the Jump and Back buttons.
- Hidden: the landing labels and the readout of the count. The line shows a number only every 10.
- Idea used: counting in twos, fives and tens.

### Get from the start to the target in nine jumps or fewer
- Goal: the jumps land on the target, and there are nine or fewer of them.
- Drawn each time: a start from 1 to 99 and a target that lies 2 to 9 jumps of 10 or 2 to 9 jumps of 100 above the
  start, up to 1000. When the gap is a number of hundreds, only jumps of 100 reach it in nine jumps. The line opens
  with no jumps.
- Reached by: the jump size choice of 10 or 100, and the Jump and Back buttons.
- Hidden: the landing labels and the count of jumps. The line shows a number only every 100.
- Idea used: counting in tens and in hundreds from any start.
