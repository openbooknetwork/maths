# `comparing-decimals` Comparing decimals

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `tenths-and-hundredths`
- Leads to: no beginner story lists this one as a need. The end page suggests `mass` and `capacity`, whose scales
  are read in decimals.
- Sections: comparing on grids, the wholes first and then each place, more digits and a smaller number, comparing on
  the number line
- In the world: race times on a stopwatch, the rain measured on two days
- Figure: two decimals, each shaded on its own 10 by 10 grids and marked on one number line, which the reader sets by
  tapping the grids, stepping the digits in place value columns or dragging a point, with the sign between them.
- Engine: the new `parts` scene `decimal` (see `tenths-and-hundredths`) with a second number, digit steppers on the
  place value columns, and a `sign` measure like the one in `compare`. The challenges need new goals
  `between-decimals` and `one-digit`.

## Challenges

### Shade a decimal between these two
- Goal: the shaded decimal is larger than the smaller drawn decimal and smaller than the larger one.
- Drawn each time: two decimals from 0.01 to 0.99 in hundredths, at least 0.02 apart so a hundredth fits between them,
  and with different tenths digits in at least half the draws. The grid starts empty, so the page opens unsolved.
- Reached by: tapping columns and squares.
- Hidden: the decimal readout of the grid and the signs.
- Idea used: comparing on grids.

### Change one digit to make the second decimal larger
- Goal: the second decimal is larger than the first, and it differs from its drawn start in one digit only.
- Drawn each time: two decimals from 0.01 to 9.99 in hundredths, with the second smaller than the first and a digit
  of the second that can be raised to make it larger. The two differ in at least two
  places. The second starts smaller, so the page opens unsolved.
- Reached by: the digit steppers of the second number. A Reset button brings back the drawn start.
- Hidden: the sign between the numbers.
- Idea used: the wholes first and then each place, more digits and a smaller number.

### Put the point between these two decimals
- Goal: the point lies strictly between the two drawn decimals.
- Drawn each time: two decimals from 0.01 to 2.99 in hundredths, inside the same tenth and from 0.02 to 0.06 apart,
  with one of them written with a single digit after the point in half the draws, as 1.4 and 1.43. The point starts
  at 0 and the line starts zoomed out to tenths, so the page opens unsolved.
- Reached by: dragging the point and the zoom control.
- Hidden: the point's label and the tick labels below the tenths.
- Idea used: more digits and a smaller number, comparing on the number line.
