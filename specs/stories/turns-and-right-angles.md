# `turns-and-right-angles` Turns and right angles

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `position-words`, `halves-and-quarters`
- Leads to: `lines-parallel-perpendicular`, `angles`
- Sections: a whole turn, half a turn, quarter turns, clockwise and anticlockwise, the right angle
- In the world: turning a key in a lock, a walker turning on a map with north, east, south and west
- Figure: an arrow on a round dial that the reader turns by dragging its tip or by turn buttons. The dial shades
  the turn made since the start as a part of a circle and names it. On later pages a second, fixed arm and a right
  angle checker card join the arrow.
- Engine: new scene `dial` in a new beginner family. The arrow turns by drag, snapping to quarter turns or to eighth
  turns as the page sets, and by buttons for a quarter, a half and a whole turn each way. It shades the turn from the
  start in halves and quarters, counts whole turns, names the amount and the direction, and can show a toy robot or
  a walker in place of the arrow. It also holds a right angle checker card that drags and turns onto the corners of
  a shape and says whether a corner fits it, is smaller or is larger.

## Challenges

### Make the turn on the card
- Goal: the turn made from the start equals the amount on the card, in the direction on the card.
- Drawn each time: a start direction from the four quarter positions, an amount from a quarter, a half, three
  quarters and a whole turn, and a direction, clockwise or anticlockwise. The arrow starts with no turn made.
- Reached by: dragging the arrow, or the quarter and half turn buttons.
- Hidden: the shading, the amount named and the direction named.
- Idea used: quarter turns, clockwise and anticlockwise.

### Mark every right angle
- Goal: the marked corners are exactly the right angles of the shape.
- Drawn each time: a shape with 4 to 6 corners, drawn from corners on a hidden square grid, with one to three right
  angles, at most two on a shape with 4 corners, and at least one corner within a sixth of a right angle of square, turned to a random direction. No corner
  starts marked.
- Reached by: dragging and turning the checker, tapping corners to mark or clear them.
- Hidden: the right angle squares on the corners.
- Idea used: the right angle.

### Turn the arm to make a right angle
- Goal: the free arm has turned exactly a quarter turn from the fixed arm in the direction on the card.
- Drawn each time: the direction of the fixed arm from the eight eighth turn positions, and the direction to turn,
  clockwise or anticlockwise. The free arm starts on the fixed arm and snaps to eighth turns, so a diagonal start
  makes the right angle sit between the four main directions.
- Reached by: dragging the free arm.
- Hidden: the right angle square, the shading and the direction named.
- Idea used: quarter turns, clockwise and anticlockwise, the right angle.
