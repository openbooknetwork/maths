# `converting-units` Converting units

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `ten-times-the-place`, `mass`, `capacity`, `telling-the-time`
- Leads to: no beginner story. The end page offers `perimeter` as the next measuring story.
- Sections: kilometres and metres, kilograms and grams, litres and millilitres, one rule for three pairs, hours and
  minutes
- In the world: juice cartons for a party bowl, the running time of a film
- Figure: a double scale, a bar with the larger unit marked above and the smaller unit below, and a marker the
  reader drags along it. For mass and capacity the bar stands beside a scale or a jug, and for time it is a time
  line.
- Engine: family `measure`, new scene `two-scales`. It draws one bar with two scales, a marker that snaps to a
  chosen step, and readings in the large unit, the small unit and both together (2 km 350 m). It can sit beside
  the `scale` and `jugs` figures so the bar follows their reading.

## Challenges

### Set the marker to 3 km 40 m
- Goal: the marker stands at the drawn distance. The title gives it in kilometres and metres.
- Drawn each time: kilometres from 1 to 9 and metres from 10 to 990 in steps of 10, with fewer than 100 metres in
  about half the draws so that the 0 in the hundreds place matters. The bar runs from 0 to 10 000 m. The marker
  starts at 0.
- Reached by: dragging the marker, which snaps to every 10 m, and two buttons beside the bar that move it 10 m
  back or on.
- Hidden: the kilometre scale and the reading in kilometres and metres.
- Idea used: kilometres and metres.

### Balance 2 kg 300 g with gram weights
- Goal: the balance is level. The title gives the bag's mass in kilograms and grams.
- Drawn each time: kilograms from 1 to 3 and grams from 100 to 900 in steps of 100. The weights are three of
  1000 g, one of 500 g, two of 200 g and one of 100 g, which make every drawn mass. The other pan starts empty.
- Reached by: dragging weights onto and off the pan.
- Hidden: the total of the weights on the pan.
- Idea used: kilograms and grams.

### Put each card where it belongs on the bar
- Goal: every card sits at its value on the bar.
- Drawn each time: one kind of quantity, length, mass, capacity or time, and four different amounts in it. The bar
  runs from 0 to 5 of the large unit, with a step of 250 for metres, grams and millilitres and of 15 for minutes, so a phone can place a card, and
  every amount sits on a step above 0. Two cards give their amount in the small unit and two in both units, such as
  2250 m and 2 km 500 m. The cards start beside the bar.
- Reached by: dragging cards onto the bar, where each snaps to the nearest step.
- Hidden: readings at the marker.
- Idea used: all three pairs and hours and minutes.
