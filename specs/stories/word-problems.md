# `word-problems` Word problems

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `column-methods`, `sharing-and-grouping`, `rounding-and-estimating`
- Leads to: no later beginner story. The intermediate story `solving-equations` builds on its bars.
- Sections: a bar for parts and a whole, two bars to compare, a bar of equal parts, choosing the operation, two
  steps
- In the world: packing a bakery order into bags, the free lockers at a gym
- Figure: a bar diagram beside a short story. The reader drags the ends of bars, splits a bar into parts or into
  equal parts, places the story's numbers on the pieces and puts a ? on the amount the story asks for. The
  operation that the bar calls for is written beneath and works itself out in columns when the bar is right.
- Engine: the `bar` scene of `fact-families`, extended to numbers up to 9 999, two bars one above the other for
  comparing, a bar of 2 to 10 equal parts, a ? on any piece, and two bars in a row for two steps. It needs number
  cards from the story that the reader drags onto pieces, and a check that the bar matches the story.

## Challenges

### Build the bar for the story
- Goal: the bar's pieces carry the story's numbers in the right places, with the ? on the amount the story asks for.
- Drawn each time: one story from templates for adding, taking away, times and dividing, with the unknown on the
  whole or on a part. Adding and taking away use numbers from 100 to 999 with a whole of 1 999 or less. Times and
  dividing use parts of 2 to 10 and 2 to 10 parts, and a dividing story always shares exactly. The bar opens empty, so the page opens unsolved.
- Reached by: dragging the bar's end, the Split and Equal parts buttons, and dragging the number cards and the ?.
- Hidden: the operation line beneath the bar.
- Idea used: a bar for parts and a whole, a bar of equal parts, choosing the operation.

### Show how many more on two bars
- Goal: the two bars and their gap carry the story's numbers, with the ? on the amount the story asks for.
- Drawn each time: a comparing story with a smaller amount from 100 to 899 and a gap from 20 to 500, and the ? on
  the larger amount, the smaller amount or the gap. Half the stories say "more" where the answer is a take away.
  Both bars open empty, so the page opens unsolved.
- Reached by: dragging the ends of both bars, and dragging the number cards and the ?.
- Hidden: the operation line beneath the bars.
- Idea used: two bars to compare, choosing the operation.

### Build both bars of a two step story
- Goal: both bars match the story, the first bar's ? is carried into the second bar, and the second bar's ? is on
  the amount the story asks for.
- Drawn each time: a two step story from templates that join a times or dividing step with an adding or taking
  away step, with parts of 2 to 10, 2 to 10 parts, a second number from 10 to 200, and every answer from 1 to 9 999.
  A dividing step always shares exactly. Both bars open empty, so the page opens unsolved.
- Reached by: everything the first challenge offers, and dragging the first bar's whole into the second bar.
- Hidden: the operation lines beneath both bars and any answer until both bars are right.
- Idea used: a bar for parts and a whole, a bar of equal parts, choosing the operation, two steps.
