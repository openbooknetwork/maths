# `mixed-numbers` Mixed numbers

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `fractions-on-a-line`
- Leads to: no beginner story lists this one as a need. The end page suggests `tenths-and-hundredths`, where 1.25 is
  another way to write a number past one whole.
- Sections: fractions above one, wholes inside a fraction, from a fraction to a mixed number, from a mixed number to a
  fraction, mixed numbers on the line
- In the world: lengths swum in a pool, shelves cut from planks of wood
- Figure: up to three bars in a column, each one whole cut into the same parts, which the reader fills by tapping, and
  a number line from 0 to 3 for the line pages.
- Engine: `parts` `mixed` with `wholes: 3` and its `mixed` measure and formula, and the new `parts` scene `line` (see
  `fractions-on-a-line`) with a label that writes the point both ways. The scene needs a second worked formula that
  goes from a mixed number back to a fraction. The challenges need a new goal `fill` that draws a fraction or mixed
  number for the bars, and the goal `place` from `fractions-on-a-line` with a mixed number as its target.

## Challenges

### Fill the bars to show this fraction
- Goal: the bars hold the drawn fraction.
- Drawn each time: a bottom from 2 to 6 and a top from one more than the bottom to one less than three times the
  bottom, leaving out multiples of the bottom. The bars are cut into the drawn bottom and start empty, so the page opens unsolved.
- Reached by: tapping parts.
- Hidden: the mixed number and the fraction beside the bars.
- Idea used: fractions above one, wholes inside a fraction.

### Put the point on this mixed number
- Goal: the point sits on the drawn mixed number.
- Drawn each time: a whole number of 1 or 2, a bottom from 2 to 6 and a top from 1 to one less than the bottom. The
  line starts in steps that differ from the bottom, and the point starts at 0, so the page opens unsolved.
- Reached by: the steps slider and dragging the point.
- Hidden: the tick labels and the point's label.
- Idea used: mixed numbers on the line.

### Fill the bars to show this mixed number in the parts you choose
- Goal: the bars hold the drawn mixed number, in any bottom from 2 to 12.
- Drawn each time: a whole number of 1 or 2 and a fraction with a bottom from 2 to 4 and a top from 1 to one less
  than the bottom, shown as a mixed number. The bars start cut into a bottom that is not a multiple of the drawn
  bottom, with nothing shaded, so the page opens unsolved.
- Reached by: the bottom slider and tapping parts.
- Hidden: the mixed number and the fraction beside the bars.
- Idea used: from a mixed number to a fraction, wholes inside a fraction.
