# `remainders` Remainders

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `sharing-and-grouping`
- Leads to: `written-division`
- Sections: some are left over, the remainder is smaller than the divisor, writing a remainder, what the remainder
  means
- In the world: marbles shared between players, minibuses for a group trip, tennis balls sold in tubes of three,
  whose turn it is in a board game
- Figure: the pile of counters above plates or bags from `sharing-and-grouping`, with the leftovers kept apart in a
  tray. The reader deals or packs, and changes the total and the plates with steppers. One page colours the number
  grid by remainder.
- Engine: `share` from the beginner family that holds `counters`, with a leftover tray that holds what one more round
  cannot deal, a Deal that stops when the pile holds fewer than the plates, a fact that reads "14 ÷ 4 = 3 remainder
  2" and its check "3 × 4 + 2 = 14", a ring of seats for turns taken round a table, and pictures of buses, tubes and
  players. Page 10 uses `grid` `remainders`.

## Challenges

### Leave R over on N plates
- Goal: after the deal the pile holds exactly the drawn remainder. The title shows the numbers, such as "Leave 2 over
  on 5 plates".
- Drawn each time: a number of plates from 3 to 8 and a remainder from 1 to one less than the plates. The pile
  stepper runs from 10 to 60 and opens on a number of the plates' table.
- Reached by: the pile stepper and Deal.
- Hidden: the division fact.
- Idea used: some are left over and the remainder is smaller than the divisor.

### Find the buses for the group
- Goal: every person has a seat and no bus is empty.
- Drawn each time: a bus size from 4 to 9 seats and a group of 15 to 60 people that is not a number of the bus size's
  table, so the last bus is part full. The page opens with one bus.
- Reached by: the bus stepper from 1 to 15 and Load.
- Hidden: the division fact and the count of people left standing.
- Idea used: writing a remainder and what the remainder means.
