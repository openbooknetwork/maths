# `written-division` Written division

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `remainders`, `long-multiplication`
- Leads to: none at the beginner level. The end page suggests `word-problems`, which uses division in problems of
  two steps.
- Sections: sharing hundreds, tens and ones, exchanging a place that does not share evenly, the short division
  layout, a remainder at the end, dividing by a two digit number, the long division layout
- In the world: cutting a roll of ribbon into pieces of one length, planting bulbs in equal rows in a park
- Figure: Base ten blocks for the number sit above a set of equal groups, with the short division layout below
  them. The reader drags blocks into the groups and taps a leftover block to exchange it for ten of the next place,
  and the layout writes each digit as its place is shared. For a two digit divisor the groups give way to a list of
  the divisor's multiples beside the long division layout.
- Engine: new scenes `share` and `long-divide` in the place value family proposed in `ten-times-the-place`
  (`place`). `share` drags blocks into groups, exchanges a block for ten of the next place and fills the short
  layout with its small carried digits. `long-divide` lists multiples the reader taps and writes each subtraction
  and each digit brought down, one step per page.

## Challenges

### Share the blocks into equal groups
- Goal: Every group holds the same amount, and the blocks left outside are fewer than the number of groups.
- Drawn each time: A divisor from 2 to 6 and a number from 100 to 999 that needs at least one exchange to share.
  The page opens with all blocks outside the groups, so the goal does not hold.
- Reached by: dragging blocks into groups and back, and tapping a block to exchange it for ten of the next place.
- Hidden: The short division layout and the amount in each group.
- Idea used: sharing hundreds, tens and ones, exchanging, a remainder at the end.

### Find the number that was shared
- Goal: The reader's number divided by the divisor gives the answer and the remainder shown.
- Drawn each time: A divisor from 3 to 9, an answer from 12 to 99 and a remainder from 0 to one less than the
  divisor. The number they make is at most 999. The page opens at 100, and a draw whose number is 100 is drawn again.
- Reached by: steppers for the hundreds, tens and ones of the number.
- Hidden: The division of the reader's number.
- Idea used: a remainder at the end, the short division layout.

### Pack the order into boxes
- Goal: Full boxes times the box size plus the items left equal the order, and the items left are fewer than a box
  holds.
- Drawn each time: A box size from 12 to 25 and an order from 150 to 999 that does not fill whole boxes. The page\n  opens with no boxes and no items left, so the goal does not hold. The steppers reach 83 full boxes and 24 items left.
- Reached by: a stepper for the full boxes and a stepper for the items left.
- Hidden: The long division layout and the total the reader's boxes hold.
- Idea used: dividing by a two digit number, the long division layout, a remainder at the end.
