# `bar-charts` Bar charts

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `sorting-and-pictograms`, `number-line`
- Leads to: `pie-charts`
- Sections: blocks for counts, bars with a title and axes, a scale on the axis, how many more, drawing a bar chart
  from a table
- In the world: swimmers at a pool each day of a week, tickets sold for each film at a cinema
- Figure: a bar chart whose bars the reader drags up or down, with a scale on the axis and the table of its counts
  beside it. The reader drags bar tops, switches the scale step and the direction of the bars, and taps two bars to
  compare them.
- Engine: new family `chart`, shared with `sorting-and-pictograms` and `pie-charts`. This story needs its scene
  `bars`: columns of blocks or solid bars, upright or lying down, bar ends that snap to whole numbers, a scale step of
  1, 2, 5 or 10 with a choice of where the axis starts, a bracket that shows the gap between two tapped bars, and
  labels the reader drags into their slots.

## Challenges

### Draw the chart of this table
- Goal: every bar ends at the count its table row shows.
- Drawn each time: a step of 2, 5 or 10 and four counts above 0, on a chart ten steps high. With a step of 2 or 10
  each count is a whole number of half steps, so some bars end halfway between two marks. With a step of 5 each
  count sits on a mark. At least two counts differ. The bars start at 0.
- Reached by: dragging each bar's end.
- Hidden: the value readout of each bar.
- Idea used: a scale on the axis, drawing a bar chart from a table.

### Make one bar a number more than another
- Goal: the free bar is the drawn number more than the fixed bar.
- Drawn each time: a step of 2 or 5, a fixed bar from one to six steps high and a gap of up to four steps, on a
  chart ten steps high, so the free bar stays on the chart. With a step of 2 the gap is any whole number from 1 to
  8. With a step of 5 the gap is 5, 10, 15 or 20. The free bar starts at the same height as the fixed bar.
- Reached by: dragging the free bar's end.
- Hidden: the value readout of both bars and the bracket's number.
- Idea used: a scale on the axis, how many more.

### Make the chart fit the clues
- Goal: all four bars match the clues.
- Drawn each time: a step of 2, 5 or 10 and four counts, each on a mark from one to ten steps, all different. The
  clues give one count as a number and each other bar as a number more or fewer than a bar that the clues before it
  fix. They come from the drawn counts, so one chart fits them all. The bars start at 0.
- Reached by: dragging each bar's end.
- Hidden: the value readout of each bar and the gap bracket.
- Idea used: a scale on the axis, how many more, drawing a bar chart.
