# `any-order` Order and grouping

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `times-tables`, `adding-within-20`
- Leads to: `order-of-operations`
- Sections: adding in any order, grouping when adding, multiplying in any order, grouping when multiplying,
  multiplying a sum part by part
- In the world: items in a shopping basket at the till, the seats of a cinema split by an aisle, the scores of three
  darts
- Figure: runs of coloured blocks for a sum, and an array of counters for a product. The reader drags runs into a new
  order, loops two runs to add them first, turns an array on its side, and drags a split line across an array.
- Engine: two scenes of the beginner family that holds `counters`. A new scene `runs` draws two to four runs of
  blocks in a row with their numbers, swaps runs by drag, loops a pair the reader picks to add first and reads out
  that pair's sum and the total. `array` adds a Turn button that turns the array a quarter turn with motion, a
  split line dragged between rows or columns that reads out both parts, and a second array beside the first for
  two trays. Page 10 uses the `grid` scene `times` from `times-tables`, which lights a tapped cell's partner.

## Challenges

### Find the pair that makes ten
- Goal: the looped pair adds to 10.
- Drawn each time: three numbers from 1 to 9 in which exactly one pair adds to 10, set in an order where that pair
  is apart, so the page opens with no pair making ten next to each other.
- Reached by: dragging the runs and the loop.
- Hidden: the sum of the looped pair. The total shows.
- Idea used: adding in any order and grouping when adding.

### Split the array into two facts from the tables
- Goal: the split leaves two arrays whose rows and columns are each 10 or fewer.
- Drawn each time: an array of 11 to 19 rows and 3 to 9 columns, shown either way round, so a wide array needs the
  split across its columns or a turn first. The split line opens at the edge, leaving one part empty.
- Reached by: the split line and the Turn button.
- Hidden: the product of each part and the total.
- Idea used: multiplying in any order and multiplying a sum part by part.
