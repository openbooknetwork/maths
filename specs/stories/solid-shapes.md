# `solid-shapes` Solid shapes

- Level: beginner, first ideas
- Needs: `flat-shapes`
- Leads to: nothing at this level. The intermediate story `boxes-and-cubes` follows it.
- Sections: faces, edges and corners, cube and cuboid, curved surfaces, prisms, pyramids
- In the world: the poles and walls of a tent, the shapes of packets and tins in a shop
- Figure: one solid in a 3D view that the reader turns by dragging. A tap on a face, an edge or a corner marks it,
  and the panel counts the marks. Sliders change a solid's edges or the number of sides of its base.
- Engine: the `axes` solids `cube`, `cuboid`, `prism`, `pyramid`, `solid-cylinder`, `cone` and `ball`, with a new
  mode that marks a tapped face, edge or corner and counts each kind, names the flat shape of a tapped face, and
  hides volume and surface. Pages 1 and 9 add a tilting board on which a solid rolls when it rests on a curved
  surface and slides when it rests on a flat face.

## Challenges

### Mark every edge of this solid
- Goal: every edge of the solid carries a mark, and only the edges do.
- Drawn each time: a prism or a pyramid with a base of 3 to 6 sides, shown at a random turn with at least two
  edges out of sight. No edge is marked when the page opens.
- Reached by: turning the solid by drag and tapping edges. A second tap clears a mark.
- Hidden: the edge count and the solid's name.
- Idea used: faces, edges and corners.

### Build a solid with this many edges
- Goal: the solid on the stage has the number of edges on the card.
- Drawn each time: a solid from the prisms with 3 to 8 sided ends and the pyramids with 3 to 8 sided bases, and
  the card shows its edge count. The start is a solid with a different edge count. A count that two solids share,
  such as 12 for a cube and a hexagonal pyramid, accepts either.
- Reached by: the prism or pyramid switch and the base slider.
- Hidden: the counts of faces, edges and corners.
- Idea used: prisms, pyramids.

### Build the solid from its faces and corners
- Goal: the solid has the number of faces and the number of corners on the card.
- Drawn each time: one prism or pyramid with a base of 3 to 8 sides, and the card shows its faces and corners.
  Each pair belongs to exactly one solid, since a pyramid has as many faces as corners and a prism does not. The
  start is a different solid.
- Reached by: the prism or pyramid switch, the base slider, turning the solid.
- Hidden: the three counts and the solid's name.
- Idea used: faces, edges and corners, prisms, pyramids.
