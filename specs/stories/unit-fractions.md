# `unit-fractions` Unit fractions

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `halves-and-quarters`
- Leads to: `fractions-on-a-line`, `adding-like-fractions`
- Sections: one of equal parts, more parts make smaller parts, counting unit fractions, the parts that make a whole
- In the world: the slices of a loaf of bread, the equal legs of a relay race
- Figure: a bar and a pie cut into 2 to 12 equal parts, which the reader shades by tapping, with a slider for the
  number of parts.
- Engine: `parts` `shade` and `fraction` for most pages, `parts` `compare` for two bars side by side. The scene needs
  one new measure, `rest`, for the unshaded fraction. The challenges need a new goal `count` (shade a drawn fraction
  with the bars cut by the reader) and a new goal `rest` (leave a drawn fraction unshaded). Both draw their targets
  when the page opens.

## Challenges

### Shade the unit fraction the page names
- Goal: the bar is cut into the drawn number of parts and exactly one part is shaded.
- Drawn each time: a bottom number from 2 to 12, and a starting number of parts from 2 to 12 that differs from it.
  Nothing is shaded at the start, so the goal does not hold.
- Reached by: the parts slider and tapping parts.
- Hidden: the fraction name beside the bar and the parts count.
- Idea used: one of equal parts.

### Shade the fraction the page names
- Goal: the bar is cut into the drawn number of parts and the drawn number of them is shaded.
- Drawn each time: a bottom number from 3 to 12 and a top number from 2 to one less than the bottom, and a starting
  number of parts that differs from the bottom. Nothing is shaded at the start, so the goal does not hold.
- Reached by: the parts slider and tapping parts.
- Hidden: the fraction name beside the bar and the parts count.
- Idea used: counting unit fractions.

### Leave the fraction the page names unshaded
- Goal: the bar is cut into the drawn number of parts and the unshaded parts make the drawn fraction.
- Drawn each time: a bottom number from 3 to 12 and a top number from 1 to one less than the bottom, and a starting
  number of parts that differs from the bottom. Nothing is shaded at the start, so the goal does not hold.
- Reached by: the parts slider and tapping parts.
- Hidden: the fraction name beside the bar, the parts count and the unshaded readout.
- Idea used: counting unit fractions, the parts that make a whole.
