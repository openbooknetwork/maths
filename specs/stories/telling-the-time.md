# `telling-the-time` Telling the time

- Level: beginner, first ideas
- Needs: `skip-counting`, `halves-and-quarters`
- Leads to: `converting-units`, `time-intervals`
- Sections: the hour hand, o'clock, the minute hand and one hour, half past, quarter past and quarter to, five
  minutes at a time, the digital time
- In the world: the clock at a railway station and a train's departure, the kick off of a football match
- Figure: A clock face with an hour hand and a minute hand that the reader drags. The hands turn together like a
  real clock, so the hour hand creeps on as the minute hand goes round, and a digital display and the time in words
  sit under the face.
- Engine: new family `clock` with scene `clock`. It draws the face, lets the reader drag either hand with the
  other following, snaps the minute hand to a step the page sets (hours, halves, quarters or five minutes), and can
  hide either hand, the digital display or the words. `time-intervals` adds scenes to the same family.

## Challenges

### Set the clock to the time in words
- Goal: The hands show the drawn time.
- Drawn each time: An hour from 1 to 12 and one of o'clock, quarter past, half past and quarter to. The page opens at
  12 o'clock, and a draw of 12 o'clock is drawn again.
- Reached by: dragging either hand, with the other turning with it. The long hand snaps to quarter steps.
- Hidden: The digital display and the words for the clock's own time.
- Idea used: the hour hand, half past, quarter past and quarter to.

### Set the clock to the digital time
- Goal: The hands show the drawn digital time.
- Drawn each time: An hour from 1 to 12 and minutes from 05 to 55 in fives that are not 15, 30 or 45. The page opens
  at 12 o'clock.
- Reached by: dragging either hand, with the other turning with it. The long hand snaps to five minute steps.
- Hidden: The digital display and the words for the clock's own time.
- Idea used: five minutes at a time, the digital time.

### Set the clock to a time before the hour
- Goal: The hands show the drawn time.
- Drawn each time: An hour from 1 to 12 and one of twenty five to, twenty to, ten to and five to that hour. The page
  opens at 12 o'clock.
- Reached by: dragging either hand, with the other turning with it. The long hand snaps to five minute steps.
- Hidden: The digital display and the words for the clock's own time.
- Idea used: the hour hand, quarter to, five minutes at a time.
