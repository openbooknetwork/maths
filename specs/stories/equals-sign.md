# `equals-sign` The equals sign

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `adding-within-20`
- Leads to: `missing-number`
- Sections: both sides have the same value, true and false, a sum on each side, keeping the balance
- In the world: two teams after two rounds of a quiz, packing two shopping bags with the same number of tins
- Figure: a pan balance with numbered weights on each pan and the equation they make written beneath it. The
  scale tips toward the heavier side and stays level when both sides match. The reader drags weights on and off
  the pans and moves weights between groups on one pan.
- Engine: the `balance` scene with numbers only. It needs a variant with no boxes of x, where each pan holds one
  to three numbered weights from 0 to 20, the equation is written as the pans show it (such as 6 + 4 = 7 + 3), the
  scale tilts when the sides differ, and the reader drags weights from a tray and between pans. It also needs a
  tray of equation cards that the reader sorts into True and False.

## Challenges

### Balance the scale with two weights
- Goal: the scale is level and the right pan holds exactly two weights that are not the two numbers on the left.
- Drawn each time: a left side of two numbers from 1 to 9 each, with a sum from 5 to 18, so the tray always holds another pair with that sum. The right pan opens empty.
- Reached by: dragging weights from a tray that holds each weight from 1 to 10 once onto the right pan and off again.
- Hidden: the value of the left side written as one number.
- Idea used: both sides have the same value, a sum on each side.

### Make the equation true with one change
- Goal: the equation is true and only one of its four numbers differs from the draw.
- Drawn each time: a + b = c + d with every number from 1 to 9, where a + b and c + d differ by 1 to 4 and
  the d that makes the sides equal is from 1 to 9, so changing d always works.
- Reached by: a stepper on each of the four numbers, each from 0 to 9. A second number changed shows a note that
  only one change is allowed.
- Hidden: the value of each side written as one number.
- Idea used: true and false, a sum on each side.

### Leave one weight on the left and keep the balance
- Goal: the scale is level and the left pan holds one weight whose value is less than a + b, so joining the two
  left weights into one does not count.
- Drawn each time: a + b = c + d with a and b from 2 to 9 and a + b from 4 to 16, and c and d from 1 to 9 with
  c + d = a + b and c equal to neither a nor b. Those limits always leave such a c. The page opens with two
  weights on each pan, so the left pan does not start with one weight.
- Reached by: a Take from both button with a stepper for the amount, and dragging pieces between weights on one pan.
- Hidden: the value of each side written as one number.
- Idea used: a sum on each side, keeping the balance.
