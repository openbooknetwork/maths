# `the-calendar` The calendar

- Level: beginner, first ideas
- Needs: `counting`
- Leads to: none at the beginner level. The end page suggests `time-intervals`, which measures shorter stretches of
  time on a clock.
- Sections: the days of the week, a week as a row of seven, the months and their lengths, a year of twelve months,
  leap years, counting on in days and weeks
- In the world: a library book due back in three weeks, a birthday on 29 February
- Figure: A month page of a calendar with seven columns headed by the days of the week. The reader steps the month
  and the year, and taps dates to mark them. A strip of the twelve months, each drawn as long as its number of days,
  sits above the page on the pages about months and years.
- Engine: new scene `month` in the `grid` family, whose `mark` scene already marks tapped cells in rows. The grid
  starts on the month's first weekday, holds as many cells as the month has days, carries weekday headings in the
  reader's language, and steps month and year by the real calendar from 2000 to 2099, where every fourth year is a
  leap year. A year strip of twelve bars is the small variation.

## Challenges

### Tap the date a number of days later
- Goal: The marked date is the drawn number of days after the start date.
- Drawn each time: A month from January 2000 to November 2099, a start date in that month, and a number of days from
  3 to 20. At least half the draws cross into the next month. No date is marked when the page opens.
- Reached by: tapping a date to mark it and stepping the month forward or back.
- Hidden: The count of days between the start and the marked date.
- Idea used: a week as a row of seven, counting on in days and weeks.

### Tap where the date falls
- Goal: The tapped cell is the cell of the drawn date.
- Drawn each time: A month and a year from 2000 to 2099 and a date from 8 to the last date of that month. The page
  shows the 1st and hides every other date.
- Reached by: tapping a cell.
- Hidden: The dates in the cells.
- Idea used: the days of the week, a week as a row of seven.

### Find a year where the date falls on the weekday
- Goal: The drawn date, in the month and year on the page, falls on the drawn weekday.
- Drawn each time: A weekday and a date, which is 29 February in a third of the draws, the 31st in
  another third, and a day from 1 to 28 in the rest. The reader picks the month, and the 31st holds only in a month
  of 31 days. The page opens in a month and year from 2024 to 2040
  where the date does not fall on that weekday, and the months and years from 2024 to 2040 hold at least one where it
  does.
- Reached by: steppers for the month and the year, limited to 2024 to 2040.
- Hidden: The weekday the date falls on in years the reader has not stepped to.
- Idea used: the months and their lengths, leap years, the same date a year later.
