# `centimetres-and-metres` Centimetres and metres

- Level: beginner, first ideas
- Needs: `comparing-lengths`, `number-line`
- Leads to: `tenths-and-hundredths`, `mass`, `capacity`, `triangles`, `quadrilaterals-and-polygons`, `perimeter`, `area-by-counting-squares`
- Sections: a unit everyone shares, the ruler, the metre, estimating
- In the world: a long jump measured from the board, cloth cut to length at a market stall
- Figure: a ruler or a tape measure under objects that slide along it, with markers that snap to the centimetre
  marks. The tape can zoom out to show whole metres.
- Engine: family `measure`, new scene `ruler`. It draws a ruler or tape in centimetres up to a few metres, objects
  and markers that slide along it and snap to marks, and reads a length in centimetres or in metres and
  centimetres. It shares its bars and units with `line-up`.

## Challenges

### Draw a line 7 cm long
- Goal: the line from the pen's start to its end is the drawn length. The 7 in the title is the drawn length.
- Drawn each time: a length from 3 to 15 cm and a start mark from 1 to 10 on a 30 cm ruler. The pen's end
  starts on the start mark, so the line opens with no length.
- Reached by: dragging the pen's end, which snaps to centimetre marks.
- Hidden: the length readout.
- Idea used: the ruler.

### Pull the tape out to 1 m 35 cm
- Goal: the tape's end reads the drawn length. The title gives it in metres and centimetres.
- Drawn each time: a length from 1 m 5 cm to 2 m 95 cm in whole centimetres, never a whole number of metres. The
  tape starts at a length other than the target and pulls out to 3 m.
- Reached by: dragging the tape's end.
- Hidden: the reading in metres and centimetres. The tape shows only its centimetre numbers.
- Idea used: the ruler and the metre.

### Mark 25 cm on a blank strip
- Goal: the marker sits within 2 cm of the drawn length.
- Drawn each time: a length from 12 to 60 cm that is not a multiple of 10, on a strip 70 cm long that shows 0 and
  10 cm only. The marker starts at 0.
- Reached by: dragging the marker. Check shows the ruler, and Another one hides it again.
- Hidden: every mark beyond 10 cm until Check.
- Idea used: estimating and the ruler.
