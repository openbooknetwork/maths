# `money` Money

- Level: beginner, first ideas
- Needs: `adding-tens-and-ones`
- Leads to: none by its needs. The end page suggests `tenths-and-hundredths`, which writes 3 pounds and 45 pence as
  £3.45.
- Sections: what each coin is worth, making an amount, the fewest coins, pounds and pence, adding prices, giving
  change
- In the world: buying a snack at a café counter, a parking meter that takes coins and gives no change
- Figure: A till drawer of coins and notes sits beside a tray. The reader drags coins and notes from the drawer onto
  the tray and back, and the total of the tray shows under it. On the pages about shopping, a price tag and a
  payment sit above the tray.
- Engine: new family `coins` with scenes `tray` and `change`. `tray` draws the coins and notes of the reader's
  language to scale, drags them between drawer and tray, swaps a set of coins for one of the same value, and totals
  the tray. `change` adds a price and a payment and counts the change on from the price. English uses 1p, 2p, 5p,
  10p, 20p, 50p, £1 and £2 coins and £5, £10 and £20 notes. Turkish uses its own set. Amounts are written in pounds
  and pence, as £3 and 45p, with no decimal point.

## Challenges

### Make the amount with coins
- Goal: The tray holds exactly the drawn price.
- Drawn each time: A price from 11p to 99p that is not the value of one coin. The tray opens empty.
- Reached by: dragging coins between the drawer and the tray.
- Hidden: The total of the tray.
- Idea used: what each coin is worth, making an amount.

### Make the amount with the fewest coins
- Goal: The tray holds exactly the drawn price, with the least number of coins and notes that can make it.
- Drawn each time: A price from £1 and 1p to £19 and 99p whose fewest pieces number at least four. The tray opens
  empty.
- Reached by: dragging coins and notes between the drawer and the tray, and the swap button.
- Hidden: The total of the tray. The count of pieces stays shown.
- Idea used: the fewest coins, pounds and pence.

### Give the right change
- Goal: The change tray holds the payment minus the price.
- Drawn each time: A price from 5p to £19 and 95p in steps of 5p, and a payment that is the smallest of £1, £2, £5,
  £10 and £20 above the price, or the next one up where there is one. The change is never 0. The change tray opens
  empty.
- Reached by: dragging coins and notes from the drawer onto the change tray and back.
- Hidden: The total of the change tray and the amount of change due.
- Idea used: pounds and pence, giving change.
