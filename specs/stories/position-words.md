# `position-words` Position words

- Level: beginner, first ideas
- Needs: nothing
- Leads to: `turns-and-right-angles`
- Sections: above and below, left and right, between, in front and behind, two words for one place
- In the world: stocking a shop shelf from a list, joining the queue at a ticket office
- Figure: a front view of a set of places, such as the shelves of a bookcase, a row on a counter or a queue, with
  named objects in them. The reader drags an object from place to place and the panel says where it is.
- Engine: new scene `place` in a new beginner family. It draws places as shelves in rows, a single row or a queue
  facing a counter, with an optional back row that the front row partly hides. Objects drag between places, the
  scene works out which of above, below, left of, right of, between, in front of and behind hold for any two
  objects, and a person in the scene can face the reader or face away.

## Challenges

### Put each thing above or below
- Goal: three objects sit on the shelves so that both rules on the note hold.
- Drawn each time: three objects from a set of ten, and two rules that each use above or below. The rules are drawn
  from an arrangement the engine makes first, so they always fit together, and the objects start in an
  arrangement that breaks at least one rule.
- Reached by: dragging objects between the three shelves.
- Hidden: the panel that lists which relations hold.
- Idea used: above and below.

### Set the counter as the note says
- Goal: three objects sit in a row on the counter so both rules hold from the side of the person behind it.
- Drawn each time: three objects, two rules that use left of, right of or between, and whether the person faces
  the reader or faces away. The rules come from an arrangement made first, and the start breaks at least one rule.
  When the person faces the reader, at least one rule gives a different place from the reader's own left and right.
- Reached by: dragging objects along the row.
- Hidden: the relation panel and the hand drawings at the ends of the row.
- Idea used: left and right, between.

### Follow every rule on the note
- Goal: four objects sit on the shelves so that all three rules on the note hold.
- Drawn each time: four objects and three rules, one of them using in front of or behind and one using between.
  The rules come from an arrangement made first, they leave exactly one arrangement or a few, and the start breaks
  at least two rules.
- Reached by: dragging objects between shelves, places in a row and the front and back rows.
- Hidden: the relation panel.
- Idea used: above and below, left and right, between, in front and behind, two words for one place.
