# `number-line` The number line

- Level: beginner, first ideas
- Needs: `counting`
- Leads to: `centimetres-and-metres`, `fractions-on-a-line`, `rounding-and-estimating`, `bar-charts`
- Sections: numbers in order on a line, marks that count in twos, fives or tens, a line that starts past zero,
  placing a number, estimating where a number sits
- In the world: a bookmark in a book of 100 pages, finding a house on a long street
- Figure: a straight line from left to right with marks at equal steps and some marks labelled. The reader drags a
  point along the line, hops it with + and − buttons, and drags label cards onto marks.
- Engine: `axes` `number-line`, extended. The scene needs a range that starts at 0 or above, a step between marks,
  a choice of which labels show, a point that snaps to marks or moves freely, hops drawn as arcs, label cards to drop
  on marks, and a Show button that reveals the true place of a number.

## Challenges

### Put the point on 37
- Goal: the point sits on the target number. The 37 in the title is the drawn target.
- Drawn each time: a target from 11 to 99 that does not end in 0. The point starts at 0.
- Reached by: dragging the point, which snaps to the marks, and the + and − hops.
- Hidden: the number of the point and the labels between the tens.
- Idea used: numbers in order on a line.

### Find the step and place the number
- Goal: the point sits on the target number.
- Drawn each time: a step of 2, 5 or 10, a first mark from 0 to 50 that is a multiple of the step, and ten marks
  after it. Two marks next to each other carry labels, and the target is another mark at least three steps from both.
  The point starts on one of the labelled marks.
- Reached by: dragging the point, which snaps to the marks.
- Hidden: the number of the point and every label but the two drawn.
- Idea used: marks that count in steps, a line that starts past zero.

### Estimate 63 on a bare line
- Goal: the point sits within one twentieth of the line's length of the target. The 63 in the title is the drawn
  target.
- Drawn each time: a left end from 0 to 30 and a right end from 40 more than the left end up to 100, both multiples
  of ten. The target is a number between them at least a tenth of the length from each end and from the middle.
  The point starts at the left end.
- Reached by: dragging the point freely along the line.
- Hidden: the number of the point and every mark between the ends.
- Idea used: placing a number, estimating where a number sits.
