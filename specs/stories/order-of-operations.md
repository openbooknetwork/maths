# `order-of-operations` Order of operations

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `any-order`, `sharing-and-grouping`, `adding-tens-and-ones`
- Leads to: none at the beginner level. The end page suggests `word-problems`, which writes a problem of two steps
  as one line.
- Sections: adding and subtracting from left to right, × before + and −, ÷ before + and −, × and ÷ from left to
  right, brackets first, one line for a situation
- In the world: a basic calculator and a scientific calculator that give different answers, the score of a
  basketball game
- Figure: A line of tiles holds numbers and signs. The reader taps a sign to work it out, and that sign and its two
  numbers merge into one tile, until one tile is left. The reader also drags a pair of brackets round part of the
  line.
- Engine: new scene `expression`, closest to the symbolic steps of `balance`. Tiles merge with a short glide when
  the reader taps a sign. A page sets one of two modes: free, where any sign can go first, and rule, where only the
  step the rule picks merges and any other sign shakes. Brackets snap round whole numbers, and the value of the line
  by the rule is a measure.

## Challenges

### Work the line in the right order
- Goal: The last tile holds the value of the line by the rule.
- Drawn each time: Four or five numbers from 1 to 12 with three or four signs from +, −, × and ÷, with at least
  one × or ÷ after a + or −. Every step is whole and not below 0 in any order the reader can tap, and working the\n  line from the left gives a different value from the rule. The line opens unworked, with more than one tile.
- Reached by: tapping signs in free mode, and Reset to start the line again.
- Hidden: The value of the line.
- Idea used: × and ÷ before + and −, × and ÷ from left to right.

### Make the target with one pair of brackets
- Goal: The line with the reader's brackets equals the target.
- Drawn each time: Four numbers from 2 to 9 with three signs from +, − and ×. The places the brackets can go give at
  least three different values, all whole and not below 0. The target is one of them and differs from the value of
  the line with no brackets. The page opens with the brackets off the line.
- Reached by: dragging the pair of brackets onto the line and along it.
- Hidden: The value of the line.
- Idea used: brackets first, × before + and −.

### Choose the signs that make the target
- Goal: The line, worked by the rule, equals the target.
- Drawn each time: Four numbers from 1 to 9 and a set of three signs that uses × or ÷ at least once, where every
  step is whole and not below 0. The target is the value of that line and differs from the value with three + signs,
  which is how the page opens.
- Reached by: tapping each sign tile to step it through +, −, × and ÷.
- Hidden: The value of the reader's line.
- Idea used: adding and subtracting from left to right, × and ÷ before + and −, × and ÷ from left to right.
