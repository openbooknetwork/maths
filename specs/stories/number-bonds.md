# `number-bonds` Number bonds

- Level: beginner, first ideas
- Needs: `counting`
- Leads to: `adding-within-20`
- Sections: a whole and two parts, every split of a number, swapping the parts, pairs that make 10, pairs that make
  20, the missing part
- In the world: pins knocked down in ten-pin bowling, free spaces in a car park of 20
- Figure: a part-whole diagram of a whole circle and two part circles above one or two ten frames. The reader drags
  counters between the two parts, taps cells of the frame to fill or empty them, and lifts a cup that hides a part.
- Engine: new scene `bonds` in the beginner family that holds `counters`. It draws one or two ten frames with
  counters of two colours, a part-whole diagram that follows the frames, a cup that covers one part, and a list
  that logs each split the reader finds.

## Challenges

### Make 10 in the frame
- Goal: the frame holds 10 counters in all.
- Drawn each time: a number of red counters from 1 to 9, placed in cells in any order.
- Reached by: tapping empty cells to add a blue counter and tapping a blue counter to take it out.
- Hidden: the count of blue counters and the count of empty cells.
- Idea used: pairs that make 10.

### Fill the frames to 20
- Goal: the two frames hold 20 counters in all.
- Drawn each time: a number of red counters from 1 to 19 that is not 10, placed across both frames in any order.
- Reached by: tapping empty cells to add a blue counter and tapping a blue counter to take it out.
- Hidden: the count of blue counters and the count of empty cells.
- Idea used: pairs that make 10, pairs that make 20.

### Build the part the cup hides
- Goal: the tray holds the part that the cup hides.
- Drawn each time: a whole from 5 to 20 and a part in sight from 1 to one less than the whole. The tray starts
  empty, and the frames are left out so their empty cells give no count.
- Reached by: dragging counters from a pile of 20 into the tray and back. The cup lifts once the goal holds.
- Hidden: the count in the tray and the part under the cup.
- Idea used: a whole and two parts, pairs that make 10 and 20, the missing part.
