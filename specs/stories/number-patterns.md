# `number-patterns` Number patterns

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `skip-counting`
- Leads to: no beginner story lists this one as a need. The end page suggests `times-tables`, where every table is a
  pattern with the rule "add n".
- Sections: patterns that repeat, patterns that grow, the rule of a pattern, odd and even
- In the world: posts painted along a fence, house numbers on the two sides of a street, tables pushed together for a
  party, beads on a bracelet
- Figure: a strip of numbered places that holds a pattern. The places hold coloured tiles that repeat, or figures
  of dots that grow from one step to the next, with the numbers of the pattern under them. The reader taps places to
  colour them, adds steps with a stepper and sets a start and a rule.
- Engine: new scene `pattern` in the beginner family that holds `counters`. It draws up to 30 numbered places that
  hold tiles of up to four colours or figures of up to 60 dots, repeats a unit of 2 to 4 tiles the reader paints,
  grows a figure by a rule of "add n" or "double" from a start, shows a number as dots in two rows of pairs, joins
  two such pairs figures, and outlines the unit or the dots each step adds. The hundred square pages use `grid`
  `multiples`, and the last challenge uses `grid` `mark`.

## Challenges

### Carry on the pattern
- Goal: the next four places hold the colours the unit puts there.
- Drawn each time: a unit of 2 to 4 tiles in 2 or 3 colours that is not a shorter unit repeated, shown for two full
  units so that only one unit fits. The empty places open bare, so the page opens unsolved.
- Reached by: tapping the empty places to choose their colour.
- Hidden: the outline of the unit.
- Idea used: patterns that repeat.

### Make the pattern from its start and rule
- Goal: the reader's row matches the shown numbers.
- Drawn each time: a start from 1 to 20 and a rule from "add 2" to "add 10". The start stepper runs from 1 to 20.
  The reader's start and rule open on a pair that differs from the drawn pair, so the two rows
  do not match when the page opens.
- Reached by: the start stepper and the rule chips.
- Hidden: the jump between the shown numbers.
- Idea used: patterns that grow and the rule of a pattern.

### Mark every number of the pattern
- Goal: the marked numbers are exactly the numbers of the pattern from its start to 50.
- Drawn each time: a start from 1 to 9 and a rule from "add 2" to "add 10", chosen so the pattern holds 5 to 25
  numbers up to 50. With a rule of "add 2" the start is 1 or 2, so the pattern is the odd or the even numbers. The
  grid opens with nothing marked.
- Reached by: tapping numbers to mark and clear them.
- Hidden: the count of marked numbers.
- Idea used: the rule of a pattern, odd and even, and skip counting.
