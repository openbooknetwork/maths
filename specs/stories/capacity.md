# `capacity` Capacity

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `centimetres-and-metres`, `skip-counting`, `hundreds-and-thousands`
- Leads to: `converting-units`
- Sections: full and empty, comparing by pouring, litres and millilitres, reading a jug, amounts add up
- In the world: a dose of medicine measured with a spoon, a fish tank filled jug by jug
- Figure: containers of different shapes with a water level, and a measuring jug with marks. The reader fills
  from a tap, pours one container into another by dragging it over the other, and drags a level to a mark.
- Engine: family `measure`, new scene `jugs`. It draws containers whose shape sets how high a given amount stands,
  pours from one into another with any overflow shown as a spill, and draws a measuring jug whose step between
  marks and between numbers the page sets.

## Challenges

### Put the containers in order by how much they hold
- Goal: the three containers sit in the slots from the least capacity to the most.
- Drawn each time: three capacities from 200 to 900 ml that differ by at least 100 ml, and shapes chosen so that the
  tallest container is not the one that holds most. The starting order is not the answer.
- Reached by: filling, pouring one container into another, dragging containers into slots.
- Hidden: capacities and marks on the containers.
- Idea used: comparing by pouring.

### Fill the jug to 350 ml
- Goal: the water stands at the drawn mark. The 350 in the title is the drawn amount.
- Drawn each time: a step between marks of 10, 20 or 50 ml, a step between numbers of 100 or 200 ml, and a target
  from 50 to 950 ml on a mark that carries no number. The jug holds 1 litre and starts empty.
- Reached by: the tap, which adds one step at a tap or flows while held, the drain, and dragging the level.
- Hidden: a digital readout of the amount.
- Idea used: reading a jug.

### Fill a 1350 ml bottle with a 500 ml jug
- Goal: the bottle is full and nothing has spilled. The 1350 in the title is the drawn capacity.
- Drawn each time: a bottle capacity from 1050 to 1450 ml or from 2050 to 2450 ml in steps of 50 ml, so whole jugs
  count up to the thousands and the rest is one jug filled to a mark. The jug holds 500 ml with a mark every 50 ml.
  A pour empties the whole jug into the bottle. The bottle starts empty. A spill empties the bottle and the reader
  starts again.
- Reached by: filling the jug to a mark, pouring it into the bottle, more than once.
- Hidden: the amount in the bottle, which has no marks.
- Idea used: reading a jug, amounts add up, how much more fills it.
