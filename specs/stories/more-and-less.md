# `more-and-less` More and less

- Level: beginner, first ideas
- Needs: `counting`
- Leads to: `adding-within-20`, `comparing-lengths`, `hundreds-and-thousands`, `times-as-many`
- Sections: matching one to one, more and fewer, one more and one less, comparing two numbers, the signs <, > and =
- In the world: a game of musical chairs, the scoreboard of a football match
- Figure: two rows of counters, one above the other. The reader adds and removes counters and draws a line from a
  top counter to a bottom one to pair them. Later pages set two numbers with sliders and put a sign between them.
- Engine: new scene `match` in the beginner family that holds `counters`. It draws two rows of up to 40 objects,
  pairs them by a drag from one row to the other or by a Pair button, marks the unpaired ones, and shows two numbers
  with a tappable sign that reads true or false.

## Challenges

### Make the two rows the same
- Goal: the top row has as many counters as the bottom row.
- Drawn each time: a bottom row of 3 to 15 counters and a top row of 1 to 15 counters, different from the bottom.
- Reached by: the top row's stepper, which runs from 0 to 20, and dragging counters in and out of the top row.
- Hidden: the counts of both rows.
- Idea used: matching one to one.

### Make the top row one less than the bottom
- Goal: the top row has one counter fewer than the bottom row.
- Drawn each time: a bottom row of 5 to 20 counters and a top row of 1 to 20 counters that is not one fewer.
- Reached by: the top row's stepper, which runs from 0 to 20, and dragging counters in and out of the top row.
- Hidden: the counts of both rows.
- Idea used: matching one to one, one more and one less.

### Put the right sign between the groups
- Goal: the sign between the two groups is true.
- Drawn each time: two groups of 11 to 40 counters each, scattered loosely. One draw in three makes them equal. The
  tile starts on a sign that is false.
- Reached by: tapping the sign tile, and pairing counters with the pair drag to check.
- Hidden: the counts of both groups.
- Idea used: matching one to one, comparing two numbers, the signs.
