# `ten-times-the-place` Ten times the place

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `hundreds-and-thousands`, `times-tables`, `sharing-and-grouping`
- Leads to: `long-multiplication`, `tenths-and-hundredths`, `converting-units`
- Sections: ten of one place make one of the next, multiplying by 10, multiplying by 100 and 1000, dividing by 10,
  100 and 1000, multiplying by a multiple of ten
- In the world: packing screws into bags, boxes and cartons in a hardware warehouse, a café ordering boxes of tea
  bags
- Figure: A place value chart with columns for thousands, hundreds, tens and ones holds a digit card in each column,
  with base ten blocks drawn above it. The reader sets the number with steppers and taps ×10, ×100, ×1000, ÷10 or
  ÷100, and the digit cards slide left or right along the chart.
- Engine: new scene `shift` in a new place value family (proposed id `place`). It draws the chart and its blocks,
  slides the digit cards on a time slider, fills an empty place with a 0, bundles ten blocks into one of the next
  place and breaks one block into ten. Every number on the chart stays a whole number up to 9999.

## Challenges

### Move the digits onto the target
- Goal: The chart holds the target number.
- Drawn each time: A start number with one or two digits that are not zero, followed by up to three zeros, and one
  move from ×10, ×100, ×1000, ÷10 and ÷100. The target is the start after that move. The start and the target are whole, at most
  9999, and differ from each other. A button whose move would leave the chart is switched off.
- Reached by: the ×10, ×100, ×1000, ÷10 and ÷100 buttons, and Reset to bring back the start.
- Hidden: The name of the move and how many places it slides.
- Idea used: multiplying by 10, multiplying by 100 and 1000, dividing by 10, 100 and 1000.

### Fill the order with the fewest packages
- Goal: The cartons, boxes, bags and loose screws hold exactly the ordered number, with no column at ten or more.
- Drawn each time: An order from 1000 to 9999 screws with at least one digit that is not zero after the
  thousands. The chart opens with no packages, so the page does not open solved.
- Reached by: one stepper for each kind of package, from 0 to 19.
- Hidden: The total of screws in the packages.
- Idea used: ten of one place make one of the next.

### Make the product from a times table fact
- Goal: The first digit times the second digit, moved one place for each zero, equals the target.
- Drawn each time: Two digits from 2 to 9 and a number of zeros from 1 to 3. The target is their product with
  that many zeros, at most 9999. The page opens with both digits at 1 and no zeros, which never makes the target.
- Reached by: a stepper for each digit from 1 to 9 and a stepper for the number of zeros from 0 to 3.
- Hidden: The product of the reader's digits and the slide of the cards until the reader taps to multiply.
- Idea used: multiplying by 10, 100 and 1000, multiplying by a multiple of ten.
