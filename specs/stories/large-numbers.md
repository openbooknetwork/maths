# `large-numbers` Large numbers

- Level: beginner, building on the first ideas
- Needs: `hundreds-and-thousands`
- Leads to: `rounding-and-estimating`
- Sections: ten thousands, hundred thousands and a million, digits in groups of three, reading and writing a large
  number, comparing and ordering, counting on in thousands and ten thousands
- In the world: the crowds that stadiums hold, the populations of towns and cities read from a road sign
- Figure: a row of up to seven digit wheels with a gap after every three places from the right, and a block view
  above that repeats cube, rod and flat in each group. The reader turns the wheels, taps + and − on a place, and
  drags blocks to join ten of a kind into the next.
- Engine: `wheels` and `blocks` from `hundreds-and-thousands`. `blocks` adds the next three shapes, a long rod of
  ten thousand, a big flat of a hundred thousand and a big cube of a million, drawn at a smaller scale. `wheels`
  adds the gaps between groups, the name in words for up to seven digits, and number cards that drop into places.

## Challenges

### Set the wheels to the number in words
- Goal: the wheels show the number named in words.
- Drawn each time: a number of five to seven digits with at least one 0 inside it, and in one draw of two a whole
  group of 000. The wheels start at 0, so the page opens unsolved.
- Reached by: turning the seven wheels.
- Hidden: the name in words of what the wheels show.
- Idea used: digits in groups of three, reading and writing a large number.

### Make the greatest number from these digits
- Goal: the cards make the greatest number their digits can make.
- Drawn each time: six digits from 0 to 9 with at least four different values and at least one 0. The cards start
  in a shuffled order that is not from largest to smallest and does not begin with 0.
- Reached by: dragging a card onto another place to swap the two.
- Hidden: nothing, because no readout points to the answer.
- Idea used: comparing and ordering.

### Reach the target in thousands and ten thousands
- Goal: the wheels show the target.
- Drawn each time: a start from 100 000 to 899 000 that is a multiple of 1 000 and a target that differs from it by
  2 to 9 ten thousands and 1 to 9 thousands, up or down, with the two numbers in different hundred thousands and the target from 100 000 to 999 000. The wheels start\n  at the start number, so the page opens unsolved.
- Reached by: the +1 000, −1 000, +10 000 and −10 000 buttons. The wheels themselves are locked.
- Hidden: the difference between the wheels and the target.
- Idea used: six digit numbers, comparing, counting on in thousands and ten thousands.
