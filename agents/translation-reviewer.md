# This book's rules for a translation reviewer

Read `agents/translating.md` first. Its rule "A term is looked up" comes before every step: a term of
`agents/glossary.md` takes its word from `i18n/terms/<lang>.yaml`, and a term with no entry there stops the work on
that term until the `term-finder` has looked it up.

Then read `agents/text.md`, since a translated text keeps the shape of the English: the idea, the example, the
figure.
