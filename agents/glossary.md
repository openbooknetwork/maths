# Glossary

The terms the stories give their reader. A term is written one way across the book and translated one way per
language. The `terms` agent adds to this file as each story is planned, and a writer, a reviewer and a translator
follow it.

An entry is `term (scope)`: the scope is the kind of thing the term is said of. A word with two meanings has two
entries, one per scope, and a page that uses a second sense says what the word means there. An entry stays as it
is written once a story uses it. A change to one is agreed with the user, and then made in every story that uses
the term.

| Term | Meaning | Introduced in |
|---|---|---|
| pair (objects) | two objects, one from each of two groups, joined by a pair line | `more-and-less`, `matching` |
| partner (objects) | the object another object is paired with | `more-and-less`, `matching` |
| pair line (figure) | the line that joins the two objects of a pair | `more-and-less`, `matching` |
| the same (groups) | every object of each group has a partner in the other | `more-and-less`, `matching` |
| more (groups) | has objects left over once the two groups are paired | `more-and-less`, `more-and-fewer` |
| fewer (groups) | the other group has objects left over once the two are paired | `more-and-less`, `more-and-fewer` |
| difference (groups) | the number of objects left over, which answers "how many more" and "how many fewer" | `more-and-less`, `more-and-fewer` |
| one more (numbers) | the next number of the count | `more-and-less`, `one-more-one-less` |
| one less (numbers) | the number before in the count | `more-and-less`, `one-more-one-less` |
| one fewer (groups) | a group with one object taken away | `more-and-less`, `one-more-one-less` |
| the number before, the number after (numbers) | the numbers on either side of a number in the count | `more-and-less`, `one-more-one-less` |
| greater (numbers) | comes later in the count | `more-and-less`, `comparing-numbers` |
| less (numbers) | comes earlier in the count | `more-and-less`, `comparing-numbers` |
| least, greatest (numbers) | of several numbers, the one less than each other and the one greater than each other | `more-and-less`, `comparing-numbers` |
| full row, last row (counters) | of a number laid out in rows of ten, a row of ten counters, and the row after them | `more-and-less`, `comparing-numbers` |
| in order (numbers) | from the least to the greatest | `more-and-less`, `comparing-numbers` |
| sign (comparison) | one of <, > and =, written between two numbers | `more-and-less`, `signs` |
| is less than, is greater than, is equal to (signs) | how <, > and = are read | `more-and-less`, `signs` |
| equal (numbers) | both sides are the same number | `more-and-less`, `signs` |
| true, false (a line with a sign) | the line says what holds for its two numbers, or it does not | `more-and-less`, `signs` |

## Rules that tell two words apart

- Fewer is the word for objects, and less is the word for numbers.
- Two groups are "the same". Two numbers are "the same" before the story `more-and-less` reaches its section
  `signs`, and "equal" from there on.
