The figure draws the pair lines itself, and a ring marks each counter without a partner. The number of counters without a partner is [3](@unpaired). Tap + on the stepper of the top row until no ring is left.

When every counter of both rows has a partner, the two groups are the same, and the words "The same" show between the rows. Add one counter to each row, and the groups are still the same. Take one counter away from one row, and a counter of the other row loses its partner.
