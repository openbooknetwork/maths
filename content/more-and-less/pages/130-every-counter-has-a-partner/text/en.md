Two groups are the same when every object in each group has a partner in the other group. When two groups are the same, the pairs show what a change does. If you add one object to each group, the two new objects make a new pair, so the groups stay the same. If you take an object away from one group, its partner in the other group is left without one, and the groups are no longer the same.

Say the top row has 4 counters and the bottom row has 7. The 4 top counters pair with 4 bottom counters, and 3 bottom counters have no partner. Add 3 counters to the top row, and each one pairs with one of those 3. Now every counter has a partner, and the rows are the same.

Here the figure draws the pair lines for you, and a ring marks each counter without a partner.

- Press + on the "Top row" stepper and watch the rings in the bottom row. Each press gives one of those counters a partner and takes its ring away, until no ring is left and the words "The same" appear between the rows.
- Press + once on each stepper, and the rows are the same again.
- While the rows are the same, press − on either stepper, and a counter in the other row loses its partner.
