Saucers stand in a row on a table, and the cups wait on a tray. Is there a cup for every saucer? Drag each cup onto a saucer, or tap a cup to send it to the first empty saucer. A saucer holds one cup.

Every cup finds a saucer, and one saucer stays empty with a ring around it. The answer is no, and you found it with no counting.

This story compares two groups, then two numbers, and ends on the signs <, > and =.
