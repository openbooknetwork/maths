You can find out whether two groups are the same size without counting either of them. Put each object from one group with one object from the other, one at a time, until one group runs out. If nothing is left over in the other group either, the two groups are the same size. If something is left over, that group is the bigger one.

Say a table is laid with a row of saucers, and the cups are together on a tray. Is there a cup for every saucer? You take the cups one at a time and put each one on its own saucer. When the tray is empty, one saucer still has no cup, so the answer is no. You found that out without counting the cups or the saucers.

The cups are on the tray at the top, and the saucers are on the table below.

- Drag a cup onto a saucer, or tap a cup to put it on the first empty saucer. A saucer holds only one cup.
- Tap a cup on a saucer to send it back to the tray.
- When the tray is empty, a ring marks the saucer that has no cup.
