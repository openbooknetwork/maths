In musical chairs, the players walk round a row of chairs while the music plays, and there is one chair fewer than there are players. When the music stops, everyone tries to sit on a chair. The player left standing is out, and one chair is taken away. The game goes on until only 1 player is left, and that player wins.

Each chair takes one player, so the chairs and the players who sit down make pairs. There is one more player than there are chairs, so one player is left over in every round. With 6 players and 5 chairs, 5 players sit down and the 1 player left over is out. Then one chair goes, which leaves 5 players for 4 chairs, so again 1 player is left over. The rounds go on like this until 2 players are left for 1 chair.

The players are in a row above the chairs.

- Press "Stop the music". Each chair takes the player above it, and a ring marks the player left over.
- Press "Next round". That player leaves, and so does one chair.
- Play on until the winner gets a crown.
