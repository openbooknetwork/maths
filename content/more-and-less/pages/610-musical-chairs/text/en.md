Players walk round a row of chairs while music plays. There is one chair fewer than there are players. Press Stop the music, and each player takes a chair. A ring marks the one player left over, who is out.

Press Next round, and that player leaves with one chair, so the next round again has one chair fewer than it has players. The number of players is [6](@top) and the number of chairs is [5](@bottom).

The game ends with 1 player, who wins. Press Play again to start a new game. One chair fewer leaves one player without a seat in every round.
