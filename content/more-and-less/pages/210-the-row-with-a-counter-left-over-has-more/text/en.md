Two rows of counters are paired, and a ring marks each counter left over without a partner. The row with counters left over has more, and the other row has fewer. When every counter has a partner, the rows have the same. A tag at the end of each row says its word.

The top row opens with 6 counters and the bottom row with 4. Two top counters are left over, so the top row has more than the bottom row, and the bottom row has fewer than the top row. Knowing which row has more also tells which row has fewer.

Tap − on the stepper of the top row until the tags change, then tap it once more. The tag of the top row reads [more](@top-word) and the tag of the bottom row reads [fewer](@bottom-word). The number of counters without a partner is [2](@unpaired). The pairs decide which row has more, whatever space a row takes.
