The counters stand in rows of ten. A ring marks the last counter, and a dashed outline marks the place where the next counter goes. The three tiles under the rows show a number with the numbers on either side of it. The left tile carries the words one less, and the right tile carries the words one more.

The number of counters is [38](@count). The number before is [37](@before), which is one less. The number after is [39](@after), which is one more. Every number from 1 to 99 has a number before it and a number after it.

Start at 38 and tap the right tile until the group reaches 41. One more than 39 is 40, which fills a row. One more than 40 is 41, which starts a new row. Then tap the left tile to go back. One less than 41 is 40, the last number of the row above. One less than 40 is 39, and that row is one counter short again. Keep tapping down to 30. One less than 30 is 29, and the same thing happens one row higher.

One more and one less work the same way at every ten up to 100.
