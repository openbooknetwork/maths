The top row has 9 counters and the bottom row has 6, and both rows stay as they are. On this page you set the sign. Tap the sign under the rows, and it changes from < to = to > and then to < again.

A line of two numbers and a sign says something that is true or false. The line now reads [9 is less than 6](@reading), and the line is [false](@verdict). Beside the line a mark shows a tick with the word "true" or a cross with the word "false". The line 9 < 6 says that 9 is less than 6. That is false, because the top row has the counters without a partner.

Tap the sign until the line is true. For two numbers, one of the three signs makes a true line and the other two make false lines. For 9 and 6 the true line is 9 > 6.
