Change the top row until the two rows are the same, then press Check. The rows are the same when every counter in each row has a partner. The ends of the rows do not tell you that, because one row is spaced more widely than the other. Pair the counters instead, and the counters left without a partner show you what to change.

Picture a top row of 13 counters over a bottom row of 11. You pair them up, and once every bottom counter has a partner, 2 top counters are still without one, so the top row has 2 counters too many. Take those 2 away, and every counter has a partner. If bottom counters were left over instead, you would add that many to the top row.

The rows on the figure have no lines yet.

- Drag from a top counter to a bottom counter to draw a pair line. Once no more pairs fit, a ring marks each counter without a partner.
- Press + or − on the "Top row" stepper to add or take away a counter at the right end of the top row. If you pair from the left, the counters left over are at that end.
- Press "Another one" for two new rows.
