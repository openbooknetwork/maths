Two rows stand paired, each counter of the top row straight above its partner. Move the Spacing slider to spread the top row out until it is twice as long as the bottom row. Each pair line stays on its two counters, so the lines after the first one slant. Every counter keeps its partner.

The number of counters without a partner stays [0](@unpaired), so the two groups are still the same. A longer row looks like a bigger group. The space that a row takes does not decide whether two groups are the same. The pair lines decide it.
