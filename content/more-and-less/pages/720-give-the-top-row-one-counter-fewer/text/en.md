The figure shows two rows of counters, and the rows carry no numbers. Press + or − on the Top row stepper until the top row has one counter fewer than the bottom row, then press Check.

You can tell without counting. Drag from a counter of one row to a counter of the other row to pair them. When the top row has one counter fewer, one counter of the bottom row stays without a partner.

When the tick shows, the figure writes the number of each row. The top number is one less than the bottom number. Press Another one for two new rows.
