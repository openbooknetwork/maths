When you count a group, the last number you say tells you how many objects there are. Put another object in, and your count goes one number further. So one more than a number is the next number in the count. Take one object away, and your count stops one number earlier, so one less is the number before.

Say a group has 6 counters, and you put in another one. When you count the group, the 6 counters you had get the numbers 1 to 6, and the new one gets the next number, 7. If you pair the group now with the group as it was, you get 6 pairs, and the new counter is left over. So 7 is one more than 6, and 6 is one less than 7.

The top row, labelled "now", shows the group as it is. The bottom row, labelled "before", shows the same group one step earlier. A ring marks the counter without a partner.

- Tap + on the "Top row" stepper. The Now readout shows the number after the one on the Before readout.
- Tap − to take a counter away. Now the counter without a partner is in the bottom row, because the group had one more counter before.
