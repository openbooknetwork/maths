Two rows of counters stand paired, and a ring marks each counter without a partner. Under the rows stands a line: the number of the top row first, then a sign, then the number of the bottom row. The sign writes down what the pairing shows. The number of the top row is [5](@top), the number of the bottom row is [8](@bottom), and the line reads [5 is less than 8](@reading).

There are three signs. The sign < reads "is less than", the sign > reads "is greater than" and the sign = reads "is equal to". The open side of < and > faces the greater number, and the point faces the number that is less.

Tap + of the top row until the top row has more counters than the bottom row, and watch the sign at each step. Rings in the bottom row mean that the second number is greater, and the sign opens toward it. Rings in the top row turn the sign round. With no ring the two rows are the same and the sign is =. The two numbers are then equal, and both sides of = are the same number.
