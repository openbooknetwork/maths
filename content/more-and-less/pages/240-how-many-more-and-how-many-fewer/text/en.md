The top row opens with 9 counters and the bottom row with 5, so the top row has 4 more and the bottom row has 4 fewer. Make the rows the same. Tap + on the stepper of the bottom row to add a counter marked with a plus, or − on the stepper of the top row to leave an empty place.

Added is [0](@added) and Taken away is [0](@taken). The tag of the top row reads [more](@top-word) and the tag of the bottom row reads [fewer](@bottom-word). From the opening rows, adding to the bottom row alone takes 4 counters, and those 4 are how many more the top row had. Taking from the top row alone takes 4 counters too, and those 4 are how many fewer the bottom row had. Do some of each, and the rows are the same when Added and Taken away make 4 together.

The number that answers both questions is the difference between the two rows. Fewer is the word for objects, and less is the word for numbers. 5 counters are fewer than 9 counters, and 5 is less than 9.
