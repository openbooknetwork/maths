The counters of these two rows stay in place, and a line does the work that putting a cup on a saucer did. Drag from a counter of the top row to a counter of the bottom row to draw a line between them, or tap one and then the other. A tap on a paired counter takes its line away.

Two counters joined by a line are a pair, and each is the partner of the other. The line is a pair line. A counter takes one line, so it has one partner at most.

Draw lines until no more pairs fit. Every counter of the bottom row has a partner, and two counters of the top row have none. A ring marks each of the two. Counters left without a partner show that the two groups are not the same.
