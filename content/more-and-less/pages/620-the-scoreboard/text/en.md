A football scoreboard shows how many goals each team has scored. A match is level when the two scores are equal, and every match starts level, at 0 and 0. A team leads when it has scored more goals than the other team. If you put a sign between the two scores, it tells you who leads, because its open side faces the greater score. When the match is level, the sign is =.

The home team's score is on the left, and the away team's is on the right. At the start the number sentence is `0 = 0`. Say the home team scores. 1 is greater than 0, so the open side of the sign faces the home team's score, and the number sentence is `1 > 0`. If the away team scores next, both scores are 1, the match is level again, and the number sentence is `1 = 1`.

A ball appears under a team's score for each goal it scores.

- Press "Home goal" or "Away goal" to give that team a goal.
- After each goal, tell who leads from the sign alone.
- Press "New match" to put both scores back to 0.

The "In words" readout reads the score out, now [0 is equal to 0](@reading).
