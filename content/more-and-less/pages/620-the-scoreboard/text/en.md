A football match between the home team and the away team starts at 0 and 0. The scoreboard shows the score of the home team on the left, under Home, and the score of the away team on the right, under Away. It keeps a sign between the two scores. Press Home goal or Away goal to score a goal, and press New match to start again. A ball lies under a score for each goal.

A match is level when the two scores are equal, and every match starts level, with the sign =. A team leads when its score is greater. A goal for the home team at the start makes the line 1 > 0, and the open side of the sign faces the score of the team in the lead. A goal for the away team then brings the match level again, at 1 = 1.
