A hundred counters stand in rows of ten. The marker of the first number is a ring with a round pin above its counter, and it stands on [18](@top). The marker of the second number is a square frame with a square pin below its counter, and it stands on [25](@bottom). Drag either slider, and its marker runs along the count. The line under the rows reads [18 is less than 25](@reading).

The page opens with the markers on 18 and 25. Counting from 1 reaches 18 first and goes on 7 counters to 25. The number that the count reaches later is the greater, so 25 is greater than 18, and 18 is less than 25.

The greater number holds every counter of the less number and some more. The filled counters belong to both numbers. The hollow counters with a heavy outline are the ones that only the greater number has. More and fewer are the words for objects, and greater and less are the words for numbers. Put both markers on one counter, and the line says that the two numbers are the same.
