Set the sign that makes the number sentence true, then press Check. The numbers are hidden, so you compare the rows by pairing their counters. The row with counters left over has more, so its number is greater, and the open side of the sign faces that number. If every counter has a partner, the numbers are equal and the sign is =.

Picture 12 counters in the top row and 15 in the bottom row. When you pair them, 3 bottom counters are left over, so the bottom row has more. Its number is on the right, so the open side faces right, and the number sentence is `12 < 15`.

The left card hides the top row's number, and the right card hides the bottom row's. One row is spread out, so the ends of the rows do not tell you which has more.

- Drag between a top counter and a bottom counter to pair them. Tap a paired counter to take its line away.
- Tap the sign to change it.
- Press Check. If the sign is wrong, a note says whether the top row has more or fewer counters than the sign says. If it is right, the cards turn over to show the numbers.
