The two rows show no numbers. Under the rows two cards with a question mark stand in place of the numbers, the left card for the top row and the right card for the bottom row. The sign between the cards makes a false line. One row stands wide and the other close, so the ends of the rows do not show which row has more.

Drag from a top counter to a bottom counter to draw a pair line. The pair lines show which row has counters without a partner. Tap the sign until its open side faces the card of the row with more, or until it shows = when every counter has a partner.

Then press Check. When the line is true, the two cards turn to show the numbers. Press Another one for two new rows.
