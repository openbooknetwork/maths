This story has shown you how to compare two groups and two numbers.

- Two groups are the same when every object has a partner after you pair them.
- The group with objects left over has more, and the number left over tells you how many more.
- One more than a number is the next number in the count, and one less is the number before it.
- Of two numbers, the one that comes later in the count is greater.
- A sign writes a comparison down as a number sentence, and its open side faces the greater number.

The figure starts with two rows of 8 counters. Every counter has a partner, so the two numbers are equal, and the number sentence is `8 = 8`. If you add one counter to the bottom row, that counter has no partner, so the bottom row has 1 more. The rows now have 8 and 9 counters, and 9 comes later in the count, so the number sentence is `8 < 9`. Try it with + on the "Bottom row" stepper, and watch the sign change.

The "In words" readout now reads [8 is equal to 8](@reading).
