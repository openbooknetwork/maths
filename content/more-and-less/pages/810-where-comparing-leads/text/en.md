The line under the two rows reads [8 is equal to 8](@reading). Tap + and − on either row, and the pairs, the rings and the sign follow.

Pairing shows whether two groups are the same. The counters without a partner say which group has more and how many more. Of two numbers, the one that comes later in the count is the greater. A sign writes the comparison down as a line.

Comparing leads to comparing lengths, adding within 20, hundreds and thousands, and times as many.
