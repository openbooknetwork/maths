Two moves can be made one after the other. Does the order matter? Try it with the turn R and the shear S.

Here the plane turns first and then shears. Press Start to watch both moves. The matrix that does both is

[S R = ((1, 1), (0, 1)) ((0, −1), (1, 0)) = ((1, −1), (1, 0))](@formula-turn-90-then-shear)

S stands on the left because it acts second, on what R has made.
