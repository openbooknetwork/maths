A transformation moves every point of the plane by one rule. The simplest rule slides every point the same distance in the same direction. This is a translation.

The dashed house is where we started. Its corner P = [(−3, −2)](@P) slid to P′ = [(1, 1)](@image). Every other point made the same trip, so the house keeps its size, its shape and the way it faces.

The trip is a vector, an arrow with a length and a direction. Here the vector is [(4, 3)](@vector). It goes [4](@tx) across and [3](@ty) up. Add it to P to get P′:

[P′ = P + v = (−3, −2) + (4, 3) = (1, 1)](@formula-image)

Drag the house, or the knob at the tip of the arrow. A negative number across means left, and a negative number up means down. Can you slide the house back onto its start? Then the vector is (0, 0).
