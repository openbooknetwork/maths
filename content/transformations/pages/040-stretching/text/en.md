An enlargement stretches the plane out from a centre C. Every point moves along the line from C through it, and its distance from C is multiplied by the scale factor k.

Here C = [(−2, −2)](@centre) and k = [2](@k). To find P′, take the step from C to P = [(−1, −1)](@P), multiply it by k and add it to C:

[P′ = C + k × (P − C) = (−2, −2) + 2 × (1, 1) = (0, 0)](@formula-image)

With k = 2 every length of the house doubles. It gets twice as wide and twice as tall, so its area is 4 times as big. In general the area is multiplied by k × k, which is now [4](@area-scale).

Drag the knob at P′ to change k. A k between 0 and 1 shrinks the house. A negative k sends it through C to the other side, upside down. Drag C to move the centre.
