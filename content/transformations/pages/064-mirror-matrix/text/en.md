This matrix flips the plane in the y-axis. The arrow i goes over to (−1, 0), and j stays where it is:

[M = ((−1, 0), (0, 1))](@formula-mirror)

Press Start, or drag the time slider slowly. The plane gets narrower as i shrinks towards (0, 0). Halfway through, the plane is folded flat. Then it opens out on the other side, and the F comes out reading backwards.
