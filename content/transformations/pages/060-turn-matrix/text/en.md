Some matrices come up so often that they have names. Here the plane makes a quarter turn around (0, 0), against the clock.

Press Start to watch the plane move from where it started to where the turn sends it. The time slider goes back through the move. The matrix under the figure follows the plane all the way.

The turn sends i up to (0, 1) and j over to (−1, 0). Read off the columns and you have its matrix:

[R = ((0, −1), (1, 0))](@formula-turn-90)
