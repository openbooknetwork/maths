A turn, or rotation, swings the plane around one fixed point, the centre C. Every point travels along a circle around C, and every point turns through the same angle.

Here the F turned [90°](@angle) around C = [(0, 0)](@centre). Its corner P = [(2, 0)](@P) went to P′ = [(0, 2)](@image).

Drag the knob at P′, or the F itself, to turn it. A turn against the clock has a positive angle. Turn with the clock and the angle is negative.

Now drag C somewhere else. The F turns through the same angle around the new centre, so it lands somewhere new. Whatever you do, the F keeps its size and shape and still reads as an F.
