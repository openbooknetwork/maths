The rule of a parabola is y = ax² + bx + c. It has three numbers in it, a, b and c. Each slider changes one of them and the curve follows. The dashed curve shows where it started.

c lifts the whole curve up or down. It is the height where the curve crosses the y axis, now [−1](@c).

a sets how steep the sides are. Make a negative and the parabola turns upside down. At a = 0 the x² part is gone and a straight line is left.

The lowest point of the parabola is its vertex, now at [(0, −1)](@vertex). Move b away from 0 and the vertex slides sideways and down.
