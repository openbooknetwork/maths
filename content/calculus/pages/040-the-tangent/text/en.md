On the last page the slope settled close to 1 as Q came near P. That number is the slope of the curve at P.

Here Q sits a gap h to the right of P, and the dashed secant runs through both. Now its slope is [1.5](@secant). Shrink h and the secant turns until it lies on the solid line, the tangent. The tangent touches the curve at P and runs the way the curve runs there. Its slope is [1](@slope).

Take one step of 1 to the right along the tangent and it climbs by its slope.

Drag P along the curve. The tangent tilts with it. Where the curve falls the slope is below 0, and at the bottom of the curve it is 0.
