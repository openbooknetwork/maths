How much space lies under the curve between x = 0 and x = 4? The curve bends, so no simple shape fits it. Rectangles help.

Cut the stretch into n strips of the same width and stand a rectangle in each, as tall as the curve at its left edge. With [4](@n) rectangles they cover [7.5](@sum) square units.

The rectangles miss the slivers between their tops and the curve, a difference of [1.83](@difference). Raise n. The strips get thinner, the slivers shrink, and the total creeps up toward the true area, [9.33](@area).

With 40 rectangles the difference is down to 0.2. More rectangles bring it as close to 0 as you like.
