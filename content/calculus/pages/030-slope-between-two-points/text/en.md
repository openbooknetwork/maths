How steep is a curve? Pick two points on it, P and Q. Going from P to Q, the run is how far you move across and the rise is how far you climb.

The slope is the rise for each step across:

[slope = rise / run = 4 / 2 = 2](@formula-slope)

The line through P and Q is called a secant. Its slope is how steep the curve is on average between the two points.

Drag Q toward P. The secant turns and its slope changes. With Q very close to P the slope settles close to 1.

If Q lands on P, the run is 0 and the slope has no value. The next page finds a way around that.
