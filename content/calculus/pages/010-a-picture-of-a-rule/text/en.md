A rule takes a number x and gives back a number y. The rule y = 0.5x + 1 halves x and adds 1.

Here is the rule at work for x = [2](@x):

[y = 0.5x + 1 = 0.5 × 2 + 1 = 2](@formula-y)

Each x and its y make a point (x, y). The graph of the rule is all of those points at once, so it shows the whole rule in one picture.

Drag the point along the graph, or tap anywhere on the figure. The dashed lines read x on the horizontal axis and y on the vertical one.

Pick another rule under the graph. The line bends into a parabola, a cubic or a sine wave, and the point keeps its x. The working above follows the rule you pick.
