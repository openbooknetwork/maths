A regular hexagon has six equal sides and six equal angles. One fits inside a circle with each side as long as the radius.

Draw a circle about [A](@A) through the centre [O](@O). It has the same radius as the given circle and cuts it at two corners of the hexagon.

Rule the line through O and A. It meets the circle again on the far side. A circle about that point through O gives two more corners.

Now join the six corners in order with the ruler. That makes [9](@fewest) steps in all, so Hint helps when you lose your way.
