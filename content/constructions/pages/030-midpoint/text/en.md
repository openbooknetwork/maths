The same two circles find the middle of a segment. Each place where they cross lies as far from A as from B.

Draw the circle about A through B and the circle about B through A. Then rule the line through the two places where they cross.

Every point of that line lies as far from A as from B. It meets [AB](@AB) at a right angle and cuts it into two equal halves. It is called the perpendicular bisector of AB.

Mark the point where it crosses AB with the [point](@point) tool. That point is the midpoint. The whole construction takes [4](@fewest) steps.
