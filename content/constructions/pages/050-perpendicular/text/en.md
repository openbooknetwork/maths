A right angle is a quarter turn. To raise one on the line [AB](@AB) at the point [P](@P), first find two points on the line, one on each side of P and as far from it.

A circle about P through [A](@A) does this. It meets the line at A and at a second point on the other side of P.

P is the midpoint of those two points, so the method of the midpoint page works here. Draw a circle about each of them through the other, and rule the line through the places where they cross. It passes through P at a right angle to AB.

The construction takes [4](@fewest) steps, and the counter shows [0](@steps) so far.
