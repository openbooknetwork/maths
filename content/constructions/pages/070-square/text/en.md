A square needs a right angle at each end of [AB](@AB). This construction uses a fact that Thales found. A triangle in a circle with one side along a diameter has a right angle opposite that side.

Draw the circles about A through B and about B through A, and take the crossing above AB. Draw a circle about that crossing through A. It passes through B as well.

Rule the line from A through the crossing. It meets the new circle again at the far end of a diameter. The line from B through that point stands at a right angle to AB.

The circle about B through A cuts that line at the third corner. A circle about the third corner through B cuts the circle about A at the fourth. Join the corners. It takes [8](@fewest) steps.
