An angle opens between two arms that start at one corner, here [O](@O). The goal is the line that splits the angle into two equal angles.

Draw a circle about O through [B](@B). It cuts the other arm at a point as far from O as B is.

Now draw two circles of the same size. One goes about B through that new point, and the other about the new point through B. They cross on the line that halves the angle. Rule the line from O through the crossing far from O.

Fold the figure along that line and one arm lands on the other. The construction takes [4](@fewest) steps.
