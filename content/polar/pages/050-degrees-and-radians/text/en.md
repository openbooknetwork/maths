A degree is a full turn cut into 360 parts. You can also measure an angle by the arc it cuts from a circle.

Divide the length of the arc by the radius. The result is the angle in radians, and it is the same for a small circle and a large one. Change r and watch. The arc changes, and the angle in radians stays the same.

A full turn is 2π radians, so 180° is π radians.

[θ = 60° × π / 180° ≈ 1.05](@formula-radians)

With θ in radians, the length of the arc is the radius times the angle:

[s = r θ = 3 × 1.05 ≈ 3.14](@formula-arc)

Set θ so that the arc is as long as the radius. That angle is one radian, which is a little over 57°.
