On a plane, x and y say how far to walk along each axis. Polar coordinates say the same place another way. You turn to face a direction, then walk straight.

The point is [4](@r) from the origin. That distance is r.

The direction is the angle θ, turned anticlockwise from the ray that points right. Here θ is [30°](@angle).

Together they are written (r, θ). Drag the point or move the sliders. One slider walks the point along its ray, and the other swings it round the origin.
