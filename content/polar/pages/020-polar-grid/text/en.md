Square paper suits x and y. Polar coordinates have a grid of their own.

Every point on one circle has the same r. Every point on one ray has the same θ. The point at ([3](@r), [120°](@angle)) sits where its circle crosses its ray.

Keep r still and change θ. The point runs round a circle. Keep θ still and change r. The point runs along a ray.

A full turn is 360°, and it brings the point back to where 0° put it.
