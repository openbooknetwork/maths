The same point has an x and a y. Drop a line from the point to the x axis and a right triangle appears, with r as its long side.

The cosine of θ gives the side along the x axis:

[x = r cos θ = 4 cos 30° ≈ 3.46](@formula-x)

The sine of θ gives the side that stands up:

[y = r sin θ = 4 sin 30° = 2](@formula-y)

Swing the point past 90° and x turns negative. Past 180°, y turns negative too. The two formulas hold all the way round.
