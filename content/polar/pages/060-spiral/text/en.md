A rule that gives r for every θ draws a curve. The simplest rule lets r grow in step with θ, measured in radians:

[r = a θ = 0.25 × 6.28 ≈ 1.57](@formula-r)

Sweep the angle and the point winds outwards. Each full turn adds the same distance to r, so the arms of the spiral are evenly spaced.

Change [0.25](@a) to wind the spiral tighter or looser.
