A regular polygon has equal sides and equal angles. This one has [6](@n) sides of length [2](@side).

The corners together hold 180° for every side after the second.

[S = (n − 2) × 180° = (6 − 2) × 180° = 720°](@formula-angle-sum)

The corners are equal, so each one takes an equal share of that sum.

[α = ((n − 2) × 180°) / n = ((6 − 2) × 180°) / 6 = 120°](@formula-angle)

The area follows from the number of sides and their length. Add sides and the shape closes in on a circle.

[A = (n × s²) / (4 × tan(180° / n)) = (6 × 2²) / (4 × tan(180° / 6)) ≈ 10.39](@formula-area)
