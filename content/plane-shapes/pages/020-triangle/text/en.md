Three points joined by three sides make a triangle. Drag [A](@A), [B](@B) or [C](@C) to change it.

The area is half the base times the height. The base is the side from A to B, and the height is how far C stands from it.

[A = ½ × b × h = ½ × 6 × 4 = 12](@formula-area)

Slide C along a line parallel to the base and the area stays the same, because the height does.

The angles are [63.4°](@angle-a), [45°](@angle-b) and [71.6°](@angle-c). However you move the corners, they add up to the same sum.

[S = (n − 2) × 180° = (3 − 2) × 180° = 180°](@formula-angle-sum)
