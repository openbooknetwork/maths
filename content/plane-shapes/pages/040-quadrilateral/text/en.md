A quadrilateral is any shape with four straight sides. Drag its corners to make a kite, a trapezium or a dart.

A diagonal splits it into two triangles, and their areas add up to the area of the whole.

[A = A(ABC) + A(ACD) = 12 + 5.5 = 17.5](@formula-area)

Two triangles also explain the angles. Each triangle holds 180°, so the four corners hold twice that.

[S = (n − 2) × 180° = (4 − 2) × 180° = 360°](@formula-angle-sum)
