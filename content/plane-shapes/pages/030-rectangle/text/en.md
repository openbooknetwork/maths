A rectangle has four right angles. Its width is [5](@width) and its height is [3](@height).

The area is the width times the height.

[A = w × h = 5 × 3 = 15](@formula-area)

The perimeter is the distance around the rectangle. It is twice the width plus twice the height.

[P = 2 × (w + h) = 2 × (5 + 3) = 16](@formula-perimeter)

Make the width and the height equal and the rectangle is a square.
