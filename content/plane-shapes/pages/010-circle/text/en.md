A circle is every point at one distance from a centre. That distance is the radius, and here it is [2](@radius).

The area is π times the radius squared.

[A = πr² = π × 2² = 4π ≈ 12.57](@formula-area)

Double the radius and the number in front of π grows four times. The area grows four times as well.

Two radii cut a sector out of the circle. With [60°](@angle) between them the sector takes the same share of the area as the angle takes of a full turn.

[A(s) = θ / 360° × πr² = 60° / 360° × 4π ≈ 2.09](@formula-sector-area)
