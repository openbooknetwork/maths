The degree of a vertex is the number of edges that meet it. Each vertex shows its degree in the small circle beside it. [Vertex B](@B) has degree [4](@degree-B).

Add up the degrees of all the vertices:

[d(A) + d(B) + d(C) + d(D) + d(E) + d(F) = 2 + 4 + 2 + 3 + 2 + 3 = 16 = 2 × 8](@formula-handshake)

The sum is twice the number of edges, [8](@edges). Each edge has two ends, and each end adds one to the degree of a vertex. This is the handshake rule. Count every handshake each guest at a party makes, and you count each handshake twice.

A vertex with an odd degree has a dashed ring. This graph has [2](@odd-nodes) of them. Join and remove edges and watch the sum. It stays even, so the number of odd vertices is even too.
