A graph is a set of dots joined by lines. The dots are called vertices, and the lines are called edges. This graph has [5](@nodes) vertices and [6](@edges) edges.

A graph records only which vertices are joined. Drag [vertex A](@A) anywhere. Its edges stretch to follow it, and the graph stays the same.

Pick Add and tap an empty spot to make a new vertex. Pick Join and drag from one vertex to another to draw an edge between them. Pick Remove and tap a vertex or an edge to take it away. A removed vertex takes its edges with it.

Graphs describe anything made of things and the links between them: friends who know each other, towns joined by roads, web pages joined by links.
