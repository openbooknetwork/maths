One flip tells you little. Many flips tell you a lot. The line shows the share of heads after each flip. The share is the heads so far divided by the flips so far.

Press Start and watch the line. At first the line jumps up and down. After a few hundred flips it settles near [1/2](@p-heads), the probability of heads. This is the law of large numbers. The more trials you run, the closer the share comes to the probability.

The shaded funnel narrows as the flips add up. In about 19 runs out of 20 the share stays inside it.

So far there are [0](@heads) heads in [0](@flips) flips, and the gap between heads and tails is [0](@gap). The gap often grows while the share settles. The share divides by all the flips, so the same gap counts for less and less.

Press Start again for another run. Each run takes its own path and ends near one half.
