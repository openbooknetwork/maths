The figure shows five runs of flips at once, and each run has its own line. The Flips slider sets how many flips the runs have. The shaded band covers the second half of those flips, and it holds every share of heads within 0.05 of one half.

The goal is reached when all five lines stay inside the band all the way across. If a line leaves the band, a ring marks the place. Find how many flips it takes, then press New run to check that it holds for other runs too.
