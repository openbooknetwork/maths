Roll two dice and add them. The sum runs from 2 to 12, and some sums come up much more often than others.

The grid shows all [36](@outcomes) ways the two dice can land, the first die down the side and the second along the top. Each cell is one outcome, and all of them are equally likely. Tap a sum on the chart or a cell in the grid to light the cells that make it.

The picked sum is [7](@pick), and [6](@ways-pick) cells make it. Its probability is its cells out of 36:

[P(7) = 6/36 = 1/6 ≈ 0.167](@formula-p-pick)

Seven has the most cells. Two and twelve have one cell each, a chance of [1/36](@p-2).

Press Start and watch the bars build a peak at 7. The short line across each bar marks the count that sum can expect. Pick another sum and compare its bar with its line.
