A bar model shows a whole and its two parts as lengths. You can get one from a row of counters: give each counter the same length of bar, so each part is as long as its counters together and the bar is as long as the row. The part with the greater number is then the longer part, and two parts are the same length only when their numbers are equal.

There are 5 red marbles and 3 blue marbles in a row. Under the row, draw a bar as long as all 8 marbles, and mark it where the red marbles end. The first part is under the red marbles, so 5 goes in it, and 3 goes in the second part. The first part is longer, because 5 is greater than 3. The whole, 8, goes above the bar. Then you can read the bar from its numbers, without counting.

On the figure the round red counters are the red marbles and the square blue counters are the blue ones. The end of the bar is its right edge, with a handle on it. Drag the end to change the whole. The first part keeps its 5 red counters, so only the second part changes.

- Drag the end to 10. Two square counters join the row, so both parts are 5 and the same length.
- Then drag it to 6. The second part shrinks to 1 counter.
