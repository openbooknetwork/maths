A fact is an addition or a subtraction written with its right answer. When a whole is made of two sorts of things, you only need to count each sort once, and those two counts give you an addition and two subtractions.

A box of 10 eggs has 6 brown eggs and 4 white eggs. The brown eggs are one part, the white eggs the other, and the full box is the whole. 6 and 4 make 10, so `6 + 4 = 10`. Take the 4 white eggs out and the 6 brown eggs are left, so `10 − 4 = 6`. Take the 6 brown eggs out instead and the 4 white eggs are left, so `10 − 6 = 4`.

The three facts are under the box. Tap a fact to see which eggs it is about.

- Tap [6 + 4 = 10](@written), and every egg lights up, because the two parts together fill the box.
- Tap [10 − 4 = 6](@written-minus-second). The white eggs turn into dashed rings, since they are taken out, and the brown eggs that are left light up.
- Tap `10 − 6 = 4` and say which eggs are taken out this time.

Which fact with 6, 4 and 10 could go in the empty place beside `6 + 4 = 10`? It is `4 + 6 = 10`, the parts added the other way round.
