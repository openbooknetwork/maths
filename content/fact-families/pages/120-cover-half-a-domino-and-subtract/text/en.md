When you know a whole and one of its parts, you find the other part by subtracting the part you know from the whole. An addition you already know answers that subtraction without counting.

A domino has 4 dots, called pips, on one half and 3 on the other. The pips on each half are a part, and all 7 pips are the whole, so `4 + 3 = 7`. Say you cover the half with 3 pips. You still know the whole is 7 and can see the 4, so the covered half holds `7 − 4` pips. Since 4 and 3 make 7, subtracting 4 from 7 leaves 3, so `7 − 4 = 3`. If you cover the half with 4 pips instead, it holds `7 − 3`. The same addition answers that too: subtracting 3 from 7 leaves 4, so `7 − 3 = 4`. One addition answered both subtractions.

The line under the domino reads [4 + 3 = 7](@written).

- Tap the half with 3 pips to cover it with a card. The line turns into the subtraction that finds that half. Use `4 + 3 = 7` to answer it before you look.
- Tap the card to lift it and see whether you were right. Then do the same with the other half.
