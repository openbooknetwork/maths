The goal line gives you one fact, and you set the bar model of its family. The three numbers of a fact are a whole and its two parts. In an addition the two parts come before the = sign and the whole after it. In a subtraction you subtract a part from the whole, so the whole comes first, before the − sign, and the other two numbers are the parts.

Take `12 + 7 = 19`. 12 and 7 are added, so they are the parts, and 19 is the whole. Now take `19 − 7 = 12`. It starts with 19, so 19 is the whole, and 7 and 12 are the parts. Both facts give the same bar, a whole of 19 split into 12 and 7. The parts can go either way round, so a bar split into 7 and 12 is right too.

- Drag the end to the fact's whole.
- Drag the split until the number in one part of the bar is one of the fact's two parts. Since the whole is right, the number in the other part is then right too.
- Press Check. If the bar is wrong, a note tells you whether it is too long, too short or split in the wrong place.

Once your bar is right, the rest of the family appears.
