A coach picks a squad for a match. 11 players start on the pitch, and the rest wait on the bench. The coach knows how many are in the squad and on the pitch, so a subtraction tells them how many are on the bench. The squad is the whole, the players on the pitch are the known part, and the players on the bench are the missing part.

With a squad of 14, `14 − 11` asks "11 and what make 14?" You count on from 11 and say 12, 13, 14, one number for each player on the bench. That is three numbers, so 3 players are on the bench: `11 + 3 = 14` and `14 − 11 = 3`. The count is short because the known part, 11, is the greater part.

The squad on the figure has 16 players. The players on the pitch wear red shirts numbered 1 to 11, and the bench under the pitch starts empty. Before you tap, say how many will end up on the bench.

- Tap the bench, and the next player sits down. Each new shirt shows the number your count has reached.
