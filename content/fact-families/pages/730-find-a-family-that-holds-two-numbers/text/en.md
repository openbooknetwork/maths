Find a third number that makes a family with the two numbers in the goal line. A family has a whole and two parts, so first you decide what the two numbers are. If they are the two parts, the third number is the whole, their sum. If the greater number is the whole and the other is a part, the third number is the missing part, the whole minus the known part. Either way counts.

Say the numbers are 15 and 4. As the two parts they make `15 + 4 = 19`, so 19 is one answer. With 15 as the whole and 4 as the known part, the missing part is `15 − 4 = 11`, so 11 works too.

A question mark on the bar stands for the third number. With "the whole and a part" the bar is only a sketch until you find it, so its lengths give nothing away.

- On the "Use them as" switch, pick "the two parts" or "the whole and a part".
- Set the third number on the "Third number" stepper.
- Press Check. If it is wrong, a note compares the numbers to show which way to go.

Once it is right, the bar is drawn to scale, as on "A row of counters becomes a bar", the facts of your family appear, and a line names the other family.
