If you join counters to a group and then take the same ones away, the group is back where it started. Taking them away undoes the join. One calculation undoes another in the same way, when it starts from the other's answer and gives back the number the other started from.

Join 3 blue counters to 6 red ones, and you get `6 + 3 = 9`. Take the same 3 away, and the 6 red counters are left, so `9 − 3 = 6`. This subtraction starts from 9, the sum, and gives back 6, the number you started with, so it undoes the addition.

You have to take away as many counters as you joined. If you take away only 2, `9 − 2 = 7`, which is not 6. `9 − 6 = 3` is in the same family, but it subtracts 6, where you joined 3, so it leaves 3.

The first frame has 6 round red counters, and the tray has 3 square blue ones.

- Drag or tap the blue counters into the frames to join them.
- Then take them back to the tray one at a time. As each one goes back, a second line under the addition writes the subtraction so far, and the "Left" readout goes down by 1.
- When all 3 are back, the second line reads `9 − 3 = 6` and the frames look as they did at the start.
