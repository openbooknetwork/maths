On a number line, you add 8 by counting on 8 numbers. Instead of 8 jumps of 1, you can make one jump of 8 to the right. To subtract 8, you make one jump of 8 to the left and count back 8 numbers. Each number you count back takes back one number you counted on, so after 8 of them you are back on the start. That is why subtracting 8 undoes adding 8.

Start on 9 and jump 8 to the right. You count on 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, so the jump lands on 17 and `9 + 8 = 17`. Then jump 8 back to the left. You count back 16, 15, 14, 13, 12, 11, 10, 9. That is 8 numbers, one for each number you counted on, so you are on 9 again, and `17 − 8 = 9` undoes `9 + 8 = 17`.

The point starts on 9, inside the start ring.

- Press "Jump right". The point jumps to 17 along an arc over the line, and the addition appears under the number line.
- Then press "Jump left". The point jumps back to 9 along an arc under the line, and the subtraction appears under the addition. The two arcs make a loop.
