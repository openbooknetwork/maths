The goal line names a subtraction, and you put in the missing part it asks for. The number before the − sign is the whole. The number after it is the known part, and the red counters in the frames show it. The missing part is how many blue counters you add to the known part to make the whole.

Take `8 − 6`, which asks what you add to 6 to make 8. You count on from 6 and say 7, 8. You said two numbers, so the missing part is 2, and you would put in 2 blue counters.

The counters you put in carry no numbers, so keep the count yourself as you go.

- Tap an empty cell to put a blue counter in the next cell. Tap a blue counter to take the last one out.
- Press Check when you think the blue counters are the missing part. If they are not, a note tells you what your two groups make, so you can see whether to put in more or take some out.
- Press "Another one" for a new subtraction.

Once you have the missing part, the facts of its family appear under the frames.
