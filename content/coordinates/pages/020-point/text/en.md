Cross a second number line over the first at zero. The crossing is the origin, and the two lines are the x axis and the y axis.

A point on the plane needs two numbers. Drop a line from [P](@P) straight down to the x axis and read [3](@x). Draw a line straight across to the y axis and read [2](@y).

The pair is written with x first. Swap the two numbers and you name a different point.
