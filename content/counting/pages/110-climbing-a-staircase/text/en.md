The steps of a staircase are numbered from the bottom: 1, 2, 3 and so on. The floor counts as step 0, because you have not climbed any steps yet. A step's number tells you how many steps you have climbed from the floor. So going up one step is counting on by one, and going down one step is counting back by one.

Say you are standing on step 3. You go up two steps, and you count on from 3: 4, 5. Now you are on step 5. Then you go down three steps, and you count back from 5: 4, 3, 2. You end up on step 2.

The staircase has 20 steps, each with its number on its front, and a person is standing on step 3.

- Drag the person up or down the stairs, and say each step's number as they reach it.
- Climb past step 10, then come back down to the floor.

The person is on step [3](@count) now.
