The goal asks you to take counters away until a smaller number is left. You do it by counting back. Start from how many counters there are, and say the number before each time you take one away. Stop when you say the number the goal asks for. On the way you always count back past a ten, such as from 40 to 39.

The counters are in rows of ten, so you can find how many there are without counting from 1. Start from the ten written at the end of the last full row, and count on along the row after it.

Say there are 22 counters and the goal is 18. That is two full rows, which make 20, and two more counters. You take a counter away and say 21, then 20, then 19, then 18. Four counters are gone, and 18 are left.

- Tap a counter to take it away. Take them from the end of the last row, so the rows stay easy to read.
- Tap an empty place to put its counter back.
- Press Check when you think the right number is left. If it is not, a note tells you whether to take more away or put some back.
