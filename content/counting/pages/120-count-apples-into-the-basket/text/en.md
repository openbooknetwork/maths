Your goal is to put a number of apples in the basket, no more and no fewer. You count the apples as they go in, one number for each apple, and stop as soon as you say the number the goal asks for. The last number you say tells you how many apples are in the basket, so at that moment the basket holds the right number.

Say you need 4 apples. You put one in and say 1, then 2, then 3, then 4. You have just said 4, so you stop, and there are four apples in the basket.

There is a pile of apples next to an empty basket, and the goal tells you how many to put in.

- Drag an apple into the basket, or tap the pile to move the top apple in.
- Tap the basket to take its last apple back out.
- If you lose count, count the apples in the basket again from 1.
- Press Check when you think you are done. If the number is wrong, a note tells you whether to put more in or take some out.
- Press "Another one" to get a new number.
