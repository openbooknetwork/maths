When you know how many counters a group has, you can add more and count on from that number. Each counter you add makes one more, so you say the next number for it. Counting on carries on past ten in the usual order: 9, 10, 11, 12 and so on.

Say the box holds 8 and you put four counters beside it. You start from 8 and say the next number for each counter: 9, 10, 11, 12. The last number is 12, so there are 12 counters in all.

The box holds 5 at first. The dashed space by the box is empty, and the counters you can add are in the pile.

- Tap the pile, or drag a counter from it into the dashed space. Each counter you add gets the next number.
- Tap a counter in the dashed space to put it back on the pile.
- Set the "In the box" stepper to 8, and add four counters to count on past ten.

When you change the number in the box, the counters you added get new numbers that follow on from it. The "Last number said" readout shows how many the box and the counters you added make together, [5](@count) right now.
