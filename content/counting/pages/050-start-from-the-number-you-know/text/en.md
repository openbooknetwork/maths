Sometimes you already know how many objects there are in part of a group. Then you do not need to count that part again. You start from the number you know and say the next number for each object you have not counted yet. This is called counting on.

It works because counting the known part one by one would end on the number you already know. So you can say that number straight away and carry on from there.

Say a closed box holds 4 counters, and there are two more counters beside it. You say 4 for the box. Then you say 5 for the first counter beside it and 6 for the second. The last number you said is 6, so there are six counters in all.

The closed box has 6 written on its front, so you know it holds 6 counters, and there are more counters in the dashed space beside it. Tap each of those counters to count on from 6. The first one you tap gets 7, because you have already said 6 for the box. The "Last number said" readout starts at 6 for the same reason, and it now shows [6](@count). Press Clear to take the numbers off and count on again.
