When you count on past a ten, you finish one row of ten and start the next. When you have counted 39 counters, you have three full rows and nine counters in the fourth row. One more counter fills the fourth row, and four full rows make 40. So 40 comes after 39, and the next number, 41, is the first counter of the fifth row.

Counting back crosses the ten the other way. When you count back one from 40, the fourth row is one counter short of full again, so you get to 39.

Say you count on four from 37. You say 38 and 39 as you go along the fourth row. The 40 is the last counter of that row, so the row is full, and the 41 starts the fifth row.

The marker is on counter [37](@count). That counter shows its number, and every counter up to it is filled in.

- Press + on the Marker stepper to count on past 39, and watch the fourth row fill up and the fifth row begin.
- Press − to count back from 40 to 39.
