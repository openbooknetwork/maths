A ten is the number at the end of a row, so when you count on past a ten, you fill one row and start the next. Say you have counted 39 counters. That is three full rows and nine counters in the fourth row. One more counter fills the fourth row, and four full rows make 40. So 40 comes after 39, and the next number, 41, is the first counter of the fifth row.

Counting back crosses the ten the other way. When you count back one from 40, the fourth row is one counter short of full again, so you get to 39.

The marker is on counter [37](@count). That counter shows its number, and every counter up to it is filled in.

- Press + on the Marker stepper until the marker reaches 40, and watch the fourth row. At 40 it is full, and the next press puts the marker on the first counter of the fifth row.
- Press − to count back from 41 to 39, and watch the fourth row lose its last counter.
- Try the same between 69 and 70, and between 99 and 100.
