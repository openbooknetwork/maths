A hundred counters are easier to count when you put them in rows of ten. Every row you finish adds ten more, so the rows end on 10, 20, 30 and so on up to 100. A number that ends a row like this is called a ten. The next row starts just after it, so the row after 30 starts at 31.

That means you do not have to count from 1 to find a counter's number. You start from the ten at the end of the row above it, and you count on along its own row.

Take the fourth counter in the sixth row. There are five full rows above it, and five rows of ten counters make 50. So you start from 50 and count on four counters: 51, 52, 53, 54. That counter is 54.

There are ten rows of ten counters in the tray, with a small gap after the fifth counter of each row. The ten that ends each row is written at its right end.

Pick a counter and work out its number, then tap it to see if you were right. Each counter keeps its number once you tap it.
