Apples lie on a market stall. Tap each apple once, and each tap puts the next number on it. An apple keeps its number when you tap it again.

The last number said is [0](@count). How many shows a question mark until every apple has its number. Then the last number says how many apples lie on the stall.

This story counts objects one by one, counts on and back, and counts all the way to 100.
