A closed box has a number on its front, which tells you how many counters are inside. Your goal is to put counters beside it until the box and the counters together make the number the goal asks for. You do not need to open the box and count what is inside, because its number already tells you how many there are. So you start from that number and count on, saying the next number for each counter you put beside the box. You stop when you say the goal's number.

Say the box holds 68 and the goal is 72. You start from 68, and for each counter you put beside the box you say the next number: 69, 70, 71, 72. Your count passes the ten 70 on the way and carries on. You said four numbers, so four counters go beside the box.

- Drag counters from the pile to the dashed space, or tap the pile.
- Tap a counter in the dashed space to put it back on the pile.
- Press Check when you think the box and the counters make the number the goal asks for. If they do not, a note tells you whether to add counters or take some away.
