A bus driver needs to know how many passengers are on the bus. Counting everyone again at each stop would take too long. So the driver keeps count by counting on and counting back. Each person who gets on makes one more passenger, so the driver says the next number. Each person who gets off makes one fewer, so the driver says the number before.

Say there are three passengers on the bus. Two people get on, so the driver counts on from 3 and says 4, 5. Then one passenger gets off, so the driver counts back from 5 to 4. There are now four passengers on the bus.

The bus has three passengers, and five people are waiting at the stop.

- Tap someone at the stop to let them on. They take the next free seat.
- Tap a passenger to let them off.
- Before each tap, say how many passengers there will be after it. Then see if the Passengers readout agrees. It shows [3](@count) now.
