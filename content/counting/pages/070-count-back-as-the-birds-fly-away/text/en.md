Counting back means saying the numbers that come before a number, one at a time. From 9 you count back 8, 7, 6 and so on. When one object leaves a group, there is one fewer, so how many are left is the number before. If you counted the group again, you would stop one number earlier. When the last object leaves, the group is empty, and you have counted back to 0. Zero is how many objects an empty group has.

Say four birds are sitting on a fence. One flies away, so you count back from 4 to 3, and three birds are left. Another flies away, and you count back to 2. After two more fly away, you have counted back to 1 and then to 0, so no birds are left on the fence.

There is a row of birds on the wire. Tap a bird and it flies away, leaving an empty place. Tap an empty place and its bird comes back. Count back each time a bird flies away. The Left readout now shows [9](@count).
