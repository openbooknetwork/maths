When you move objects around, you change where they are, but no object comes or goes. So when you count them again, each object still gets one number, and you end on the same number as before. It does not matter whether they are spread out or close together.

Say you lay four spoons in a long line and count them: 1, 2, 3, 4. Then you push them together into a heap. The heap looks smaller, but it holds the same four spoons. Count them again and you say 1, 2, 3, 4 once more, so there are still four spoons.

There are five counters in the tray. Drag a counter to move it, and tap a counter to give it the next number. A counter keeps its number when you drag it.

- Spread the counters far apart, then tap each one to count it.
- Press Clear, push the counters close together, and count them again.

However you place the counters, the "How many" readout shows 5 once every counter has its number.
