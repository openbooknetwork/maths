Here you count in twos, fives or tens, from 0 or back from the right end of the line, to land on a target number.
The line has numbers at its two ends and nowhere else, so you keep track by saying the count, one number for each
jump. The ends are multiples of the size of a jump, so every number you land on is a multiple too, and in fives the
target ends in 5 or 0.

Say you have to count back in fives from 45 and land on 15. Each jump back subtracts 5, so you say 40, 35, 30, 25,
20, 15, and you stop when you say 15. After 40 comes 35, because 5 less than a ten ends in 5. You said six numbers,
so you made six jumps. Count the jumps and not the marks, since one jump of 5 goes over five gaps.

- Press "Jump right" to count on, or "Jump left" to count back, and say a number with each press.
- Press the other button to take back a jump that went too far.
- Count all the way to the target before you press Check. A wrong Check sends the point back to the start, with a
  note that says whether you stopped short or went past.
