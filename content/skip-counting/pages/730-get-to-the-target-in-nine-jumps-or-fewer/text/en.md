Land on the target in nine jumps or fewer, with jumps of 10 and of 100. The start and the target end in the same
digit. Make the big jumps first, because one jump of 100 takes you as far as ten jumps of 10. Jump in hundreds as
long as the next jump does not take you past the target, then jump in tens the rest of the way. A jump of 10 keeps
the last digit, which is already the target's, so the tens can land exactly on it.

Say you go from 8 to 528. In hundreds you say 108, 208, 308, 408, 508. The next jump would land on 608, past 528, so
you stop at 508. In tens you say 518, 528. That is five jumps of 100 and two of 10, seven in all, where jumps of 10
alone would take 52. Counting back works the same way, hundreds first.

Only the ends of the line and every hundredth number from the left end have labels, and the numbers you land on stay
hidden, so say the count aloud.

- Pick 100 or 10 under "Size of a jump", then press "Jump right" or "Jump left".
- The other jump button takes back your last jump.
- Plan your jumps before you press Check. If you miss, the point goes back to the start and you can count again.
