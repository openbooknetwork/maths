This story has shown you how to count in jumps of the same size, forward and back.

- Counting in twos from 0 lands on the even numbers.
- Counting in tens from 0 lands on the numbers that end in 0, and a jump of 10 keeps the last digit.
- Counting in fives from 0 lands on the numbers that end in 5 or 0, the multiples of 5.
- Counting in hundreds reaches 1000 in ten jumps, and a jump of 100 keeps the last two digits.
- A bigger jump reaches a number in fewer jumps.

When objects come in groups of the same size, you make one jump for each group. For four groups of 5 counters you say
5, 10, 15, 20, so 4 groups of 5 make 20 counters.

Press "Jump right" four times and watch the two readouts. Groups counts the groups and Point counts the counters,
so they end on 4 and 20. Right now Groups reads [0](@groups) and Point reads [0](@point).
