When you count back in twos, you say every second number going down. Each jump back subtracts 2, so from 18 you say 16, 14, 12. If you take objects away two at a time, each number you say is how many are left.

Picture 18 counters in 9 groups of 2. Take one group away and 16 are left, because 2 less than 18 is 16. Group by group you say 16, 14, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 0. After 12 comes 10, which is 2 less than 12.

A count back also checks a count in twos. If 9 groups of 2 really make 18, then taking the groups away one at a time from 18 has to reach 0 on the ninth jump back. It does, so 9 groups of 2 make 18.

The point starts on 18, with 9 groups of 2 counters over the line.

- Press "Jump left" to take a group away, and say the number you land on. The group turns into a dashed outline.
- The Groups readout, [9](@groups) now, goes down by one at each jump while the number at the point goes down by 2.
- Press "Jump right" to take back a jump, and its group comes back.
