A teacher has 4 boxes of 10 pencils and 7 loose pencils. Each box is a group of 10, so you can count the boxes in
tens and say one number for ten pencils. Then you count on the loose pencils in ones from the last ten.

Count the boxes first: 10, 20, 30, 40. Then say the next number for each loose pencil: 41, 42, 43, 44, 45, 46, 47.
There are 47 pencils. You said 11 numbers, where counting every pencil means saying 47. Take care not to mix up
the 4 boxes with the 47 pencils.

If you count the loose pencils first and then count in tens from 7, you say 17, 27, 37, 47 and land on 47 too.

On the number line, each box is drawn over a jump of 10 and each loose pencil over a jump of 1. The number at the
point is how many pencils you have counted so far. Make the teacher's count.

- The "Size of a jump" buttons start on 10. Press "Jump right" four times and say each ten.
- Press the 1 button, then press "Jump right" seven times. The jumps you made before stay. Watch the Jumps readout
  end on 11 while the point ends on 47, the number of pencils.
- "Jump left" takes back the last jump, whatever its size.
