A bike has 2 wheels, so you can count the wheels of a row of bikes in twos, one jump for each bike. When some bikes are in the rack already, you count their wheels first and then count on in twos from there. You do not say the number you start from again, so the first number you say is 2 more than it.

In the morning 5 bikes are in the rack. You count their wheels 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, so there are 10 wheels. Then 3 more bikes come in. You start from 10 and say one number for each new bike: 12, 14, 16. After 10 comes 12, because the first new bike adds 2 wheels to 10. Now there are 8 bikes and 16 wheels.

The rack starts with 5 bikes, and the point is on 10, the number of their wheels.

- Press "Jump right" to bring in a bike. It stands in the next slot, and its two wheels are a group of 2.
- Watch the two readouts as the bikes come in. Groups counts the bikes and goes up by 1 at each jump, while Point counts the wheels and goes up by 2. They read [5](@groups) and [10](@point) now.
- Drag the start ring to fill the rack with another number of bikes, and count on from there.
