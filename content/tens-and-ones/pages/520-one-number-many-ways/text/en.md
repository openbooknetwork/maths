You can show a number with tens and ones in more than one way. Each time you break a ten, you have 1 ten fewer and 10 more ones, and the number stays the same.

Take 23. Its digits give 2 tens and 3 ones, so 23 splits into 20 and 3, and `20 + 3 = 23`. If you break one ten, you have 1 ten and 13 ones, so 23 also splits into 10 and 13, and `10 + 13 = 23`. If you break the last ten, you have 0 tens and 23 ones, and `0 + 23 = 23`. The 3 ones of the first way are too few to bundle, and the last way has no ten left to break, so these three are all the ways.

In this order, the tens go 2, 1, 0 and the ones go 3, 13, 23. Only the first way has fewer than 10 ones, and it is the one the digits of 23 give.

The "Ways found" list starts with 2 tens and 3 ones, the way the chart shows.

- Find every way to show 23. Tap the tens column to break a ten, and each new way joins the list, while the number above the chart stays 23.
- Press "Bundle ten ones" to go back up the list.
