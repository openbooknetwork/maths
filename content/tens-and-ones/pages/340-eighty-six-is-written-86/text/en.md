The name of a number from 20 to 99 says its tens first and then its ones. Eighty-six says eighty, which is 8 tens, and then six. If you write just what you hear, 80 and then 6, you get 806. That number has three digits, so it is more than 99 and far more than eighty-six.

Think of 8 tens and 6 ones. The 8 tens make 80, and 80 has a 0 in the ones place because it has no ones. Here there are 6 ones, so the 6 goes in the ones place, where the 0 was. That gives 86, eighty-six, with the tens digit 8 and the ones digit 6 side by side.

The chart holds 8 rods and 6 cubes, with the name "eighty-six" above it. Below the digits 8 and 6 are two cards, the 80 card for the rods and the 6 card for the cubes. Written next to each other, they would read 806, the way the name sounds.

- Tap the 6 card. It slides onto the 80 card over its 0, and the cards read 86, the same as the digits above them.
- Tap the cards again to slide the 6 back, and look at the 0 that was under it.
