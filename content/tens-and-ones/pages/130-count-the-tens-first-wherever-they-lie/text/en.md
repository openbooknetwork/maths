Blocks are not always set out with the rods first. Wherever the rods lie, you find them and count them in tens first, then count on the cubes in ones. Moving the blocks does not change how many there are, so you get the same number however they are set out.

Say 4 rods and 6 cubes are mixed up. You count the rods first, 10, 20, 30, 40, which is 4 tens. Then you count on the cubes from 40 in ones, 41 to 46. That makes 46, which is 4 tens and 6 ones. If you start with a cube instead, you say 1, and a rod after it takes you to 11. From there you keep switching between tens and ones, and the numbers get hard to keep track of. With the rods first, you switch from tens to ones just once.

- Press "Mix up the blocks", and the rods move in among the cubes.
- Find the rods and tap them first, then tap the cubes. The "Last number said" readout, now [0](@count), ends on 46 wherever the rods lie.
- Press "Mix up the blocks" again, so the numbers come off and the readout goes back to 0. This time tap a cube and then a rod, and see 1 and then 11 appear.
