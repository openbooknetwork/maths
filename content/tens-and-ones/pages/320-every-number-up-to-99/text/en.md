Ten ones can always be bundled into a ten, so a number needs at most 9 ones. Up to 99 it needs at most 9 tens too, because 10 tens are already 100. So each digit is one of 0 to 9, and with two digits you can write every number up to 99. A number from 10 to 99 is called a two-digit number. A number from 0 to 9 has no tens, so you write it with its ones digit alone.

Start at 2 tens and 8 ones, which is 28. To get to 98, you add tens until there are 9. The ones stay at 8, so only the tens digit changes. You can hear the 9 tens in "ninety-eight". Add 1 to the ones, and 9 tens and 9 ones make 99, the last two-digit number, because 100 needs three digits.

The Tens stepper adds or takes away rods, and the Ones stepper does the same with cubes.

- Step the tens up to 9 and watch the number. Only its tens digit changes, and the 8 stays. Then step the ones up to 9.
- Step the tens down to 0 to see a number with one digit.
- Now make 74 from where you are. Step the tens and the ones until each digit is the one you need, with no need to start again from an empty chart.
