Each round puts a number on the chart and asks you to show it with a given number of ones. Breaking a ten keeps the number the same and gives you ten more ones. So the ones you are asked for tell you how many tens to break.

At the start, the ones column holds as many cubes as the ones digit says. Say the number is 27 and you are asked for 17 ones. 27 is 2 tens and 7 ones, so the ones column starts with 7 cubes. 17 ones are those 7 and 10 more, and breaking a ten gives exactly 10 more ones. So you break one of the 2 tens. That leaves 1 ten and 17 ones, which is still 27.

The goal line names the number and the ones. The counts under the columns are hidden, so work it out from the ones digit, or count the cubes in the ones column by their rows of five.

- Tap the tens column to break a ten.
- If you break one too many, press "Bundle ten ones" to join ten cubes back into a rod.
- Press Check when you are done, and "Another one" for a new number.
