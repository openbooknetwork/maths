The numbers from 11 to 19 are called the teen numbers. Each of them is 1 ten and some ones, from 1 ten and 1 one for 11 up to 1 ten and 9 ones for 19. 10 has no ones and 20 is 2 tens, so neither is a teen number.

Sixteen is the number name of 16, the word you say when you read it aloud. From thirteen to nineteen, the name says the ones first and then "teen", which means ten. "Thir" in thirteen means three, and "fif" in fifteen means five. Eleven and twelve have names of their own that do not say their ones.

Take a rod and 6 cubes. The rod is 1 ten, so you start at 10 without counting its ten cubes. Then you count on, one number for each cube: 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16. So 1 ten and 6 ones make 16, and its name is sixteen. The 1 in 16 stands for the ten, and the 6 stands for the ones.

Each press on the Ones stepper adds a cube beside the rod or takes one away, and [16](@number) and [sixteen](@name) change with it. Watch the 1 at the front of the number. It stays the same, because the rod is always there. Step the ones down to 2 and 1 to see twelve and eleven.
