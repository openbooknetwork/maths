Fill the customer's order from the shelf, which has fewer packs than the order has tens. Ten single pencils are as many pencils as a pack, so ten pencils can take the place of each missing pack.

Say a customer asks for 84 pencils and the shelf has only 6 packs. 84 is 8 tens and 4 ones, so 2 packs are missing. The pencils for the order go in the tray on the counter. Put all 6 packs in the tray first. They make 60, and 84 is 24 more than 60, so 24 pencils go in with them. The 24 pencils are the 4 ones of 84 and ten pencils for each of the 2 missing packs. 6 packs and 24 pencils make 84.

The shelf has too few single pencils to make the order on their own, so count on from what the packs make.

The counts under the tray's columns help you count what you put in.

- Drag a pack or a pencil onto the tray, or tap it on the shelf.
- Drag one off the tray to send it back.
- Press Check when the tray holds as many pencils as the order asks for, and "Another one" for a new order.
