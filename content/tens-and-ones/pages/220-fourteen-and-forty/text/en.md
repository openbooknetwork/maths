Fourteen and forty sound alike, but they are very different numbers. The end of each name tells them apart. "-teen" means 1 ten, so fourteen is 1 ten and 4 ones, which is 14. "-ty" means tens, so forty is 4 tens and 0 ones, which is 40. In 14 the 4 counts ones, and in 40 it counts tens.

A teen name says its ones first, as fourteen says four before "teen". A "-ty" name works the other way round. Forty-one says the tens first and the ones after, so it is 4 tens and 1 one, which is 41.

In blocks, 14 is 1 rod and 4 cubes, so its 4 is just 4 cubes. 40 is 4 rods and no cubes. Each rod is 10 cubes, so the 4 in 40 stands for 40 cubes. 41 is 4 rods and 1 cube.

The number above the blocks is [14](@number), and its name [fourteen](@name) is under it.

- Set the Tens stepper to 4 and the Ones stepper to 0. Now the 4 counts rods where it counted cubes, and the name turns into forty.
- Press + on the Ones stepper once to make forty-one.
- Now make fifteen, then fifty. Say each name aloud and listen for the "-teen" and the "-ty".
