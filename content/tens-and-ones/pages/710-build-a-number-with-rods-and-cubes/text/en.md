Your goal is to build the number the goal line names, with rods and cubes on the empty chart, and then press Check. The tens digit tells you how many rods you need, and the ones digit tells you how many cubes. If you read the number as tens and ones first, you do not have to count cubes all the way up to it.

Say the number is 58. Its tens digit is 5 and its ones digit is 8, so 58 is 5 tens and 8 ones. You put 5 rods in the tens column and 8 cubes in the ones column. To make sure, count the rods in tens up to 50, then count on the cubes, 51 to 58.

There are no digits under the columns this time, so count your rods and cubes yourself.

- A tap on the rod or the cube in "Blocks to use" puts one on the chart, in its column, and so does a drag.
- Drag a piece off the chart to take it away.
- If the ones column has 10 cubes or more, press "Bundle ten ones".
- Press Check when you think the blocks show the number. If they do not, a note tells you what to change.
