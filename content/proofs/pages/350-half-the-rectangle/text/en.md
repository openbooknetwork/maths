The rectangle is n wide and n + 1 = [5](@n-plus-1) tall, so it holds [20](@rectangle) squares. The two staircases are the same size, so each one is half the rectangle.

[1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = (4 × 5)/2 = 10](@formula-staircase)

Change n. The rectangle grows with the staircase, and half of it is still the sum.
