Pick a whole from 2 to 10 with the Whole stepper. The diagram splits [6](@whole) into [3](@first) and [3](@second).

Drag counters to split the whole in other ways. The list holds [1](@found) of the [7](@ways) splits.

Every number up to 10 splits into two parts in several ways. A whole has one more split than its own number, because the first part can be any number from 0 to the whole.
