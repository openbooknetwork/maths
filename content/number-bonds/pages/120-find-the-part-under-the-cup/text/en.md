When you know the whole of a number bond and one of its parts, you can work out the other part. The part you know is called the known part, and the part you have to find is the missing part. It is how many you add to the known part to get to the whole.

You find it by counting on from the known part to the whole. Each number you say is one more counter, so how many numbers you say is how many counters you added. Say the whole is 8 and the known part is 5. From 5, you say 6, 7, 8. That is three numbers, so the missing part is 3.

The top circle holds the whole, 10. The left circle holds the known part, 6, and the frame shows it as red counters. A cup covers the missing part in the right circle.

- Tap an empty cell to put a blue counter in it, and count on from 6 as you go. Tap a blue counter to take it out again.
- When you say 10, the frame is full. The Added readout shows [0](@added) now.
- Press "Lift the cup" to see the missing part, and "Put the cup back" to cover it again.
