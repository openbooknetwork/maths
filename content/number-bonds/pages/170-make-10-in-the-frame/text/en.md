Your task is to make 10 in a ten frame. The red counters in the "First part" tray are given, and you choose how many blue counters go with them, so that the two parts make 10 together. A ten frame holds 10, so the second part is the number of cells the red counters leave empty.

You find it by counting on from the first part to 10, because each number you say fills one more cell. Here is the idea with a whole of 7 in place of 10. If the first part is 5, you count on 6, 7. Two numbers take you to 7, so the second part is 2, and 5 and 2 make 7.

- Count the red counters, then count on from there to 10.
- Set your number on the "Second part" slider. That many blue counters appear in the "Second part" tray.
- Press Check. All the counters drop into the frame, the red ones first. If the frame is full and no blue counter is left out, you are right. If not, a note says whether to add counters or take some away.
- Move the slider to try again, and press "Another one" for a new first part.
