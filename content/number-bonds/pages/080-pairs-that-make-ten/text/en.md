Tap cells to fill the frame with any number of counters from 0 to 10. Each pair in the list gives the counters first and the empty cells second.

The list holds [1](@found) of the [11](@ways) pairs so far.

10 has eleven pairs, from 0 and 10 to 10 and 0. Each pair has its swap in the list, such as 3 and 7 with 7 and 3.
