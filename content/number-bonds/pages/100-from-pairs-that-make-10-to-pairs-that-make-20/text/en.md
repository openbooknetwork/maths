If you take a pair that makes 10 and add a full ten to one of its parts, you get a pair that makes 20. That part goes up by 10, the other part stays as it was, and the whole goes up from 10 to 20.

3 and 7 make 10, so 3 red counters and 7 blue ones fill one ten frame. Now fill a second ten frame with red counters. The red part is now a full ten and the 3 from before, and a full ten and 3 more is 13. The 7 blue counters have not moved. Both frames are full, and two full frames hold 20 counters, so 13 and 7 make 20.

The circles at the top show the whole and its two parts. The upper frame is the first one, with 3 red counters and 7 blue ones, and the second frame is empty.

- Press "Fill the second frame" to put a full ten of red counters in it. Watch the circles: the first part goes from 3 to 13 and the whole from 10 to 20, while the second part stays 7.
- Press "Empty the second frame" to take them out again.
