Picture a car park with 20 spaces, in two rows of ten. Each space has a car in it or is free, so the parked cars and the free spaces make 20 together. Many car parks have a sign at the entrance with the number of free spaces, so drivers know before they drive in whether there is room.

Say 14 cars are parked. 14 is a full ten and 4 more. 4 and 6 make 10, and when you add the full ten back to the 4, you get 14 and 6, which make 20. So 6 spaces are free. When one more car parks, there are 15 cars and 5 free spaces, because each car that parks fills one free space.

A lane runs between the two rows, and a car is waiting in it. The sign at the entrance shows how many spaces are free.

- Drag the waiting car into a free space, or tap a free space to park it there.
- Tap a parked car, or drag it into the lane, to make it drive away.

The Parked readout now shows [12](@first) and the "Free spaces" readout shows [8](@empty). However many cars come and go, the two make 20.
