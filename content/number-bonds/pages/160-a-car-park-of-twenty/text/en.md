This car park has 20 spaces in two rows of ten. Drag the waiting car into a free space. Drag a parked car into the lane between the rows, and it drives away. A tap on a free space or on a parked car does the same.

The number of parked cars is [12](@first), and the number of free spaces is [8](@empty).

The cars parked and the free spaces make 20. So a sign at the entrance can show the free spaces, and a driver knows at once whether there is room.
