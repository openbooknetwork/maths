Hold seven beads, some in your left hand and the rest in your right. When you pass a bead from one hand to the other, one hand has one more and the other has one fewer. No bead is lost and none is added, so you still have seven beads. The number in each hand changes, and the two numbers together still make 7.

Say your left hand holds 3 beads and your right hand holds 4. Pass one bead from your left hand to your right. The left hand now has 2 and the right hand has 5, because the bead that left one hand went into the other. 2 and 5 make 7, just as 3 and 4 did. Pass one more and you have 1 and 6, which make 7 as well.

The hands on the figure start with 3 beads in the left hand and 4 in the right. Drag a bead from one hand to the other, or tap it to send it across. Watch the "Left hand" and "Right hand" readouts, now [3](@first) and [4](@second). However you move the beads, the two numbers still make 7. Try putting all the beads in one hand too. Then the other hand holds none, and there are still 7.
