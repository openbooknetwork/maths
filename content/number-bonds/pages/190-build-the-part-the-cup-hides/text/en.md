Your task is to build the missing part of a number bond out of counters. You can see the whole and the known part, and a cup hides the missing part. You find the missing part by counting on from the known part to the whole, because each number you say is one more counter.

Suppose you can see a whole of 4 and a known part of 1. Counting on from 1, you say 2, 3, 4. You need one counter for each of those numbers, so the cup hides 3.

The top circle holds the whole. The left circle shows the known part as red counters, and the cup is on the right. The tray is empty, and the blue counters are in the pile.

- Count on, and put one blue counter in the tray for each number you say. Drag them, or tap a counter to move it between the pile and the tray.
- Press "Lift the cup". If the tray holds as many counters as the cup hides, you are right. If not, a note tells you what to change, and the cup goes back down when you change the tray.
- Press "Another one" for a new whole and a new known part.
