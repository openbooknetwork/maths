When you split a number, you break it into two parts. The number you start with is called the whole. Splitting only puts the things you count into two groups, so when you put the parts back together, you have the whole again. A whole and its two parts are called a number bond.

Split 7 counters into 3 and 4, then put the two groups back together, and you count 7 again. 7 splits in other ways too. The first part can be any number from 0 to 7, and the second part is the rest of the 7. Each first part gives one split, from 0 and 7, then 1 and 6, and so on up to 7 and 0. There are eight numbers from 0 to 7, so 7 has eight splits.

The figure has the whole in the circle at the top and the parts in the two circles under it. Every split you make is written in the list.

- Move a counter into the other part by dragging it there or tapping it.
- Find all eight splits of 7. The "Splits found" readout shows [1](@found) now.
- Then set the Whole stepper to 10 and find all 11 splits of 10.
