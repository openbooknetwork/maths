The circle at the top holds the whole, which is [7](@whole). The two circles under it are the parts. The first part has [3](@first) in it, and the second part has [4](@second).

Drag counters from one part to the other, or tap a counter to send it across. The two parts together make the whole, and a whole split into two parts in this way is a number bond. Each new split goes into the list.

Find every split of 7, from 0 and 7 to 7 and 0. Then pick another whole with the Whole stepper, and set it to 10 to find the eleven pairs that make 10. A whole has one more split than its own number, because the first part can be any number from 0 to the whole.
