The circle at the top holds the whole, which is [7](@whole). The two circles under it are the parts. The first part has [3](@first) in it, and the second part has [4](@second).

Drag counters from one part to the other, or tap a counter to send it across. The parts change, and the whole stays [7](@whole).

The two parts together make the whole. A whole split into two parts in this way is a number bond.
