The frames hold [3](@first) red counters and [7](@second) blue ones, and together they make [10](@whole).

Tap the button to fill the second frame with red. The red part grows by a full ten, and the blue part stays the same.

Adding a full ten to one part of a pair for 10 gives a pair for 20. 3 and 7 make 10, so 13 and 7 make 20.
