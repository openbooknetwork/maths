A ten frame has two rows of five cells, so it holds ten counters. Tap an empty cell to put a counter in it, and tap a counter to take it out.

The number of counters is [0](@first), and the number of empty cells is [10](@empty).

The empty cells show how many more counters make ten. Fill the top row, and the frame shows 5 counters and 5 empty cells.
