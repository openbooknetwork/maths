A fraction is a share of a whole. Cut the whole into equal parts and count the parts you take.

The bar is cut into [8](@bottom-a) equal parts and [3](@top-a) of them are shaded, so [3/8](@a) of the bar is shaded. The pie shows the same share of a round whole.

Tap a part of the bar or the pie to shade it, and tap it again to clear it. Drag a finger along the bar to shade several parts in one go. The fraction follows every tap.

Any [3](@top-a) of the [8](@bottom-a) parts make the same share, whichever ones you pick. Counting only works when the parts are equal in size.

Step the denominator under the picture to cut the whole into more parts or fewer.
