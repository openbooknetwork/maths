A fraction can be more than one whole. In [7/4](@a) the numerator is bigger than the denominator, and a fraction like that is called improper.

Step the numerator up. When a bar is full, the next part goes into a new bar.

Count the full bars, then the parts in the last one. That gives the mixed number [1 3/4](@mixed-a), which names the same amount as [7/4](@a):

[7/4 = 4/4 + 3/4 = 1 + 3/4 = 1 3/4](@formula-mixed-a)

Try eight quarters, or step the denominator down and see how many wholes the same numerator fills.
