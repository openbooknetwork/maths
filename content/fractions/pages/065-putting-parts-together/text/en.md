Once the parts are the same size, the shaded parts of both bars move into the bottom bar. [4/12](@common-a) and [3/12](@common-b) make [7/12](@total). Written out, the sum is:

[1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12](@formula-sum)

Try two fractions that make more than one whole, such as [3/4](@3/4) and [2/3](@2/3). The parts run on into a second bar.
