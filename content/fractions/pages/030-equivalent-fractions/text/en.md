Two fractions can name the same amount. The shaded share of the bar is [2/3](@a).

The slider cuts every part into [2](@pieces) equal pieces. The shaded share stays the same size, and now it counts [4/6](@equal). There are more pieces, and each one is smaller.

Multiplying the numerator and the denominator by the same number gives an equivalent fraction:

[2/3 = (2 × 2)/(3 × 2) = 4/6](@formula-equal)

Move the slider and watch the new cuts sweep in while the shaded share stays put. Then pick another fraction with the steppers and cut it again.
