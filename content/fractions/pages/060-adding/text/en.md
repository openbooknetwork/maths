Adding fractions means putting their parts together. The parts must be the same size first, so both wholes are cut into the same parts.

Both bars are now cut into [12](@common) parts, the largest parts that fit both. Now [1/3](@a) is [4/12](@common-a) and [1/4](@b) is [3/12](@common-b).

Step either fraction and watch both bars cut themselves again to suit the new pair.
