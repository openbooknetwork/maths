Which is more, [2/3](@a) or [3/4](@b)? Both bars are the same length, so you can compare the shaded shares by eye: [2/3](@a) [<](@sign) [3/4](@b).

The number line shows both fractions in one place. The one further to the right is the larger.

To compare them with numbers, cut both wholes into the same parts. Press "Use the same parts" and both bars are cut into [12](@common) parts. Now the shares are [8/12](@common-a) and [9/12](@common-b), and you can count:

[2/3 = 8/12 < 9/12 = 3/4](@formula-sign)

Change either fraction with the steppers and watch the sign between them. Can you find two different fractions with = between them?
