A fraction, a decimal and a percent can name the same number. [3/8](@a) is the decimal [0.375](@decimal-a) and the percent [37.5%](@percent-a).

The bar sits over a number line from 0 to 1, and the shaded share ends at the value.

To write the fraction as a decimal, divide the numerator by the denominator:

[3/8 = 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375](@formula-decimal-a)

A percent counts hundredths, so multiply by 100:

[3/8 = (3 × 100)/8% = 37.5%](@formula-percent-a)

Now try thirds or sevenths. The digits go on for ever, and a bar over them marks the digits that repeat.
