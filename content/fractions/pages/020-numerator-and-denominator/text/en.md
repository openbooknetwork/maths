A fraction is written as two numbers, one over the other. The fraction in the picture is [3/4](@a).

The bottom number is the denominator. It says how many equal parts the whole is cut into, here [4](@bottom-a).

The top number is the numerator. It counts the parts you take, here [3](@top-a).

Press + on the denominator and every part gets smaller, because the same whole is shared among more parts. Press + on the numerator and one more part is shaded. Hold a button to keep stepping, or tap a number and type a new one. You can also tap a part of the picture to shade up to it.

When the numerator equals the denominator, every part is shaded and the fraction is one whole.
