Many fractions name the same share. The one with the smallest numbers is its simplest form.

Take [12/18](@12/18). The numbers 12 and 18 share the factors 2 and 3. Press ÷ 2 under the picture. The numerator and the denominator are divided by 2, each pair of parts merges into one, and the fraction becomes [6/9](@6/9). Then press ÷ 3 to reach [2/3](@2/3).

When the only factor the numerator and the denominator share is 1, the fraction is in its simplest form. The simplest form of [12/18](@a) is [2/3](@simplest-a).

You can also get there in one step by dividing by the greatest common factor, [6](@gcd-a):

[12/18 = (12 ÷ 6)/(18 ÷ 6) = 2/3](@formula-simplest-a)

Set another fraction with the steppers and simplify it.
