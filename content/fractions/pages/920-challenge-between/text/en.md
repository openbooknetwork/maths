Find a fraction that is larger than [1/2](@above) and smaller than [2/3](@below). Two dashed lines mark these fractions on the bar, and a strip on the number line shows the gap between them.

Set your fraction with the steppers, type it, or tap the bar. Its shaded share must end between the dashed lines without touching either of them. When the gap is narrower than one part, smaller parts may fit.
