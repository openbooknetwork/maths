From here on the elements are numbers. The box around the circles is the universe U, which holds every element we are talking about. Here U = [{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8}](@universe).

The difference A ∖ B holds the elements of A that are outside B. Here A ∖ B = [{1, 2}](@A-minus-B).

The order matters. B ∖ A holds the elements of B that are outside A, and here B ∖ A = [{5, 6}](@B-minus-A).

The complement A′ holds every element of U outside A. Here A′ = [{5, 6, 7, 8}](@not-A). Drag [7](@7) into A and it leaves the complement.
