|A| is the number of elements in A. Here |A| = [5](@count-A) and |B| = [4](@count-B).

Adding the two counts takes the elements in the overlap twice, once for each set. Subtract the overlap once and the count is right:

[|A ∪ B| = |A| + |B| − |A ∩ B| = 5 + 4 − 2 = 7](@formula-count-union)

Move numbers in and out of the overlap and watch each step of the formula follow.
