Two sets can share elements. A fruit where the circles overlap belongs to both sets.

The intersection A ∩ B holds the elements that are in A and in B. Here A ∩ B = [{🍒}](@intersection).

The union A ∪ B holds the elements that are in A, in B, or in both. Here A ∪ B = [{🍎, 🍐, 🍒, 🍋, 🍇}](@union). The [🍒](@cherry) is in both sets and the union still lists it once.

Tap a value under the diagram to see its region. Then drag every fruit out of the overlap. The intersection becomes the empty set, written ∅.
