Cut a cube along some of its edges and lay it flat. The flat shape is a net of the cube.

Move the fold slider down to open the cube and up to close it. The net is made of six squares, each with an area of [4](@face), and that is why the surface is six times one face:

[S = 6a² = 6 × 2² = 24](@formula-surface)

Folding changes no face, so the net and the cube have the same surface area. The net holds no space at all, and the cube has all its volume.
