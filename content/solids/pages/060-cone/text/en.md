A cone is to a cylinder what a pyramid is to a prism. Its base is a circle, and its sides lean in to one point. The same third applies.

[V = ⅓ πr² h = ⅓ π × 2² × 4 ≈ 16.76](@formula-volume)

The straight line from the edge of the base up to the point is the slant. It is the long side of a right triangle with r and h, and here it is [4.47](@slant) long.
