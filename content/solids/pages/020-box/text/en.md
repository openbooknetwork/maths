Give the three directions their own lengths and the cube becomes a box. Counting small cubes works the same way. A row holds a cubes, a layer holds b rows, and the box holds c layers.

[V = abc = 4 × 3 × 2 = 24](@formula-volume)

The faces come in three pairs. Each pair is a rectangle and the one opposite it:

[S = 2(ab + bc + ca) = 2(12 + 6 + 8) = 52](@formula-surface)

Set all three sliders to the same value and the box is a cube again.
