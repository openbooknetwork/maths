Every point on a sphere is the same distance r from its centre. One number sizes the whole solid.

[V = 4/3πr³ = 4/3π × 2.5³ ≈ 65.45](@formula-volume)

Its surface is as large as four circles of the same radius:

[S = 4πr² = 4π × 2.5² ≈ 78.54](@formula-surface)

A sphere fits inside the cylinder that just holds it, with radius r and height 2r, and fills two thirds of it.
