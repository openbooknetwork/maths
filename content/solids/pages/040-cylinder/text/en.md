Give a prism's base more and more sides and it comes closer to a circle. A cylinder is that limit. It is a circle pushed straight up.

The rule is still base times height, and the base is a circle of area πr²:

[V = πr² h = π × 2² × 4 ≈ 50.27](@formula-volume)

The surface is two circles and a wall. Unroll the wall and it is a rectangle, as wide as the circle is round and as tall as the cylinder:

[S = 2πr² + 2πr h = 2π × 2² + 2π × 2 × 4 ≈ 75.4](@formula-surface)
