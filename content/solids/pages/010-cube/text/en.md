A flat shape has an area. A solid takes up space, and the amount of space is its volume.

A cube has six square faces and twelve edges, all of length a. Fill it with small cubes of edge 1. A row holds a of them, a layer holds a rows, and the cube holds a layers.

[V = a³ = 3³ = 27](@formula-volume)

Its surface is six squares:

[S = 6a² = 6 × 3² = 54](@formula-surface)

Make the edge twice as long. The surface grows 4 times and the volume grows 8 times. Drag the figure to turn it.
