A pyramid stands on a base like a prism, but its sides lean in to meet at one point.

It holds exactly one third of the prism with the same base and height. Three such pyramids of sand fill the prism. Here the base has an area of [9](@base).

[V = ⅓ Bh = ⅓ × 9 × 4 = 12](@formula-volume)

The third does not depend on the base. Change n and it holds for every base.
