A box is a rectangle pushed straight up. Push any flat shape straight up and the result is a prism.

Every slice of a prism, cut level with its base, is a copy of the base. So the volume is the area of the base times the height. Here the base has an area of [2.71](@base).

[V = Bh = 2.71 × 4 ≈ 10.83](@formula-volume)

Change n to give the base more sides. With 4 sides and the right height the prism is a cube. The rule V = Bh stays the same for every base.
