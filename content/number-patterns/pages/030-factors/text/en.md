A factor of a number divides it with nothing left over. Tap a number to light up its factors. The factors of [12](@n) are [1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12](@factors), which makes [6](@count-factors) factors.

Factors come in pairs that multiply to the number. Each pair is a way to set out that many dots in a rectangle:

[12 = 1 × 12 = 2 × 6 = 3 × 4](@formula-pairs)

So [12](@n) dots make [3](@rectangles) rectangles. Now tap 7, 13 or 29. A prime number has only two factors, 1 and itself, and its dots make a single row.

Tap 16 or 36. One of the rectangles is a square, and the number has an odd count of factors.
