The hundred square holds the numbers from 1 to 100 in rows of ten. Tap a number and its multiples light up one after another, counting up from it in steps of that number.

A multiple of 3 is 3 times a whole number: 3, 6, 9, 12 and so on. Up to 100 there are [33](@count-multiples) multiples of [3](@n), and the largest is [99](@last-multiple).

Tap other numbers and look at the pattern each one makes. The multiples of 5 fill two columns. The multiples of 9 run down a slanting line, and so do the multiples of 11.

Small numbers have many multiples and large ones have few. Tap 1, and every number lights up, because every number is a multiple of 1. Tap 51, and only 51 lights up, because its next multiple is 102.
