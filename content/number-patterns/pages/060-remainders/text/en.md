Divide a number by n, and something may be left over. That part is the remainder. Dividing [17](@x) by [3](@n) leaves [2](@remainder):

[17 = 5 × 3 + 2](@formula-division)

Every number on the grid takes the colour of its remainder. Dividing by n leaves a remainder from 0 to n − 1, and the colours repeat in that order all the way down. Tap a number to see how it divides, and move the first slider to divide by another number.

The clock has one place for each remainder. Its hand counts round from 0, a whole turn for every n, and stops at the remainder. Counting that starts again at 0 after each turn is called clock arithmetic. For example, a 12-hour clock shows 5 when it is 17 hours after midnight.

Set the columns to the same number as n. Each remainder then fills a column of its own.
