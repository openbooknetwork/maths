Now the angle runs along a line. The graph puts the angle across and the height of P up. As P turns, its height is drawn as a curve.

Drag the θ slider from 0° to 360°, or drag P round. P goes round once while the curve is drawn. The dotted line joins P to the end of the curve, and the two coloured bars have the same length.

This curve is the sine wave. P is at [120°](@angle) and its height is [0.87](@sin). The wave climbs from 0 to 1 at 90° and falls back to 0 at 180°. It goes on down to −1 at 270° and climbs back to 0 at 360°.

After a whole turn P is back where it started, so the wave repeats.
