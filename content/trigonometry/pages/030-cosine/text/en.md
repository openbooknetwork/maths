The reach across can be drawn the same way. Its curve is the cosine wave. It starts at 1, because at 0° the point is all the way to the right.

Here θ is [30°](@angle), the height is [0.5](@sin) and the reach across is [0.87](@cos). The two bars on the graph are the two legs of the triangle on the circle.

The cosine wave is the sine wave a quarter turn early. The cosine reaches its top at 0°, and the sine reaches its top a quarter turn later, at 90°.

Drag P round and compare the two. When one wave is at its top or bottom, the other crosses zero. That happens when P is on an axis and one leg of the triangle has shrunk to nothing.
