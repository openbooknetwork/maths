A number line does not have to start at 0. It can show just a piece of the count. The first mark of the line is called its left end, and the last mark is its right end.

Take the line from 0 to 100 that counts in tens. Between the marks for 40 and 50 there is one gap, and the nine numbers from 41 to 49 belong in it. If you draw that gap again, much longer, there is room for a mark for each of them. This new line starts at 40, so 40 is its left end, and it stops at 50, so 50 is its right end. Its marks go up in ones, 40, 41 and on to 50, so it shows a piece of the count that starts past zero.
