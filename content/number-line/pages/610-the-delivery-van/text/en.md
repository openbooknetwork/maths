A van brings parcels to a street where the houses are numbered in order along one side, from 1 to 60. Only houses 1, 20, 50 and 60 have a number on the door. The houses are like the marks on a number line, and the door numbers are like its labels. So the driver finds a house the way you find a number on a line. They go to the nearest door with a number and count the houses from there.

The first parcel is for house 46. The nearest numbered door is 50, because 46 comes only four houses before it. So the driver stops at house 50 and counts four houses back: 49, 48, 47, 46.

On the figure, the van starts at house 1, and the parcel is for house [46](@find).

- Drag the van along the road. While you drag it, a lens above the van shows the nearby houses close up.
- Stop at the house you counted to and press Show. A flag appears over the house the parcel is for, and the van shows the number of the house it is at.
- Press "Next number" for the parcels for 22 and then 57. Find the nearest numbered door for each one first.
