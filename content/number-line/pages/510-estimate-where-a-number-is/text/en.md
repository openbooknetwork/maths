An unmarked number line has a label at each end and nothing between them. Every number between the ends still has its own place, where its mark would be if the line had all its marks, with equal gaps. To estimate where a number is, you judge that place by eye, from how far the number is from each end. Your estimate only has to be close.

Take a line from 0 to 10. If it had all its marks, 8 would be eight gaps from 0 and only two gaps from 10. So 8 is much nearer 10, and its place is a short way from 10. The number 4 is harder, because it is far from both ends. It is four gaps from 0 and six from 10. That is only a little nearer 0, so its place is just left of the middle.

The point starts on 0, and the Find readout shows [8](@find).

- Drag the point to where you think the number is. It stops wherever you let go, and shows no number.
- Press Show to put a flag at the number's place. The closer your point is to the flag, the better your estimate.
- Press "Next number" for 2, and again for 4.
