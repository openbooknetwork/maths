Look at the edge of a closed book of 100 pages. You cannot see the page numbers, but the pages are all there, in order from the front to the back. The front cover is 0, and page 100 is at the back. So the edge is like an unmarked number line, and you can find a page on it the way you find a number on that line, from the ends and the middle. That is much quicker than counting the pages one by one.

Say you want to put a bookmark at page 75. Page 50 is in the middle of the edge. Page 75 is as far from page 50 as from page 100, so the bookmark goes halfway between the middle and the back.

The figure draws the edge of the book, and the bookmark starts at the front cover. The page to find is now [75](@find). There is no Middle button here, so you judge the middle by eye.

- Slide the bookmark to where you think the page is.
- Press "Open the book". A flag appears where the page really is, so you can see how close the bookmark came.
- Then use "Next number" for page 25 and page 90.
