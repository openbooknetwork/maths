A number that is not a ten is between two tens. The ten before it is the first ten you reach when you count back, and the ten after it is the first ten you reach when you count on. On a number line, the ten before is to its left and the ten after is to its right.

Take 46. Counting back, you say 45, 44, 43, 42, 41 and then 40, so 40 is the ten before 46. Counting on, you say 47, 48, 49, 50, so 50 is the ten after it. The number 30 is a ten that comes before 46 too, but you reach 40 first, so the ten just before 46 is 40.

The line on the figure goes from 30 to 60 in ones, with labels at the tens. A bracket over the line reaches from the ten before the point to the ten after it.

- Put the point on 45. The bracket still reaches from 40 to 50, so 40 is the ten before 45 and 30 is not.
- Put the point on 53 and press "Jump left" three times. On 50 the bracket disappears and the "Ten before" and "Ten after" readouts show a dash, because 50 is a ten.
- Press "Jump left" once more. On 49 the bracket reaches from 40 to 50.
