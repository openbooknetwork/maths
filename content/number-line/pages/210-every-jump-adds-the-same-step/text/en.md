Each jump to the right adds the same number, called the step of the line. Two labels next to each other are one jump apart, so how far you count on from one to the other is the step. A line with the step 10 counts in tens, and lines with the step 5 or 2 count in fives or twos.

The step is a number, and a gap is a piece of the line. If you change the step, the labels change, but every gap keeps its length.

Take a line with 10 gaps, labelled 0, 10, 20 and so on up to 100, the tens you know from counting to 100. Counting on ten from 20 gets you to the next label, 30, so the step is 10. Three jumps from 0 add 10 each time and land on 10, 20 and 30. Now label the same marks in fives, up to 50. The same three jumps now add 5 each, so they land on 5, 10 and 15.

The point starts on 0.

- Press "Jump right" three times.
- Press 5 next to Step. The point, its arcs and the Jumps readout stay as they were, but the Point readout goes from 30 to 15.
- Press 2, then 1. The point is still three jumps from 0, so it is on 6 and then on 3.
