Your goal is to put the point close to a number on an unmarked number line. Find the middle first, and decide whether the number is before or after it. Then judge how far the number is from the middle and from the nearer end. Your point counts if it is no more than a tenth of the line away from the number, on either side. A tenth of the line is one of ten equal pieces of it.

Say the line runs from 0 to 20, and your number is 16. The middle is 10, and 16 comes after it. Halfway between 10 and 20 is 15, and 16 is just past that. Counting in twos from 0 reaches 20 in ten counts, so a tenth of this line is 2, and a point anywhere from 14 to 18 counts.

The goal line gives your number, and the point starts at the left end.

- Judge where the middle is, then press Middle to check.
- Drag the point to where you judge the number is, and press Check.
- If you miss, a note tells you which way to move the point. When you are close enough, a tick appears and a flag shows where the number is.
- Press "Another one" for a new line.
