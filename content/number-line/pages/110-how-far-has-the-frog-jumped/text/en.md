A frog is sitting on the bank of a pond, and a row of stepping stones crosses the water. The stones are numbered 1 to 10, in the order you count. The frog jumps from the bank onto the first stone, and then from each stone to the next one. So the number on a stone tells you how many jumps the frog needs to get there from the bank.

Say the frog is on stone 7. Its first jump took it to stone 1, and each jump after that took it one stone further. So counting its jumps is the same as counting the stones it has landed on, from 1 to 7. On the way back, each jump takes the frog back one stone, and the last one takes it from stone 1 to the bank, so the way back is 7 jumps too.

The frog starts on the bank, and the Jumps readout shows [0](@jumps). An arc over the water shows each jump.

- Press "Jump right" four times, and read the number on the frog's stone.
- Keep jumping until the frog is on stone 7, and compare the stone's number with the Jumps readout.
- Press "Jump left" until the frog is back on the bank.
