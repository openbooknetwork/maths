On an unmarked number line from 0 to 100, counting along from 0 by eye goes wrong. You tend to leave too much room between the first few numbers, so you run out of room and squeeze the large numbers together near 100. It works better to mark the middle first. The middle splits the line into two halves, so you know which half a number is in. Then you judge how far it is from the middle and from the nearer end.

Each half has a middle of its own. Halfway between 0 and 50 is 25, and halfway between 50 and 100 is 75, since 75 is 25 past 50 and 25 short of 100.

Take 78. It is greater than 50, so it is in the right half. It is only 3 more than 75, so its place is a little to the right of where 75 goes.

The point starts at the left end, and you are looking for [60](@find).

- Drag the point to where you think the middle is, then press Middle to check. A long mark with the label 50 appears, and you see how close your point came to it.
- Drag the point to where you think the number is, press Show, and compare it with the flag.
- "Next number" then gives 44, and after that 78.
