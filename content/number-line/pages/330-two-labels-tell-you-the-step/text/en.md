Two labels on marks next to each other are one jump apart, so they tell you the step of the line. The step then gives you every missing label. You count on in steps from the label on the right until you reach the right end, and back in steps from the label on the left until you reach the left end.

Take a line with seven marks where only the second and third marks are labelled, 20 and 25. You count on 5 from 20 to get to 25, so the step is 5. Counting on in fives from 25 gives 30, 35, 40 and 45 for the four marks to its right, so the right end is 45. One step back from 20 gives the left end, 15. When you count in ones, 26 comes just after 25, but the mark after 25 is for 30, so 26 has no mark on this line.

On the figure, five marks have empty label boxes. One of the six label cards under the line fits no mark, like 26 in the example.

- Drag each card onto the mark where its number belongs. A card on the wrong mark slides back to the row.
- Watch the Placed readout. It counts the cards on their own marks.
- Press "New line" for another line.
