Two labels on marks next to each other are one jump apart, so they tell you the step of the line. The step then gives you every missing label. You count on in steps from the right label up to the right end, and back in steps from the left label down to the left end.

Take a line with seven marks where only the second and third marks are labelled, 10 and 15. You count on 5 from 10 to get to 15, so the step is 5. Counting on in fives from 15 gives 20, 25, 30 and 35 for the four marks to its right, so the right end is 35. One step back from 10 is the left end, 5. In ones, 16 comes just after 15, but the mark after 15 is for 20, so 16 has no mark on this line.

On the figure, five marks have empty label boxes. One of the six label cards under the line fits no mark, like 16 in the example.

- Drag each card onto the mark where its number belongs. A card on the wrong mark slides back to the row.
- The readout counts the cards on their own marks.
- Press "New line" for another line.
