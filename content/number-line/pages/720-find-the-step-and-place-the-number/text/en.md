Your goal is to put the point on a number, on a line where only two marks next to each other have labels. One jump takes you from one label to the other, so the two labels give you the step. Start at the label nearer your number and move from it one mark at a time. Each mark to the right adds the step, and each mark to the left takes it away.

Take a line with the labels 80 and 82. Counting on from 80, you say 81 and then 82, so the step is 2. To find 88, you start at 82 and count on in twos: 84, 86, 88. So 88 is three marks to the right of 82. If you counted in ones, you would say six numbers, 83 to 88, and land on 94.

The point starts on one of the labelled marks, and the goal line tells you which number to find.

- Drag the point onto the mark you counted to, and press Check. If it is on the wrong mark, a note says which way to move it.
- When you get it right, every mark shows its label, so you see the whole count the step makes.
