Every jump to the right adds one, so every jump should be the same length. That is why the gaps have to be equal. That way, the place of a number shows how far along the count it is.

Take a line from 0 to 10 where the mark for 4 is too far to the right. The jump from 3 to 4 is long and the jump from 4 to 5 is short, but both still add one. So 4 looks closer to 5 than to 3, while in the count it is one after 3 and one before 5. To fix the line, you move the mark for 4 to the left until its two gaps are as long as the others.

On this line the mark for 4 and one other mark are out of place. The words under the line read [Some gaps are longer than others.](@gaps)

- Press "Jump right" until the point is on 10. A mark with an arc bigger than most on one side and smaller than most on the other is out of place.
- Drag each of those marks until its two arcs look the same.

Once both marks are nearly in place, they move onto their places and the words change.
