Every jump to the right adds one, whichever gap it crosses. For the place of a number to show how far along the count it is, every jump has to cover the same length of the line. That is why the gaps on a number line have to be equal.

Take a line from 0 to 10 where the mark for 4 is too far to the right. The jump from 3 to 4 is long and the jump from 4 to 5 is short, but both still add one. So 4 looks closer to 5 than to 3, while in the count it is one after 3 and one before 5. To fix the line, you move the mark for 4 to the left until its two gaps are as long as the others.

The figure has that line, and one more mark on it is out of place. The words under the line read [Some gaps are longer than others.](@gaps)

- Press "Jump right" until the point is on 10, and compare the arcs. A mark is out of place when the arc on one side of it is longer than most, and the arc on its other side is shorter.
- Drag each of those marks until the arcs on its two sides look the same length.

Once both marks are nearly in place, they move onto their places and the words change.
