On a line from 0 to 100 that counts in ones, often only 0 and the tens have labels. Those labels are on marks drawn longer than the others, called long marks, and the marks between them are short marks with no label. The step is 1, so the nine short marks between 30 and 40 are 31 to 39.

To find a number that is not a ten, find the two long marks it is between. Count on from the long mark on its left, or back from the one on its right when that one is nearer.

Take 37. It comes between 30 and 40, so its short mark is between their long marks. From 30 you count on 31, 32 and so on to 37, which is seven jumps. From 40 you count back 39, 38, 37, which is only three jumps, so 40 is the nearer long mark. Either way, the labels save you counting 37 jumps from 0.

The point starts on the long mark for 30.

- Press "Jump right" seven times, and the Point readout shows 37.
- Press "Jump right" three more times. The point reaches 40, so 37 is three jumps before it, and ten jumps take you from one long mark to the next.
- Drag the point onto the long mark for 80, then press "Jump left" once. The point is on 79, the number before 80.
