On a line from 0 to 100 that counts in ones, often only 0 and the tens have labels. Their marks are longer and are called long marks. The marks between have no label and are called short marks. The step is 1, so the nine short marks between 30 and 40 are 31 to 39.

To find a number that is not a ten, find the two long marks it is between. Count on from the left one, or back from the right one if it is nearer.

Take 37. It comes between 30 and 40, so its short mark is between their long marks. From 30 you count on 31, 32 and so on to 37, which is seven jumps. The label 30 saves you counting 37 jumps from 0. For 79 you start at 80 and jump back once, because 79 is one before 80.

The point starts on the long mark for 30. The marks are close together, so a close-up of them opens above the point while it moves.

- Press "Jump right" seven times.
- "Jump right" three more times. Ten jumps take you from one long mark to the next.
- Drag the point onto the long mark for 80, then press "Jump left" once.
