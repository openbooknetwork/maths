Your goal is to take rods and cubes away until the chart shows the target number. Taking cubes away can only leave fewer cubes in the ones column. So when the target has more ones than the start has extra ones, break a ten first to get at the ones inside it. Then take away cubes until the ones column has the target's ones, and rods until the tens column has its tens.

Say you start from 34 and have to leave 18. 18 has 8 ones and 34 has only 4 extra ones, so you break a ten, and 34 is 2 tens and 14 ones. You take away 6 cubes, since `14 − 6 = 8`, and 1 rod, since `2 − 1 = 1`. 1 ten and 8 ones make 18, and you took away 16, so `34 − 16 = 18`.

The goal line names the start and the target. The counts under the columns are hidden, so you count the blocks yourself.

- Drag a rod or a cube off the chart, and tap the tens column to break a rod.
- Tap the rod or the cube on the "Taken away" pile to put one back.
- Press Check when the chart shows the target with 9 cubes or fewer.
