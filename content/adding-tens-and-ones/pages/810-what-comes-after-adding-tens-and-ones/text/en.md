This story has shown you how to add and subtract two-digit numbers by tens and ones.

- Adding ones keeps a number's tens while the ones add to 9 or less. When they make 10 or more, the number reaches or crosses the ten after.
- Ten more and ten less make the tens digit go up or down by 1, and the ones digit stays the same.
- To add, you add the tens to the tens and the ones to the ones.
- Ten ones of an addition make a new ten, which you count with the other tens.
- To subtract, you subtract tens from tens and ones from ones, and break a ten when the extra ones are fewer than the ones to take away.
- You can find a difference by counting on through the ten after.

In `45 + 37`, the tens give `40 + 30 = 70` and the ones give `5 + 7 = 12`. The 12 ones are 1 new ten and 2 ones, so `45 + 37 = 82`. The ones column keeps all 12 cubes.
