You can also find a sum on a number line. Start at the first number and split only the second number into its tens and its ones. Then add the tens with jumps of 10, and the ones with jumps of 1.

Take `31 + 26` again. 26 is 2 tens and 6 ones, so you add 20 and then 6. Two jumps of 10 from 31 land on 41 and then 51, so `31 + 20 = 51`. Six jumps of 1 from 51 land on 52, 53, 54, 55, 56 and 57, so `51 + 6 = 57`. That is the sum the blocks gave in "Add tens to tens and ones to ones".

The point is on [31](@point).

- The "Size of a jump" buttons start on 10. Press "Jump right" twice.
- Press the 1 button, then press "Jump right" six times.
- "Jump left" undoes the last jump, whether it was a 10 or a 1.

A tag over each run of jumps says what the run adds: +20 over the jumps of 10 and +6 over the jumps of 1, the two parts of 26. If you jump in ones first, the tags come in the other order and you still land on 57.
