A bakery packs its biscuits in packets of ten. A full packet is a ten and a biscuit outside the packets is a one, so the packets and the other biscuits show how many biscuits there are as tens and ones.

The bakery has 6 packets and 8 biscuits outside them, 68 biscuits in all. A batch of 5 comes out of the oven. The 8 and the 5 make 13, so 10 of them fill a new packet and 3 are left outside. Now there are 7 packets and 3 biscuits, so `68 + 5 = 73`. The new packet makes the tens digit go up by 1, from 6 to 7, and the ones digit is 3, for the 3 biscuits left outside.

In this story you add ones to a two-digit number, then tens, then another two-digit number, and last you subtract.

The packets are under "Packets" and the other biscuits under "Biscuits". The bakery's own [68](@first) are plain, and the 5 on the baking tray have a chocolate chip.

- Tap a biscuit on the baking tray, or drag it under "Biscuits". Drag a chocolate chip biscuit back to the baking tray to undo it.
- Once all 5 have joined, press "Fill a packet". Watch "Packets" go from 6 to 7 and "Biscuits" from 13 to 3. "Biscuits in all" stays 73, because the biscuits only move into a packet.
