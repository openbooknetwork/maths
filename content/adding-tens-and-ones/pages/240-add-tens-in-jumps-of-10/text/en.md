On a number line, a jump of 10 to the right adds 10, so it lands on ten more. A jump of 10 to the left subtracts 10
and lands on ten less. To add 30 you make three jumps of 10. Each jump adds 1 ten and no ones, so
every number you land on has the same ones digit.

Take `56 + 30`. Three jumps of 10 from 56 land on 66, 76 and 86, so `56 + 30 = 86`. The jumps add the tens one at
a time, and you can also add them at once. 56 is 5 tens and 6 ones, and 5 tens and 3 tens make 8 tens, so the
sum is 8 tens and 6 ones, 86.

The line has a mark every 10, from 46 to 96. The number sentence under it reads [56 + 0 = 56](@written).

- Press "Jump right" three times. The point goes to 66, 76 and 86, and each of them ends in 6.
- Press "Jump left" until the point passes 56, and the number sentence turns into a subtraction.
- Drag the start ring to another mark and add tens from there.
