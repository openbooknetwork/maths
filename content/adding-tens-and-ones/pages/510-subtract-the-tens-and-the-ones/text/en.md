To subtract a two-digit number, split it into its tens and its ones. Subtract the tens from the tens and the ones from the ones, then put together what is left. It works like adding place by place, with minus in place of plus.

Take `68 − 25`. 68 has 6 tens and 8 ones, and you subtract 2 tens and 5 ones. Subtracting 25 is the same as subtracting 20 and then 5, so you can work out each place on its own, in either order. 2 tens from 6 tens leave 4 tens, so `60 − 20 = 40`. 5 ones from 8 ones leave 3 ones, so `8 − 5 = 3`. 4 tens and 3 ones are left, so `68 − 25 = 43`.

The chart shows [68](@first) as 6 rods and 8 cubes. Drag a piece off the chart, or tap its column, and it goes to the "Taken away" pile. Tap the rod or the cube on the pile to put one back.

Take away 2 rods and 5 cubes. The subtraction is written under the chart, and each place gets a line below it once you take a piece from its column. The three lines end on the number sentences of the example.
