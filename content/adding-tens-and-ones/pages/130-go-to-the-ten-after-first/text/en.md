Adding ones crosses a ten when one of 10, 20, 30 and so on lies between the number you start from and the answer, as 40 lies between 38 and 43 in `38 + 5 = 43`. You can then add in two steps by making ten. You split the ones you add into two parts. The first part takes the number up to the ten after it, and then you add the rest to that ten.

Take `38 + 5`. 8 needs 2 more to make ten, so you split 5 into 2 and 3. The first step is `38 + 2 = 40`, because 8 ones and 2 more make a full ten, and with the 3 tens that is 4 tens. The rest is 3, so the second step is `40 + 3 = 43`. You worked `8 + 5` the same way in "Adding and subtracting within 20", with `8 + 2 = 10` and then `10 + 3 = 13`. Here the 8, the 10 and the 13 each have 3 tens more.

The chart shows [38](@first) in red.

- Tap the blue cubes under "Blocks waiting to join", or drag them onto the chart.
- The first 2 cubes make a full ten with the 8 red ones, and the other 3 start after the gap.
- Once every cube is on, the two steps appear under [38 + 5](@written).
- Press "Bundle ten ones" to turn the full ten into a rod. The chart then shows 4 rods and 3 cubes, still 43.
