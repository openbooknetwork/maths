A sticker album holds 9 sheets of ten star stickers and 5 single stickers, 95 stickers. A sheet is a ten and a single sticker is a one. On the first day 42 stickers go on birthday cards, and on the second day 28 go on a poster. How many are left?

The first day takes 4 sheets and 2 stickers. 9 sheets take away 4 leave 5, and 5 stickers take away 2 leave 3, so `95 − 42 = 53`. The second day takes 2 sheets and 8 stickers. The 3 single stickers are fewer than the 8 to use, and the other stickers are on the sheets, so you cut one sheet into ten stickers, which breaks a ten. Now 53 is 4 sheets and 13 stickers. 13 take away 8 leave 5, and 4 sheets take away 2 leave 2, so `53 − 28 = 25`. 25 stickers are left.

- Drag 4 sheets and 2 stickers off the album into the "First day" envelope.
- On the second day, tap the Sheets column to cut a sheet. The counts under the columns go from 5 and 3 to 4 and 13, and "Stickers in the album" stays 53. Then drag 2 sheets and 8 stickers into the "Second day" envelope.

Tap a sheet or a sticker in the open envelope to put one back.
