Ten more than a number is that number plus 10, and ten less is that number minus 10. 10 is 1 ten and no ones,
so when you add it, the tens digit goes up by 1 and the ones digit stays the same. Ten less has 1 ten fewer, so its tens
digit goes down by 1.

Take `26 + 30`. 26 has 2 tens and 6 ones. The first ten you add makes 3 tens and 6 ones, so ten more than 26 is 36.
The other way round, 36 is ten more than 26. Each ten after that is ten more again, 46 and then
56. Or you can add the tens at once. 30 is 3 tens, and 2 tens and 3 tens make 5 tens, just as `2 + 3 = 5`. You add
no ones, so the 6 ones stay and `26 + 30 = 56`.

The red blocks show 26, and each blue rod with dots is a ten you add. "Ten more" shows [36](@ten-more) and "Ten less"
shows [16](@ten-less).

- Press + on the "Tens added" stepper once. The sum becomes the number "Ten more" showed.
- Add two more tens. The number sentence ends in 46 and then 56, and the 6 ones never change.
- Press − to take a ten off, and the sum becomes the number "Ten less" showed.
