When you add ones to a two-digit number, the ones you add join its ones. If the ones together come to 9 or fewer, they do not fill a ten, so the tens stay as they are and only the ones digit changes.

Take `23 + 5`. 23 is 2 tens and 3 ones, and `3 + 5 = 8`, so there are 8 ones and still 2 tens. That makes `23 + 5 = 28`, and only the ones digit changed, from 3 to 8. Knowing `3 + 5 = 8` gives you the ones, so you do not need to count on from 23.

Now add 7 instead. `3 + 7 = 10`, so the ones make a full ten. With the 2 tens that is 3 tens, so `23 + 7 = 30`, the ten after 23.

On the chart, the rods are tens and the cubes are ones. The red blocks show 23. The blue cubes with a dot are the ones you add, and the "Second number" stepper is at [5](@second).

- Press + and − on the stepper. From 0 to 6, the "Ones digit" readout changes with each press and "Tens digit" stays 2.
- Step up to 7. The tens column still holds 2 rods, but the tens digit is 3, because the 10 cubes are a full ten.
