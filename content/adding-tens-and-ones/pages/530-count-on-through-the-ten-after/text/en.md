You can find the difference of two numbers by counting on from the smaller one to the greater. With two-digit numbers you count on in three steps: to the ten after the smaller number, then in tens to the ten before the greater number, then in ones. The three steps add up to the difference.

Say Mia has 75 marbles and Sam has 46. How many more does Mia have? "How many more" asks for the difference, so it is the subtraction `75 − 46`. The word "more" can make you want to add, but `75 + 46` counts both children's marbles together.

46 needs 4 to reach 50, the ten after. From 50 you count in tens to 70, the ten before 75, so that step is 20. Then 5 more reach 75. 4, 20 and 5 make 29, so `75 − 46 = 29`, and Mia has 29 more.

The ring on 46 is Sam's marbles and the square on 75 is Mia's. You move the round point from the ring.

- Press "Jump right" four times to 50.
- Pick 10 and jump twice to 70, then pick 1 and jump five times to 75.

The tags over the runs read +4, +20 and +5, and together they make the difference. "Jump left" takes back the last jump.
