The chart shows a number in red, and the goal line names a greater number to reach, the target. Put on blue rods and cubes until the chart shows the target. Tens go with tens and ones with ones, so you can work out each place on its own. Count how many more tens the target has than the start, and put on that many rods. Then do the same with the ones and the cubes.

Say the chart shows 62 and the target is 97. 97 has 9 tens and 62 has 6, so you put on 3 rods. 97 has 7 ones and 62 has 2, so you put on 5 cubes. The chart then has 9 tens and 7 ones, which is 97, and you have added 35.

The number above the chart and the digits under it are hidden, so count the rods and cubes yourself.

- Tap the blue rod or cube in "Blocks to use", or drag it onto the chart. Drag a blue piece off if you put on too many.
- Press Check when you think the chart shows the target. If it does not, a note tells you which column has too many or too few.
- Press "Another one" for a new start and target.
