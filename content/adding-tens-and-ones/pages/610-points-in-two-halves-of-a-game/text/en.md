A basketball team's points for a game are its points in the two halves added together. A scoreboard has one place
for each digit, so when the ones make 10 or more, the board can show the score only after ten of them
join the tens as a new ten.

Say a team scores 28 points in the first half and 45 in the second. 2 tens and 4 tens make 6 tens, and 8 ones and 5
ones make 13 ones. 13 does not fit in the ones place, so ten of those ones become a new ten, and 3 are left. The
new ten and the 6 tens make 7 tens, so the team scored 73 points in the game, `28 + 45 = 73`.

Under the board, the plain red blocks are the first half's points and the blue blocks with dots are the second
half's.

- Tap or drag the blue blocks onto the chart. The Game box counts rods in its tens slot and cubes in its ones
  slot. With every block on, the ones slot stays empty and 13 appears under it, because two digits do not fit in one slot.
- Press "Bundle ten ones". 8 red cubes and 2 blue cubes make the new ten, and the Game box reads 73.
