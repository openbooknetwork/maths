The goal line names an addition. Build its sum on the empty chart, then press Check. In every addition here the
ones make 10 or more, so the sum has a new ten. Show that new ten as a rod, so the ones column keeps 9 cubes or
fewer.

Take `74 + 18`. Look at the ones first: 4 and 8 make 12, so there is a new ten. 7 tens and 1 ten make 8 tens. The 12
ones are the new ten and 2 ones, and the new ten turns the 8 tens into 9. So `74 + 18 = 92`, built with 9 rods and 2
cubes.

The rods and cubes you need are under "Blocks to use". The chart does not show its number, so keep count as you put
them on.

- Tap a rod or a cube, or drag it on, to put it on the chart. Drag a piece off to take it away.
- Put on the ones of both numbers and press "Bundle ten ones", or put the new ten on as a rod straight away.
- Press Check when you think the chart shows the sum. If it does not, a note tells you what to change.
- Press "Another one" for a new addition.
