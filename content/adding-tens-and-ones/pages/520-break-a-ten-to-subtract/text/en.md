A number's extra ones are the ones outside its tens. Each of its tens is made of ten ones too, so the number has more ones inside its tens. When the extra ones are fewer than the ones to take away, break one ten into ten ones first. The number stays the same, and then you subtract place by place.

Take `82 − 47`. 82 has 2 extra ones, fewer than the 7 ones of 47, and its other 80 ones are inside its 8 tens. Break one ten, and its ten ones join the 2, so 82 is 7 tens and 12 ones. Now `12 − 7 = 5` in the ones and `70 − 40 = 30` in the tens, so `82 − 47 = 35`.

If you take the smaller digit from the greater in each place, `8 − 4 = 4` and `7 − 2 = 5` give 45. The 7 ones belong to 47, the number you subtract, so they have to come out of the ones of 82, and 45 is wrong.

Tap the tens column to break a rod, and watch the Ones readout, [2](@ones), go up by 10 while the Left readout, [82](@left), stays the same. Then tap the ones column to take away 7 cubes, and drag 4 rods off, since a tap on the tens column breaks a rod. "Bundle ten ones" joins ten cubes back into a rod.
