To add two two-digit numbers, you add the tens to the tens and the ones to the ones. A ten and a one are different sizes, so you count each kind on its own, then put the counts together.

Take `31 + 26`. 31 is 3 tens and 1 one, and 26 is 2 tens and 6 ones. 3 tens and 2 tens make 5 tens, because `3 + 2 = 5`, so `30 + 20 = 50`. 1 one and 6 ones make 7 ones, because `1 + 6 = 7`. 5 tens and 7 ones make 57, so `31 + 26 = 57`. Each place only needed a sum within 10 that you already know. If you add the ones first, the sum is still 57.

The chart keeps rods and cubes in their own columns. The plain red blocks are the first number, [31](@first). You build the second number from the blue rod and cube in "Blocks to use".

- Tap or drag the rod to put on a ten, and the cube to put on a one.
- Put on 2 rods and 6 cubes. Drag a blue piece off to take it back.
- Watch the Sum readout, [31](@sum), as each rod adds 10 and each cube adds 1. Under the chart, a line for each place appears once that place has a blue piece.
