Each row of the hundred square holds ten numbers, so ten more than a number is in the cell just below it, and ten
less is in the cell just above. Down a column the ones digit stays the same, and the tens digit goes up by 1 from
each row to the next.

Take 58. The cell just below it holds 68, so ten more than 58 is 68. The cell just above holds 48, so ten less than
58 is 48. All three end in 8, like every number in that column from 8 down to 98.

Ten more than 90 is 100, the last cell. Ten less than 10 is 0, on the dashed tile left of the square.

The point is on [58](@point). "Ten more" shows [68](@ten-more) and "Ten less" shows [48](@ten-less). Tap either
number to light its cell.

- Tap 90, then 13, and read their ten more and ten less.
- Tap 7 in the top row, then 95 in the last row. Each time one readout shows a dash, because that number has no cell
  on the square.
- Press Jump, and the point moves to the cell that "Ten more" showed. Jump back moves it up a row again.
