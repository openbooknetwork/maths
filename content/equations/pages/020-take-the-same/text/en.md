A level scale stays level when you take the same weight off both pans. In the equation, you subtract the same number from both sides.

The scale shows [x + 3 = 7](@equation). To find x, get the box alone. Set the number under the scale to 3, then press −3. Three weights leave each pan and the beam comes back level.

Watch the line above the scale. It writes −3 on both sides, cancels the +3 against the −3, and works out 7 − 3.

Each step joins the list, and your step count is [0](@steps). A step that does not help keeps the scale level too, and Undo takes it back.
