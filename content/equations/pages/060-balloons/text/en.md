A balloon tied to a pan pulls it up. Each balloon here lifts as much as one weight pushes down, so a balloon stands for −1.

At the start the left pan holds the box and four balloons, and the right pan holds three weights. The left side is now [x − 4](@left) and the right side is [3](@right).

To get the box alone, the balloons have to go. Letting go of a balloon makes its pan heavier, as if a weight landed on it. So set the number to 4 and press +4. The balloons fly off the left pan, and four weights land on the right.
