A balance compares two weights. When the pans hold the same weight, the beam is level. When one pan is heavier, it goes down.

The box on the left pan hides a weight we call x. The left pan holds [x + 3](@left) and the right pan holds [7](@right). The scale is [level](@scale), and the line above it says [x + 3 = 7](@equation). While the scale is level, both sides weigh the same and the line is an equation.

Drag a weight off a pan, or take one from the tray and drop it on a pan. The buttons under the scale do the same. The scale tips, and the equals sign becomes ≠.
