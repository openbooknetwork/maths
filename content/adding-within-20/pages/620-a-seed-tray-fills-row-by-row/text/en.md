A gardener grows young plants in a seed tray with two rows of ten cells. They fill the first row before starting the second, because a full row is easy to count at a glance. This tray has 7 seedlings in the first row and a packet of 5 seeds, so sowing the packet is the addition `7 + 5`. Before you sow, work out how many seeds fill the first row, how many go into the second, and how many plants the tray will hold.

Say another tray has 6 seedlings and 7 seeds. 6 and 4 make 10, so 4 seeds fill the first row. The other 3 seeds start the second row. The tray then holds a full row and 3 more, `10 + 3 = 13` plants.

- Drag a seed onto a row, or tap an empty cell, to sow it.
- When the packet is empty, the sum appears under the tray, and the two steps appear if the first row is full. Compare the sum and the "Makes ten" and "The rest" readouts with your three numbers.
- If you sow into the second row before the first is full, the steps stay hidden. Tap that sprout to send it back, and sow it in the first row.
