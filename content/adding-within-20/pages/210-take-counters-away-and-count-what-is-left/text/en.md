When you take some objects away from a group, fewer are left than there were at the start, because the ones you took away are no longer in the group. To find out how many are left, you count the objects that are still there.

First there are 9 apples in a bowl. Then 4 of the apples are eaten. Now how many apples are left? You count the apples still in the bowl: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. So 5 apples are left, fewer than the 9 you started with.

The figure shows the 9 apples as round red counters in the ten frames, one counter for each apple. Taking a counter away stands for eating its apple.

- Tap a counter to take it away. An empty dashed ring takes its place, so you can still see how many there were at the start.
- Take 4 counters away, and count the counters that are left.
- Tap a ring to put its counter back, and try taking away a different number.

The "Left" readout now shows [9](@left). With 4 rings in the frames, the 5 counters left are the 5 apples still in the bowl.
