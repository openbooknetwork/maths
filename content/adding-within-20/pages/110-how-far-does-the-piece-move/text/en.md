In many board games you throw two dice and move your piece one square for each dot. The dots on each die make a group. To find how far the piece moves, you join the two groups into one group and count all of it.

Say you throw a 5 and a 3. You put the 3 dots with the 5 dots and count them all: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8. There are 8 dots altogether, so the piece moves 8 squares.

The figure shows the throw with counters in place of dots. The round red counters in the frames are the dots of the die above the frames, and the square blue counters in the tray are the dots of the other die. When you move a blue counter into the frames, you join its dot to the first group.

- Drag a blue counter onto the frames, or tap it.
- Tap a blue counter in the frames to send it back to the tray.
- Press Roll for a new throw.

When the tray is empty, count every counter in the frames. Then check your count against the "Counters in the frames" readout, which shows [5](@sum) now. That number is how many squares the piece moves.
