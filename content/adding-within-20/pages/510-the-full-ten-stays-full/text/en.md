A ten frame with a counter in every cell holds a full ten. Every number from 11 to 20 is a full ten and some more, so 12 is 10 and 2. When you add a small number to 12, the full ten has no empty cell left. So it stays, and you add to the counters after it.

Take `12 + 5`. The 2 counters after the full ten and the 5 you add make `2 + 5 = 7`. So the sum is a full ten and 7 more, and `12 + 5 = 17`.

The first frame holds 10 round red counters. The second holds 2 round red counters and then 5 square blue ones.

The second frame also shows two ideas you already know. A frame has 10 cells, so its counters and its empty cells make 10. At `12 + 5`, the second frame has 7 counters and 3 empty cells, so 7 and 3 are a pair that makes 10. Its 7 counters are 2 round ones and 5 square ones, so 7 splits into 2 and 5.

- Press + and − on the "Second number" stepper. Only the square counters come and go, and the first frame stays full.
- Press + until the sum is 20. Then both frames are full.
