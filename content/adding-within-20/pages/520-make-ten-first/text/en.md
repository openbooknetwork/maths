An addition crosses ten when its first number is below 10 and its sum is above 10. Making ten turns it into two easy steps. You split the second number into the part that takes the first number up to 10, and the rest.

Take `8 + 5`. 8 needs 2 more to make 10, so you split 5 into 2 and 3. The first step is `8 + 2 = 10`. The 3 is the rest, and a full ten and 3 more is 13, so the second step is `10 + 3 = 13`.

The first frame has 8 round red counters and 2 empty cells, and 5 square blue counters are in the tray.

- Drag 2 blue counters into the first frame and the other 3 into the second. A tap works too, since it fills the next empty cell.
- Once the first frame is full and the sum is past 10, the two steps appear under the number sentence.
- If you drop a blue counter in the second frame while the first frame still has an empty cell, there is no full ten, so no steps appear. Tap that counter to send it back to the tray, then drag it into the first frame.
