A number line is a straight line with the numbers of the count on it, in order from 0. Each number has its own mark, a short stroke across the line, and the marks are the same distance apart. The numbers get greater as you go to the right. The dot on the line is the point, and a move of the point to the next mark is a jump.

Counting on is jumping to the right, one jump for each number you say, and counting back is jumping to the left. If you count on three from 14, you say 15, 16, 17 and make three jumps. There are four marks from 14 to 17, but only three jumps, because the point was already on 14 before it moved. So you count the jumps and leave out the mark where you began.

The point starts on 14.

- Press "Jump right" three times, and say each number it lands on.
- Press "Jump left" five times. The first three presses take back your jumps, and the last two count back to 13 and 12.
- Drag the point to another mark to count from a new number.

The number at the point is [14](@point), and the Jumps readout shows [0](@jumps).
