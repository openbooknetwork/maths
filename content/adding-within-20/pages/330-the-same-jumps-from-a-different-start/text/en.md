Three jumps to the right add 3, wherever the start is. Each jump goes on to the next number, so after three jumps the point is 3 numbers past the start. Three jumps to the left take it 3 numbers back, so they subtract 3. The number at the start is the first number, and the number of jumps is the number you add or subtract.

Start on 4 and jump three times to the right, to 5, 6 and 7, so `4 + 3 = 7`. From 15 the same jumps land on 18, so `15 + 3 = 18`. The first number and the sum change, and the 3 stays.

At first, the start is on 9 and the point has made three jumps to the right. The number sentence under the number line reads [9 + 3 = 12](@written).

- Drag the start ring to 4, then 15. The point and its arcs move with it, and the 3 in the number sentence stays.

- Drag the start back to 15 and press "Jump left" six times. The first three presses take the jumps back, and the next three subtract 3. Then drag the start to 8.
