This story has shown you how to add and subtract with numbers up to 20.

- When you join two groups, the sum tells you how many there are altogether. You write it with the + sign.
- When you take a group away, you write what is left with the − sign.
- You add a small number by counting on, and subtract one by counting back.
- The difference between two numbers is the greater number minus the smaller one, and you can find it by counting on.
- When an addition or a subtraction crosses ten, you go to 10 first.

The figure starts on `8 + 5 = 13`, with its two steps `8 + 2 = 10` and `10 + 3 = 13` under it. Change the numbers on the two steppers and watch the steps. They appear while the addition crosses ten, as `7 + 6` does, and disappear when it does not, as at `4 + 5` or `8 + 2`.

These stories come next:

- **Fact families** writes the four number sentences that three numbers such as 8, 5 and 13 make.
- **Adding tens and ones** adds and subtracts bigger numbers, across 20, 30 and the other tens.
- **The equals sign** puts a calculation on each side of =.
- **Order and grouping** adds numbers in any order and groups them.
