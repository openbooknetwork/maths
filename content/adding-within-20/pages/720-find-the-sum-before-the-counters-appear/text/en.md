Your goal is to find the sum of an addition while the frames are still empty. With no counters to count, you work the sum out from the two numbers. A quick way is to count on. You start at the first number, because it already tells you how many are in the first group. Then you say the next number once for each counter in the second group, and the last number you say is the sum.

Take `8 + 3`. You start at 8 and count on three numbers: 9, 10, 11. So the sum is 11, and `8 + 3 = 11`.

The figure writes an addition under the frames and leaves out its sum.

- Set the sum on the Sum stepper with + and −, then press Check.
- If the number you set is wrong, a note tells you whether the sum is greater or less than it.
- When the sum is right, round red counters for the first number go into the frames, square blue counters for the second number join them, and the number sentence gets its = sign and its sum.
- Press "Another one" for a new addition.
