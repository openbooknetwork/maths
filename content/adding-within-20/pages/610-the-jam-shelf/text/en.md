A shop starts the day with 10 jars of jam on its shelf. It sells 3 jars in the morning and 5 more in the afternoon. How many jars does it sell, and how many are still on the shelf? The jars sold in the morning and in the afternoon are all the jars sold together, so you add the two numbers. Then you subtract that sum from 10 to find how many are left.

Say that on another day the shop sells 4 jars in the morning and 2 in the afternoon. It sells `4 + 2 = 6` jars, and `10 − 6 = 4` jars are left.

The 3 jars sold in the morning are already in the Morning crate. Work out both answers for 3 and 5 before you sell any more jars.

- Next to "Sold in the", pick Afternoon.
- Tap the shelf 5 times. Each tap sells one jar into the Afternoon crate.
- Tap a crate to put its last jar back on the shelf.

The "Jars sold" readout shows [3 + 0 = 3](@written), and the "Jars left" readout shows [10 − 3 = 7](@written-left). Once you have sold 5, check them against your answers. Then sell every jar that is left, and the "Jars left" readout ends at `10 − 10 = 0`.
