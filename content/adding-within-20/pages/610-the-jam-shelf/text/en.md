A shop starts the day with 10 jars of jam on its shelf. It sells 3 jars in the morning and 5 more in the afternoon. How many jars does it sell, and how many are still on the shelf? The jars sold in the morning and the jars sold in the afternoon together are all the jars sold, so you add the two numbers. Every jar sold has gone from the shelf, so you subtract that sum from 10 to find how many are left.

Say that on another day the shop sells 6 jars in the morning and only 1 in the afternoon. It sells `6 + 1 = 7` jars, and `10 − 7 = 3` jars are left.

The 3 jars sold in the morning are already in the Morning crate. Work out both answers for 3 and 5 before you sell any more jars.

- Next to "Sold in the", pick Afternoon.
- Tap the shelf 5 times. Each tap sells one jar into the Afternoon crate.
- Tap a crate to put its last jar back on the shelf.

The "Jars sold" readout shows [3 + 0 = 3](@written), and the "Jars left" readout shows [10 − 3 = 7](@written-left). Each jar you sell adds 1 to the jars sold and takes 1 from the jars left, so the two numbers after = always make 10. Once you have sold 5 in the afternoon, see whether the two readouts match your answers. Then sell every jar that is left, and the "Jars left" readout ends at `10 − 10 = 0`.
